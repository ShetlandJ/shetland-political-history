# LTC co-options of 1946 and 1950

Researched 2026-09-28 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times). Every link opens the
BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** for the OCR text.

Question: source elections 112 (T. A. Sinclair for David Gray, 1946; the DB's Wed 22 May was
unchecked) and 118 (Robert Blance for James Jamieson (ii), Sep 1950). Citations queue, batch 1.

Findings: **Sinclair was elected on Tue 2 Apr 1946**, not 22 May (`fix_newspapers.py` #29;
ledger start moved; the wiki's "By-Election May 1946" title is wrong on the month). Blance's
co-option is confirmed; the meeting day isn't printed, so the DB's Tue 12 Sep 1950 stands.

## Evidence

### 1. April 1946 (election 112)
- **Shetland Times, Fri 5 Apr 1946, p3 (art. 073, 074)**: the filling of the vacancy was among
  the main items "at the Town Council meeting on Tuesday" (2 Apr). "COUNCIL VACANCY. Mr T. A.
  Sinclair being the only nominee for the vacancy caused through the death of Mr David Gray,
  was duly elected."
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19460405&page=0003&article=074
- His row's end at the Nov 1946 general is inferred: five vacancies then (ST 1 Nov 1946, art.
  044/048), but the preview doesn't name him.

### 2. September 1950 (election 118)
- **Shetland Times, Fri 15 Sep 1950, p6 (art. 072)**: "Decisions From The Council": "Mr R. B.
  Blance ... top of the list of unsuccessful candidates at the last election, co-opted to the
  vacancy caused by the resignation of Mr J. A. Jamieson", following the "unwritten rule".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19500915&page=0006&article=072
