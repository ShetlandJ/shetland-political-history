# LTC wartime: the 1941 resignations of Irvine and Linklater

Researched 2026-09-27 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times, published on Saturdays in
these years). Every link opens the BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** in the toolbar
for the OCR text.

Question: clear the 13-member rows for 1941–42, 1942–46 and May–Jun 1946. The wiki's October 1941
by-election already says Gear and Inkster replaced Thomas Irvine (ii) "also" Joseph Linklater, but
the generator only ended Linklater's term, so Irvine stayed in the ledger until his death in 1946.
The Oct 1945 retiring list had already shown he was gone (`ltc-1945-1953.md`).

## Evidence

### Aug 1941: both resign
- **ST Sat 9 Aug 1941, p2 (art. 065)**: the resignations of two members "who have not been able to
  attend meetings for some time past" were intimated "at the Council's meeting on Tuesday"
  (5 Aug 1941).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19410809&page=0002&article=065
- **Same issue, p2 (art. 070)**: "RESIGNATION OF COUNCILLORS". Linklater's letter of 26 July
  resigned "three weeks from that date" (16 Aug 1941); he was away and saw no early return.
  Irvine could not attend from December 1939 because of sixteen-hour working days. The Provost
  took it that both resignations were accepted.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19410809&page=0002&article=070
- **Same issue, p2 (art. 072)**: Ganson moved not to accept Linklater's (Private Linklater, in the
  Forces); Aitken said Linklater had always meant to leave. The vacancies were filled in October,
  so it was accepted.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19410809&page=0002&article=072
- Edits: **Irvine ends 1941-08-05** (`resigned`, accepted that day). **Linklater ends 1941-08-16**,
  the date in his letter. Both confirmed.

### Oct 1941: Gear and Inkster co-opted
- **ST Sat 11 Oct 1941, p2 (art. 030)**: "The co-opting of two new members ... was agreed to
  unanimously at Tuesday's meeting" (7 Oct 1941).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19411011&page=0002&article=030
- **Same issue, p2 (art. 034)**: "Two vacancies fell to be filled on the Council, following the
  resignations of Messrs J. L. Linklater and Thomas Irvine": John Gear (proposed by Bailie Smith)
  and Charles Inkster (proposed by Williamson).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19411011&page=0002&article=034
- Edits: the by-election's date 1941-10-09 → **1941-10-07** (`fix_newspapers.py` #10), and Gear's
  and Inkster's start dates with it (left unconfirmed: their end dates weren't checked).

## Result

The three size-over rows are gone. term_issues 48 → 49: in their place are four size-short rows,
all genuine vacancies now visible: 5–16 Aug 1941 (11) and 16 Aug–7 Oct 1941 (10) while the two
seats waited for co-option; 28 Feb–9 Apr 1942 after Bailie W. G. Smith's death; and 4 Mar–22 May
1946 after David Gray's death. They were hidden before by Irvine's phantom seat.

## Not checked

The other wartime co-options (Mouat 1940, J. A. Williamson 1941, Johnson 1941, T. Sinclair 1942,
R. Anderson 1943, P. Smith 1944, Morrison 1945) keep the wiki's replaced members and dates,
unconfirmed. The six 1945 retirements are confirmed by ST 5 Oct 1945 (`ltc-1945-1953.md`), but the
rows weren't flagged confirmed because their start dates weren't checked.
