# Open questions settled from the page images (2026-10-03)

Eight open questions had stalled because the page image wouldn't render. Each was read from a
crop of the scan through the BNA image service (see the `/bna` skill, section 4). Six are settled,
one only partly, and one can't be settled from the paper.

## Settled

- **Walls, May 1930 (election 577)**: ST Sat 24 May 1930, p5. The first meeting of the
  re-constituted Council "was held in the County Hall ... on Tuesday" (20 May; art. 087–088).
  "VACANCIES FILLED": "Two petitions were submitted, one in favour of Mr Andrew Halcrow, 1 Carlton
  Place, Lerwick, bearing 17 signatures, and the other in favour of Mr William Hales, Spurries,
  Walls, bearing 15 signatures". Mouat and Ollason moved Halcrow, Ganson and Neven-Spence moved
  Hales; "On the vote being taken Mr Halcrow was elected by a large majority" (art. 088).
  → `fix_newspapers.py` #77: date 20 May, Halcrow's petition and Council vote, Hales added.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19300524&page=0005&article=088
- **Dunrossness North, June 1907 (election 337)**: ST Sat 22 Jun 1907, p8 (art. 131). "The monthly
  meeting of the County Council of Zetland was held in the County Hall ... on Thursday" (20 Jun);
  "The election of Budge was unanimous". → #78.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19070622&page=0008&article=131
- **Duncan (i)'s co-option for Bailie Hay, Nov 1884 (election 34)**: ST Sat 15 Nov 1884, p3. The
  annual meeting (Mon 10 Nov) "adjourned till Tuesday evening for the purpose of filling up the
  vacancy in the Council caused by Bailie Hay's resignation". "The adjourned meeting took place on
  Tuesday evening" (11 Nov), and after the tribute to C. G. Duncan, "the first business on the
  notice-paper was the election of a Councillor" (art. 042): William Duncan. → #79 (back to the
  baseline's 11 Nov, which #1 had moved to the 22nd); ledger row `william-duncan-i@1884-11-11`
  confirmed. The 4–22 Nov 1884 size-short row is now 4–11 Nov.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18841115&page=0003&article=042
- **Hugh T. Sutherland in 1970**: ST Fri 24 Apr 1970, p8 (art. 068), "SIXTEEN COUNCILLORS RETURNED
  UNOPPOSED", the full list of nominations. He isn't in it: Delting North Fraser Peterson, Delting
  South William C. Robb, Northmavine South Robert Balfour. So he sat for Delting South 1967–70.
  → #80 (intro).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19700424&page=0008&article=068
- **Bailie Campbell's resignation, May 1932**: Shetland News Thu 5 May 1932, p5 (art. 049). "The
  monthly meeting of Lerwick Town Council was held in the Burgh Chambers on Tuesday evening" (3 May).
  The ledger's 3 May is right; the row stays unconfirmed because its start (the 1931 result) isn't
  sourced yet.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0003210%2F19320505&page=0005&article=049
- **Unst South 1942 petition**: Shetland News Thu 29 Oct 1942, p2 (art. 031). The petition for John
  Sutherland, Bixter, forwarded by John Humphray, Uyeasound, was "signed by **23** ratepayers in
  South Unst" (the OCR's 323 was a misread). The Convener said the usual procedure was to let a
  vacancy lie over for a month, and it did. So the October petition (23) is separate from December's
  (Hunter 55, Sutherland 19). Irvine's end date is still open.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0003210%2F19421029&page=0002&article=031
