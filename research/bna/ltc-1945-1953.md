# LTC 1945–1953: retiring lists after the war

Researched 2026-09-27 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times, published on Fridays in
these years). Every link opens the BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** in the toolbar
for the OCR text.

Question: clear the overlaps for Robert Anderson (i) (1947), William Anderson (i) (1949), James
Williamson (1950) and Arthur Johnson (1952), and the 1951–52 short row. The draft ledger gave
every winner a 3-year term. After the war the Clerk made up the retiring quota each year from
the lowest winners of earlier polls, a Treasurer and a Provost were kept on past their turn, and
the 1948 election was put back six months to May 1949.

## Evidence

### Oct 1945: the six wartime co-optees retire
- **ST Fri 5 Oct 1945, p2 (art. 041)**: "50 PER CENT RETIRE": Mouat, J. A. Williamson, Johnson,
  T. A. Sinclair, R. A. Anderson and Alexander Morrison. Present and staying on: Provost Shearer,
  Bailies Aitken and Dalziel, Councillor Ganson.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19451005&page=0002&article=041
- So the six staying were Shearer, Aitken, Dalziel, Ganson, W. S. Anderson and Laing. **Thomas
  Irvine (ii) was no longer on the council** (the ledger has him sitting until his death in June
  1946, which is the 13-member rows of 1942–46). Left for the wartime co-options item.

### Oct 1946: five retire
- **ST Fri 4 Oct 1946, p5 (art. 071)**: meeting of Tue 1 Oct 1946. The Town Clerk's list: "Bailie
  J. J. Aitken, Bailie P. Dalziel, and Councillors W. Anderson, Ar. Johnson, and T. A. Sinclair".
  Johnson "took the place of ex-Provost Laing", so counted in the one-third quota; Sinclair
  (co-opted for David Gray) retired as a co-opted member.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19461004&page=0005&article=071
- Edits: Aitken and W. Anderson 1937–46 confirmed; **Dalziel's 1938 seat ends Nov 1946**, not 1947.

### 1947: Shearer resigns, Dalziel co-opted, four stand down
- **ST Fri 4 Jul 1947, p5 (art. 098)**: "Ex-Bailie Peter Dalziel returns to the Council in place
  of Lt.-Col. Magnus Shearer, who resigned last month." Dalziel had topped the losers in 1946.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19470704&page=0005&article=098
- **ST Fri 10 Oct 1947, p4 (art. 083)**: standing down are "Bailie R. A. Anderson and Councillors
  R. J. H. Ganson, A. Morrison, and P. Dalziel who was recently co-opted to fill the vacancy left by
  Lt.-Col. M. Shearer".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19471010&page=0004&article=083
- **ST Fri 10 Oct 1947, p4 (art. 084)**: Anderson and Morrison, the lowest of the six winners of
  1945 (904 and 903), retire after two years to make up the retiring number.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19471010&page=0004&article=084
- Edits: R. Anderson and Morrison 1945 terms end Nov 1947 (Morrison's had ended Nov 1946, leaving
  him off the council for a year); Ganson confirmed. Shearer's 1938 term ends `resigned`, and a new
  Dalziel row 1947-07-01 → 1947-11-04. **Both left unconfirmed**: Shearer's resignation day in June
  wasn't found, and 1 Jul 1947 (the first Tuesday, the monthly meeting) is inferred from the
  4 Jul report, which doesn't name the day. No wiki by-election page exists for this co-option.

### Mar 1949: four retire; Ollason kept on
- **ST Fri 11 Mar 1949, p5 (art. 074)**: elections "put back six months", so the next is on
  3 May 1949. Retiring: Bailie L. W. Smith and J. G. Peterson (elected 1945); "Treasurer R.
  Ollason remained in office ... two years further because of his appointment last year as
  treasurer"; so the two lowest winners of 1946 retire, "Councillor W. S. Anderson and Councillor
  Arthur Johnson".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19490311&page=0005&article=074
- Edits: Peterson and Smith 1945–49 confirmed; **Ollason's 1945 term runs to May 1951**; W.
  Anderson and **Johnson 1946 terms end May 1949** (Johnson's had ended Nov 1947).

### Mar 1950: four retire
- **ST Fri 17 Mar 1950, p4 (art. 053)**: "Four members retire this time— Provost J. J. Aitken,
  Bailie G. H. Burgess, Councillor J. D. Williamson, and Councillor Arthur Johnson."
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19500317&page=0004&article=053
- Edits: Aitken, Burgess 1946–50 and Johnson's Sep 1949 co-option confirmed; **Williamson's 1947
  term ends May 1950** (the lowest 1947 winner, making room for Ollason's extra two years).

### Apr 1951: four retire; Provost Anderson stays on
- **ST Fri 13 Apr 1951, p4 (art. 047 and 048)**: meeting of Tue 10 Apr 1951; Provost Anderson
  thanked "all four of the retiring members": Blance, Inkster, Morrison and Ollason (the only four
  nominated until Mouat stood).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19510413&page=0004&article=047
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19510413&page=0004&article=048
- Edits: Inkster and Morrison 1947–51, Blance's 1950 co-option confirmed. Provost R. A. Anderson,
  elected 1947, was not retiring: see 1953.

### Apr 1952: four retire
- **ST Fri 18 Apr 1952, p4 (art. 055)**: "EIGHT CANDIDATES FOR FOUR SEATS"; the starred retiring
  members are Peterson, Johnson, W. S. Anderson and Conochie ("has just completed his first
  three-year period").
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19520418&page=0004&article=055
- Edits: the 1949 three confirmed; **Johnson's 1950 term ends May 1952** (the lowest 1950 winner,
  retiring in the Provost's place).

### Mar 1953: four retire, including the Provost
- **ST Fri 13 Mar 1953, p4 (art. 062)**: "The four are Messrs R. A. Anderson, J. J. Aitken, G. H.
  Burgess, and J. D. Williamson."
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19530313&page=0004&article=062
- Edits: **R. Anderson's 1947 term runs to May 1953** (not 1951, which had left the 1951–52 council
  at 11); Aitken, Burgess and Williamson 1950–53 confirmed.

## Result

the 1951–52 size-short row. No new issues.
