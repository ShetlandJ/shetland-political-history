# LTC election previews 1932, 1933 and 1936

Researched 2026-09-28 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times). Every link opens the
BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** in the toolbar for the OCR text.

Question: the ledger rows citing an undated "Shetland Times preview" of the Nov 1932, Nov 1933
and Nov 1936 generals (citations queue, batch 1). Each preview is in the Saturday paper before
polling day. All three agree with the ledger; no edits beyond the citations.

## Evidence

### 1. Nov 1932 general (election 91)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 29 Oct 1932, p5 (art. 039)**: "Nine Candidates for Five Seats"; poll
  "Tuesday first, 1st November". Four of the retiring members stand: Bruce, Manson, Morrison and
  Sandison. Ex-Provost R. D. Ganson, "elected a member of Lerwick Town Council in 1911", does not
  seek re-election. Provost William Sinclair is not among the retiring.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19321029&page=0005&article=039
- Supports the ends of `william-bruce-ii@1930-11-04`, `alexander-manson@1929-11-05`,
  `laurence-sandison@1929-11-05`, `robert-ganson-i@1929-11-05`, `james-morrison@1932-06-07`,
  and (inferred) Provost Sinclair's 1929 seat running past 1932. The three 1929 rows and
  Morrison's June 1932 row stay unconfirmed: their starts (the 1929 result, the June 1932
  co-option) aren't sourced yet.

### 2. Nov 1933 general (election 92)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 4 Nov 1933, p4 (art. 083)**: eight candidates for four seats, "the four
  retiring Councillors, Provost William Sinclair, Bailie Robert Ollason, and Ex-Provosts James
  Laing and John T. J. Sinclair", and four Ratepayers' Association nominees (Nicol, Sutherland,
  Halcrow, C. A. Manson). Poll Tuesday 7 Nov.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19331104&page=0004&article=083

### 3. Nov 1936 general (election 97)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 31 Oct 1936, p4 (art. 074)**: "Four Candidates for Five Seats", "no
  poll" for the first time since 1931. Halcrow (Senior Bailie), Irvine and T. A. Sinclair (both
  co-opted) are retiring members; Shearer is new. Provost Ollason and C. A. Manson, "the other
  two members who retired", did not seek re-election. Declared elected Tuesday 3 Nov.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19361031&page=0004&article=074
