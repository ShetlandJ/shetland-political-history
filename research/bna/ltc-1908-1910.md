# LTC 1908–1910: retiring lists

Researched 2026-09-28 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times). Every link opens the
BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** in the toolbar for the OCR text.

Question: resolve the overlaps for Robert Ganson (i) (1908) and John Smith (i) (1909) (queue
item "1908–1909").

## Evidence

### 1. October 1908: Ganson retires, Provost Porteous stays on
- **Shetland Times, Sat 10 Oct 1908, p5 (art. 122)**: Provost Porteous presiding; the Clerk
  intimated the retiring Councillors as Goodlad, Ganson, Sinclair and Ratter (OCR garbled on
  the first two names; confirmed by the notice below).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19081010&page=0005&article=122
- **Shetland Times, Sat 17 Oct 1908, p1 (art. 014)**: election notice: four councillors "in the
  place of Messrs William Sinclair, Alexander Ratter, Peter S. Goodlad, and Robert D. Ganson,
  who retire by rotation".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19081017&page=0001&article=014
- Ratter, Goodlad and Sinclair are three of the 1905 four. The fourth, **Porteous**, was Provost
  and isn't on the list; Ganson (1906) retired in his place, as in 1897, 1912, 1932 and 1937.
  The papers don't give the reason; that part is an inference.
- **Ledger**: `robert-ganson-i` 1906-11-06 ends 1908-11-03; `alexander-ratter`, `peter-goodlad`,
  `william-sinclair` 1905-11-07 confirmed.

### 2. October 1909: J. Smith retires, Porteous still stays on
- **Shetland Times, Sat 9 Oct 1909, p5 (art. 163)**: retiring "Bailies Laing and Stout, and
  Councillors J. Laing and J. Smith". The Provost (still Porteous) spoke after the list.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19091009&page=0005&article=163
- **Shetland Times, Sat 16 Oct 1909, p1 (art. 007)**: election notice for four councillors in
  place of Bailies Arthur Laurenson Laing and Robert Stout and Councillors James Laing and John
  Smith.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19091016&page=0001&article=007
- Also ST 16 Oct 1909 p4 (art. 133) and ST 30 Oct 1909 p4 (art. 066) repeat the four.
- The 1906 four less Ganson, plus John Smith (1907) a year early: Smith took the seat Ganson's
  1908 re-election left over.
- **Ledger**: `john-smith-i` 1907-11-05 ends 1909-11-02; `arthur-laing`, `james-laing`,
  `robert-stout-i` 1906-11-06 confirmed.

### 3. October 1910: Porteous retires
- **Shetland Times, Sat 8 Oct 1910, p5 (art. 085)**: retiring members "Provost Porteous, and
  Messrs A. Smith, W. S. Smith, and J. A. Loggie".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19101008&page=0005&article=085
- **Shetland Times, Sat 15 Oct 1910, p4 (art. 078)**: election notice, in place of Arthur A.
  Porteous, Provost, and three others.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19101015&page=0004&article=078
- ST 8 Oct 1910 p4 (art. 068) and ST 22 Oct 1910 p5 (art. 106, "Provost Porteous's retiral"):
  he did not stand again, after over a quarter of a century of service.
- **Ledger**: `arthur-porteous` 1905-11-07 ends **1910-11-01** (was 1908-11-03, which had left no
  Porteous row for 1908–10); `andrew-smith`, `william-smith-ii` 1907-11-05 and `james-loggie`
  1910-01-01 confirmed.
