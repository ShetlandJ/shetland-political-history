# LTC 1919–1921: holdovers, Pottinger's seat and Bailie Sinclair's resignation

Researched 2026-09-28 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times). Every link opens the
BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** in the toolbar for the OCR text.

Question (queue item "LTC holdovers 1919–1921"): confirm the rows of the five members who sat on
past Nov 1919 (Ratter, Goodlad, Ganson, W. Sinclair to 1920; J. Smith (i) to 1921), and whether
Provost Goodlad left early. The Oct 1921 size-short row (W. Sinclair's vacancy) depended on the
same rows plus Pottinger's and J. M. Goodlad's.

## Evidence

### 1. The holdovers (from earlier files)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 18 Oct 1919, p5 (art. 082)**: the seven retiring in 1919 (see
  `ltc-1915-1916.md` #11). Goodlad, Ratter, Ganson, W. Sinclair and J. Smith are not on it.
- **Shetland Times, Sat 16 Oct 1920, p1 (art. 009)**: Goodlad, Ganson, Ratter and W. Sinclair
  retire by rotation (see `ltc-1920-1923.md` #1). So Goodlad sat on to Nov 1920.
- **Shetland Times, Sat 8 Oct 1921, p5 (art. 115)**: J. Smith retiring "in the ordinary course"
  (see `ltc-1920-1923.md` #2).
- **Shetland Times, Sat 14 May 1921, p4 (art. 099)**: "Ex-Provost Goodlad" in the chair of a
  meeting attended by Provost Ganson.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19210514&page=0004&article=099
- **Ledger**: `alexander-ratter`, `peter-goodlad`, `robert-ganson-i`, `william-sinclair`
  1919-11-04 (to 1920-11-02) and `john-smith-i` 1919-11-04 (to 1921-11-01) confirmed.

### 2. The Nov 1919 result
- **Shetland Times, Sat 8 Nov 1919, p4 (art. 089)**, "The Result of the Poll": Laing 656,
  M. L. Manson 610, J. J. Pottinger 546, then Morrison, Ollason, J. T. J. Sinclair and Ramsay
  (matches the DB).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19191108&page=0004&article=089
- **Same issue, p4 (art. 077)**, leader: of the seven returned, Pottinger, Morrison and Ollason
  were new members.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19191108&page=0004&article=077

### 3. Pottinger's retiral and J. M. Goodlad's co-option, 1921
- **Shetland Times, Sat 23 Apr 1921, p4 (art. 116)**: Pottinger present at a Town Council
  meeting (the last found).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19210423&page=0004&article=116
- **Shetland Times, Sat 7 May 1921, p4**, "Lerwick Town Council": monthly meeting on Tuesday
  (3 May). Pottinger not among those present. (The same issue's "Goodlad elected by nine votes to
  six" is the Education Authority, not the Town Council.)
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19210507&page=0004&article=067
- **Shetland Times, Sat 28 May 1921, p5 (art. 151)**, Zetland County Council: J. T. J. Sinclair
  appointed to Pottinger's vacant seat for Lerwick Central (8 votes to 3 for Duffin). Pottinger
  had left the County Council too.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19210528&page=0005&article=151
- **Shetland Times, Sat 11 Jun 1921, p5 (arts. 100–101)**, "Lerwick Town Council": monthly
  meeting "on Tuesday evening" (7 Jun). Under "New Councillor Elected", Manson moved William
  Bruce for "the vacancy caused by the retiral of Mr J. J. Pottinger"; Ramsay moved J. M.
  Goodlad, who "stood next to Mr Pottinger". Five votes each; the Provost's casting vote went to
  Goodlad. Manson noted that whoever was co-opted would retire in November.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19210611&page=0005&article=101
- **Shetland Times, Sat 9 Jul 1921, p5 (arts. 125, 128)**: Goodlad present on Tue 5 Jul and given
  "his predecessor, Mr J. J. Pottinger"'s committee places.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19210709&page=0005&article=128
- **Ledger**: `james-goodlad` 1921-06-11 → **1921-06-07**, confirmed; election 77 moved
  (`fix_newspapers.py` #20). `james-pottinger-iii` 1919-11-04 now ends 1921-06-07 (`retired`),
  **unconfirmed**: the day of his retiral wasn't found (searched `pottinger`, `pottinger
  resignation`, `town council vacancy`, Mar–Jul 1921).

### 4. Bailie W. Sinclair's resignation, Oct 1921
- **Shetland Times, Sat 8 Oct 1921, p5 (art. 111)**, "Resignation of Bailie Sinclair": a letter
  resigning as Magistrate and Councillor read at the monthly meeting on Tuesday (4 Oct); the
  Provost called it "rather an unexpected resignation".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19211008&page=0005&article=111
- **Same issue, p4 (art. 085)**: he would not reconsider; he could not give the time to all his
  other offices (Parish Council chairman, County Council, Insurance and Pensions Committees).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19211008&page=0004&article=085
- **Ledger**: `william-sinclair` 1920-11-02 ends **1921-10-04** (was 10-01), `resigned`,
  confirmed.
