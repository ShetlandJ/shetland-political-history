# LTC 1885–1889: vacancies, co-options and retiring lists

Researched 2026-09-28 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times). Every link opens the
BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** in the toolbar for the OCR text.

Question: the 8 size-short rows 1885–89 on /data-review (queue item "LTC 1885–1889"). Result:
8 → 5, and the 5 left are dated vacancies (see the end).

In the 1880s the Commissioners of Police met fortnightly on **Friday evenings**, and the paper
often reports "last (Friday) evening" or "yesterday". Nominations closed on the Thursday before
the election.

## Evidence

### 1. November 1885: four seats, and Stove was not retiring
- **Shetland Times, Sat 24 Oct 1885, p2 (art. 040)**: election notice, poll on "TUESDAY, the
  Third day of NOVEMBER", nominations by Thursday 29 Oct. No vacancy count.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18851024&page=0002&article=040
- **Shetland Times, Sat 31 Oct 1885, p2 (art. 024)**: nominations Duncan, Hunter, Mitchell,
  Tulloch jr; they don't exceed the vacancies, so no poll.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18851031&page=0002&article=024
- **Shetland Times, Sat 31 Oct 1885, p3 (art. 039, 043)**: adjourned meeting "on Tuesday evening"
  (27 Oct). Stove present. Bailie Mitchell's letter resigning and not standing again; a deputation
  was sent to talk him round (he stood and was re-elected).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18851031&page=0003&article=043
- **Shetland Times, Sat 21 Nov 1885, p3 (art. 049)**: statutory meeting. Present includes "L. G.
  Stove". The Clerk reported Duncan, Hunter, Mitchell and Tulloch "re-elected ... at the annual
  election on 3d instant". Stove re-elected a Harbour Trustee.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18851121&page=0003&article=049
- **Ledger**: `laurence-stove` 1883-11-06 end 1885-11-03 → **1886-11-02** (his 1883 seat was a
  full one, as with all five winners of 1883). Confirmed. The four 1885 winners' starts confirmed.
  Clears the 1885-11 → 1886-07 short row.

### 2. July–November 1886: two seats left empty, then co-opted
- **Shetland Times, Sat 23 Oct 1886, p2 (art. 044)**, "RETIRING COUNCILLORS": Stove said he
  would rather not stand again; the Chairman wanted Leisk, Stove and Harrison to stay.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18861023&page=0002&article=044
- **Shetland Times, Sat 30 Oct 1886, p2 (art. 033)**: nominations were "the five retiring
  councillors, thus still leaving two seats vacant", which the Council could fill afterwards.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18861030&page=0002&article=033
- **Shetland Times, Sat 20 Nov 1886, p2 (art. 032)**: "on Friday evening" Charles Robertson and
  John B. Anderson were "elected to fill the vacancies". "Yesterday" in the same column is Friday,
  so Friday **19 Nov 1886**.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18861120&page=0002&article=032
- **Ledger**: Jamieson, Halcrow, G. Harrison, Leisk, Stove 1886 starts confirmed. Robertson and
  Anderson start **1886-11-24 → 1886-11-19**; `fix_newspapers.py` #16 moves election 37.
- Not checked: the date of Duncan's resignation (12 Jul 1886, from his profile) and of John
  Harrison's disqualification (1 Oct 1886 in the ledger). A search for `harrison council`
  Jul–Nov 1886 found only meeting attendance lists.

### 3. January–March 1887: Hunter resigns, Porteous elected
- **Shetland Times, Sat 11 Dec 1886, p2 (art. 063)**: James Hunter, accountant of the Union Bank,
  appointed agent at Portsoy.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18861211&page=0002&article=063
- **Shetland Times, Sat 8 Jan 1887, p3 (art. 047, 048)**: adjourned meeting "Tuesday evening"
  (4 Jan). Hunter's resignation accepted; "a constant and useful member for the last ten years".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18870108&page=0003&article=048
- **Shetland Times, Sat 19 Mar 1887, p2 (art. 053, 054)**: fortnightly meeting "yesterday
  (Friday) evening"; Porteous elected "till the vacancy caused [by] Mr Hunter's resignation".
  Sworn in at the next meeting (ST 26 Mar 1887, p2, art. 040).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18870319&page=0002&article=054
- **Ledger**: `james-hunter-ii` 1885 seat ends **1887-01-04, resigned** (was 1887-03-01,
  unknown). Confirmed. Porteous starts **1887-03-18** (was the 24th); `fix_newspapers.py` #16
  moves election 38 and points its replaced member at James Hunter (ii), not the GP born 1914.

### 4. 1887–1888: Jamieson sat until 1888
- **Shetland Times, Sat 6 Oct 1888, p3 (art. 068)**, "RETIRING COUNCILLORS": Bailie Mitchell,
  Tulloch jr and A. B. Jamieson retire, plus one of the five elected last year.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18881006&page=0003&article=068
- **Shetland Times, Sat 3 Nov 1888, p2 (art. 044)**: vacancies from "the retirement [of] Mr
  Tulloch, and the departure from town [of] Mr Jamieson"; Mitchell and Anderson re-nominated,
  Goudie and Laing nominated, no poll.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18881103&page=0002&article=044
- **Ledger**: `andrew-jamieson` 1886 seat ends **1888-11-08** (was 1887-11-01). Confirmed. This
  fills the 1887–88 short row: Jamieson's 1886 seat was the one Duncan had left, due to end in
  1888. Mitchell and Tulloch 1885 rows confirmed.

### 5. 1889: Stove's death and Mitchell's resignation
- **Shetland Times, Sat 19 Oct 1889, p2 (art. 051)**: "At a meeting of the Town Council, held
  last ..." Mitchell's resignation accepted with regret. The scan at that point reads "held last"
  at a line end; the word after is lost. The item above it says the Commissioners met "last
  night" (Friday 18 Oct).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18891019&page=0002&article=051
- **Shetland Times, Sat 26 Oct 1889, p2 (art. 014)**: five vacancies instead of the usual three,
  "one being caused by the lamented death of Mr Stove, and the other by the resignation of Bailie
  Mitchell". Harrison, Leisk and Halcrow retire by rotation.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18891026&page=0002&article=014
- **Ledger**: `alexander-mitchell-i` 1888 seat ends **1889-10-18, resigned** (was 1889-09-01,
  retired). Left unconfirmed because the day isn't certain (open-questions). Halcrow, Harrison,
  Leisk and Stove 1886 rows confirmed.

## Dates not checked
- The Nov 1888 and Nov 1889 generals are dated Thu 8 Nov 1888 and Sat 9 Nov 1889, which don't
  fit the pattern. In 1885 the poll was on the first Tuesday of November, with nominations closing
  the Thursday before. ST Sat 3 Nov 1888 says the election "fell upon Thursday" (1 Nov, the
  nomination day) and no poll was needed, so the election day was probably Tue 6 Nov 1888. Not
  changed; added to the queue.
