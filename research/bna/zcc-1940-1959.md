# ZCC 1940–1959: citations for the generals and by-elections

Researched 2026-09-29 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times). Every link opens the
BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** for the OCR text.

Question: find a source for each Zetland County Council election 1940–1959 (citations batch 2,
fourth run), and check polling days and votes against the DB.

Findings:
- **Dec 1945 general**: polled **Tue 4 Dec 1945**, not Mon 3rd (`fix_newspapers.py` #49; the wiki's
  "Tuesday 3 December" has the wrong date). Seven contests, as in the DB; every readable vote
  agrees, and Bressay (126 voted) and Whiteness (225) add up. Sandsting (Garriock, Sutherland,
  John T. Henry) and Dunrossness South (Boyd, Bairnson, Leslie) had several nominations on 16 Nov
  but were unopposed by 23 Nov, so withdrawals.
- **Sandsting Dec 1945 was Simon Garriock's, not William Henry's** (added 2026-10-01, #75). William
  Henry resigned in May 1945, agreed to stay until December, and wasn't nominated. Garriock sat
  from the first meeting. The wiki's "William Henry" is probably the district council result.
- **May 1949 general**: seven contests, as in the DB. The result report's OCR is shredded by
  columns; only Cunningsburgh (110, 88) and Gulberwick (63, 55) can be read, and both agree.
- **May 1952 general**: three county contests, all agree (Walls 141 cast, 3 spoiled; Dunrossness
  South 280, none spoiled; Unst North 130, 74). The "seven contests" in the report include the
  District Councils.
- **May 1955 general**: "on Tuesday" (10 May), seven contests. All agree except possibly **Yell
  North: the OCR has Youngman 118**, the DB and wiki 119 (Stewart 62). The wiki's turnout of 181
  fits 119 + 62, and "over 47 per cent of 383" fits either. James confirmed 119 (2026-09-29): an OCR misread.
- **May 1958 general**: five contests, all agree, with the turnouts (Nesting majority 1, Whalsay
  10, Delting North 58, Unst North 88, Yell South 12).
- **By-elections**: the wiki pages give a day for each, which the parser had cut to the 1st of the
  month. Twelve dates moved (#50) to the Tuesday meeting or poll. Wrong days in the wiki: Yell
  South 1940 "Tuesday 8 December" (a Sunday; reported 14 Dec, so Tue 10 Dec); Aithsting 1959
  "9 February" (a Monday; the poll was "on Tuesday", 3 Feb).
- **Gulberwick May 1951 was a poll** on Tue 8 May, not an appointment on the 15th: Nicolson 98,
  Prophet Smith (Socialist) 49 (#51; the wiki has Nicolson "Unanimously appointed"). Prophet
  Smith is added unlinked: `prophet-smith` (id 398) may be him, James's call.
- **Council votes, not polls**: Cunningsburgh 1940 (petitions 86 and 72, ballot 18–8), Yell South
  1940 (14–2), Unst South 1942 (11–6, petitions 55 and 19) (`fix_parse_errors.py` #16) and
  Gulberwick 1945 ("After a vote" of the Council, 15–6, `fix_newspapers.py` #52).
- **Aithsting 1959**: the paper names the winner "Mrs Janet C. Anderson"; her page has Catherine
  Janet 'Kate' Anderson. Same person; no change.
- **Burra July 1959**: no report found (three searches, `data/searches.csv`). Dated to the wiki's
  Tue 21 Jul (`fix_parse_errors.py` #15).

## Evidence

### 1. County Council Election December 1945 (elections 667–690)
- **Shetland Times, Fri 16 Nov 1945, p2 (art. 048)**: "COUNTY COUNCIL ELECTION NOMINEES", returned
  unopposed and contests. Names agree with the DB.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19451116&page=0002&article=048
- **Shetland Times, Fri 23 Nov 1945, p2 (art. 034)**: "SEVEN CONTESTS": Bressay, both Northmavines,
  Fetlar, Tingwall, Aithsting and Whiteness and Weisdale.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19451123&page=0002&article=034
- **Shetland Times, Fri 7 Dec 1945, p2 (art. 049)**: "The results of the elections held on
  Tuesday" came out Wednesday and Thursday. County results and voting statistics as in the DB.
  → #49.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19451207&page=0002&article=049
- **Shetland Times, Fri 25 May 1945, p3 (art. 059)**: County Council meeting. A letter from
  William Henry, "the representative for Sandsting, intimating his resignation"; agreed "to ask
  Mr Henry to remain on the Council till the elections in December". → #75.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19450525&page=0003&article=059
- **Shetland Times, Fri 24 Aug 1945, p3 (art. 059)**: County Council meeting (W. Henry present).
  His letter: he "had accepted the Council's request, and would continue as a Councillor till the
  elections this year". → #75.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19450824&page=0003&article=059
- **Shetland Times, Fri 16 Nov 1945, p2 (art. 048)**, again: the Sandsting county nominees were
  "Simon Garriock, Westerwick, Wester Skeld; John Sutherland, Bixter; John T. Henry, The Holm,
  East Burrafirth". No William Henry. Sutherland was also nominated for Aithsting, which he won
  (165 to Clark's 139). → #75.
- **Shetland Times, Fri 7 Dec 1945, p2 (art. 050)**: district council results. Sandsting
  successful: "J. G. Williamson, 86; Creighton G. Williamson, 84; John Sutherland, 76; William
  Henry, 75; Michael Irvine, 71". This is the district council, not the county seat.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19451207&page=0002&article=050
- **Shetland Times, Fri 21 Dec 1945, p3 (art. 052)**: first meeting of the new Council (Convener
  Ganson re-appointed). Present include "John Sutherland" and "Simon Garriock"; no Henry. Same in
  the January meeting (ST 25 Jan 1946, p3, art. 044: "J. Sutherland", "S. Garriock"). → #75.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19451221&page=0003&article=052
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19460125&page=0003&article=044
