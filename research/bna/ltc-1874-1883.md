# LTC 1874–1883: results, nominations and retiring lists

Researched 2026-09-28 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times). Every link opens the
BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** in the toolbar for the OCR text.

Question: source the 1874–1883 elections (21–32) and the 37 confirmed ledger rows from the
April 2026 research that had no recorded source (citations queue, batch 1). Both ends of each
row: the result or nominations that started it, and the next retiring list that ended it.

Findings: every row agrees with the ledger except the **Nov 1881 general, which was on Tue 1 Nov,
not the 8th** (`fix_newspapers.py` #25; 10 ledger dates moved). Until 15 Mar 1875 the paper came
out on **Mondays**; Saturdays from 20 Mar 1875 (`build.py`'s publication-day check updated).
The 1876 result, votes and electorate match the DB. Every general 1877–1883 was unopposed.

## Evidence

### 1. Sept 1874 (elections 21, 22)
- **Shetland Times, Mon 7 Sep 1874, p3 (art. 032)**: Charles Gilbert Duncan, Acting Chief
  Magistrate, certifies Arthur James Hay Senior Bailie and John Robertson senior Junior Bailie,
  3 Sep 1874. These are the first group's Bailies (election 21), the group the minute book
  (p258) records as invalid. Nothing here on the second group; the 14 Sep report of the
  Commissioners' first monthly meeting is too garbled to say which group met.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18740907&page=0003&article=032

### 2. Nov 1876, the first election under the 1876 Act (election 23)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 28 Oct 1876, p3 (art. 045)**: notice of "the first Election of Town
  Council and Commissioners of Police" under the Lerwick Act of 1876 on Tuesday 7 Nov; twelve
  to be elected. So the 1874 council's terms all ended at 7 Nov 1876 (inferred).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18761028&page=0003&article=045
- **Shetland Times, Sat 11 Nov 1876, p2 (art. 050)**: result of the poll. Hunter 119, Cameron 112,
  G. H. B. Hay 110, Laurenson 106, Sievwright 106, J. Robertson sen. 105, A. J. Hay 104,
  R. Sinclair 92, W. Robertson 72, Jamieson 70, Leisk 69, L. G. Stove 69; not elected
  Sandison 68, W. S. Smith 63, Duncan 60, A. S. Stove 52, Scott 52, Gordon 17. "The list of
  voters comprised 252 names" (zoomed: the OCR reads 202), 187 voted. All match the DB. The
  ranking fits the ledger's cohorts: 9th–12th out in 1877, 5th–8th in 1878, top four in 1879.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18761111&page=0002&article=050

### 3. Nov 1877 (election 24)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 27 Oct 1877, p2 (art. 024)**: four councillors to be elected on Tuesday
  6 Nov; nominations by Thursday 1 Nov.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18771027&page=0002&article=024
- **Shetland Times, Sat 3 Nov 1877, p2 (art. 044)**: "The four retiring Councillors were all
  re-nominated", no others, so Jamieson, Robertson, Leisk and Stove to be declared on Tuesday.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18771103&page=0002&article=044

### 4. Jan 1878 co-option (election 25)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 26 Jan 1878, p2 (art. 028)**: special meeting "on Thursday evening"
  (24 Jan) for the vacancy "caused by the resignation of Bailie Sievwright". John Robertson
  junior "unanimously elected Councillor". Laurenson declined the Junior Bailieship.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18780126&page=0002&article=028

### 5. Nov 1878 (election 26)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 2 Nov 1878, p1 (art. 018)**: Town Clerk's notice of 1 Nov: A. J. Hay,
  John Robertson sen., John Robertson jun. and Robert Sinclair proposed; no more than the
  vacancies, so no poll. The four proposed are the four due out (inferred for the three 1876
  rows and Robertson jr's co-opted seat).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18781102&page=0001&article=018

### 6. Nov 1879 (election 27)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 1 Nov 1879, p2 (art. 057)**: four retiring; Major Cameron, G. H. B. Hay
  and James Hunter re-nominated. Arthur Laurenson "would not allow himself to be re-nominated",
  leaving a vacancy "to be filled up by the vote of the remainder". No co-option found before
  the 1880 general (search below): Goudie was nominated for it then.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18791101&page=0002&article=057

### 7. Nov 1880 general and co-option (elections 28, 29)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 30 Oct 1880, p2 (art. 043)**: W. Robertson, Jamieson, L. G. Stove and
  Leisk re-nominated, "while Mr James M. Goudie, ironmonger, was nominated to fill the other
  vacancy"; no poll.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18801030&page=0002&article=043
- **Shetland Times, Sat 11 Dec 1880, p2 (art. 037)**: William Duncan, "at last meeting ...
  unanimously elected" to the place vacant "by the resignation of Mr James M. Goudie", took the
  oath. The meeting day isn't given, so the DB's 25 Nov 1880 is unchecked.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18801211&page=0002&article=037

### 8. Nov 1881 (election 30)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 22 Oct 1881, p2 (art. 018)**: election to supply five vacancies "on
  Tuesday the first day of November next"; nominations by Thursday 27 Oct. **The DB had 8 Nov.**
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18811022&page=0002&article=018
- **Shetland Times, Sat 29 Oct 1881, p2 (art. 026)**: "Tuesday first is the day fixed". Five
  vacancies: Bailies Robertson and Hay, Robert Sinclair and J. Robertson jr in rotation, and
  Duncan, "elected to fill a vacancy", must seek re-election. All re-nominated except Sinclair
  (health); James Tulloch jr nominated for the fifth. No poll. Tulloch's one-year seat fits.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18811029&page=0002&article=026

### 9. Nov 1882 (election 31)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 4 Nov 1882, p2 (art. 040)**: of the four retiring, Major Cameron, James
  Hunter and James Tulloch re-nominated; Mr Hay "was not again nominated"; Alexander Mitchell,
  banker, nominated for the fourth vacancy.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18821104&page=0002&article=040

### 10. Nov 1883 (election 32)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 27 Oct 1883, p2 (art. 067)**: five vacancies: Jamieson, Leisk and
  L. G. Stove, whose term has expired, and "the resignation Major Cameron and the decease of Mr
  William Robertson".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18831027&page=0002&article=067
- **Shetland Times, Sat 3 Nov 1883, p2 (art. 083)**: the three re-nominated; Frank H. Halcrow and
  Gilbert Harrison nominated to the other two.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18831103&page=0002&article=083
- **Shetland Times, Sat 10 Nov 1883, p2 (art. 094)**: the five new councillors attended and
  declared their acceptance.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18831110&page=0002&article=094
