# LTC 1886: Duncan's resignation and John Harrison's disqualification

Researched 2026-09-28 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times). Every link opens the
BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** in the toolbar for the OCR text.

Question: the ledger ends William Duncan (i)'s 1885 seat on 12 Jul 1886 (resigned, from his
profile) and John Harrison (i)'s 1884 seat on 1 Oct 1886 (disqualified, generator guess). Neither
date had been checked in the paper. Result: both dates are now sourced as far as the paper goes,
but each row turns on a point for James (open-questions). No ledger dates changed; 0 issues (30).

## Evidence

### 1. Duncan's letter, July 1886
- **Shetland Times, Sat 17 Jul 1886, p2 (art. 043)**: "The quarterly statutory meeting ... on
  Monday" at noon, so **Mon 12 Jul 1886**. Present: Bailie Robertson, Leisk, J. Robertson jr,
  G. Harrison, Stove, Tulloch jr. Duncan absent.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18860717&page=0002&article=043
- **Same issue, p2 (art. 045)**, "RESIGNATION OF MR WM. DUNCAN": the Clerk read his letter; he
  intended to withdraw, and "three weeks hence he would cease" to have any connection with the
  council. A deputation (the Chairman and John Robertson jr) was sent to ask him to reconsider.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18860717&page=0002&article=045
- **Shetland Times, Sat 31 Jul 1886, p2 (art. 055)**: meeting on Friday evening (30 Jul). Present:
  Bailie Robertson, Leisk, J. Robertson jr, Stove. Duncan not there.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18860731&page=0002&article=055

### 2. Duncan's refusal, October 1886
- **Shetland Times, Sat 16 Oct 1886, p2 (art. 045)**: the quarterly meeting "on Monday" (11 Oct)
  adjourned to Friday evening (**15 Oct**) because of the hour.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18861016&page=0002&article=045
- **Same issue, p2 (art. 048)**, "RESIGNATION OF A COUNCILLOR": the deputation reported he "had
  definitely refused to continue in office"; G. Harrison made interim Convener of Lighting and
  Cleansing in his place. This matches his profile ("accepted by the council on October 15th").
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18861016&page=0002&article=048
- **Ledger**: `william-duncan-i` 1885-11-03 row keeps its 12 Jul 1886 end, unconfirmed, source
  updated. Options for the end date are in open-questions: 12 Jul (letter read), about 2 Aug (his
  own "three weeks hence"), or 15 Oct (refusal reported, the council's acceptance).

### 3. John Harrison: "will be disqualified"
- **Shetland Times, Sat 16 Oct 1886, p2 (art. 035)**, local news: G. Harrison, Stove, Halcrow,
  Leisk and Jamieson retire by rotation; "Mr Wm. Duncan has resigned, and one member will [be]
  disqualified", making seven vacancies at the coming election. Future tense, after the 15 Oct
  meeting, so John Harrison (i) was still a member then and the ledger's 1 Oct is too early.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18861016&page=0002&article=035
- **Shetland Times, Sat 30 Oct 1886, p2 (art. 033)**: nominations were the five retiring
  councillors, "thus still leaving two seats vacant" (Duncan's and Harrison's). No reason given.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18861030&page=0002&article=033
