# Thomas Johnston (i)/(ii) and James A. Smith (i): which man held which seat

Researched 2026-10-01 in the Shetland Times (BNA). Raised by an outside review of the person links.

## Thomas Johnston: both seats were the solicitor's, Thomas Johnston (i) (1873–1936)

The wiki contradicts itself. The election pages link the 1922 Cunningsburgh ZCC seat to (i) and
the 1932 Lerwick Town Council seat to (ii). (i)'s page and the disambiguation page give him the
Town Council, and (ii)'s succession box gives Tammy Cunningsburgh 1922–25. The papers give both
seats to the solicitor, and Tammy no Cunningsburgh seat at all.

### 1. The 1932 Town Council result
- **Shetland Times, Sat 5 Nov 1932, p4 (art. 025)**: the candidates included "Mr Thomas Johnston,
  solicitor, who stood as an independent candidate".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19321105&page=0004&article=025
- Supports: candidacy 548 (Lerwick Town Council Election November 1932) is `thomas-johnston-i`, not (ii).

### 2. The solicitor's obituary
- **Shetland Times, Sat 7 Nov 1936, p4 (art. 073)**: "Obituary. Mr Thomas Johnston, Solicitor."
  In December 1922 he "was elected to represent the electoral division of Cunningsburgh", served
  one full three-year term and then retired from the Council. "Though not in robust health", he
  stood for the Lerwick Town Council in November 1932 and was elected, but "was not able to attend
  many meetings". (The OCR gives his retirement as "November, 1931", a misprint or OCR slip for 1934.)
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19361107&page=0004&article=073
- The same obituary says he stayed with Hay & Co. at Lerwick "until early in 1925" and qualified
  as a solicitor in April 1925. The wiki biography's "He stayed with the shipbuilders until 1925"
  is a misreading: he was with Duthie's in Aberdeen about five years, well before the war.
- **Shetland Times, Sat 14 Nov 1936, p5 (art. 146)**: pulpit reference to "Mr Thomas Johnston,
  solicitor": broken health for three years.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19361114&page=0005&article=146
- Supports: the 1922 Cunningsburgh link to (i) is right; the 1932 seat (and the ledger term
  `thomas-johnston-ii@1932-11-01`, with its four citations) belongs to `thomas-johnston-i`. The
  ill-health leave of Nov 1933 and the resignation of Oct 1934 fit him.

### 3. The 1922 Cunningsburgh candidate
- **Shetland Times, Sat 25 Nov 1922, p5 (art. 096)**: nominations: "Cunningsburgh—Mr L. Anderson,
  Hool... and Mr Thomas Johnston, Lower Leog".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19221125&page=0005&article=096
- **Shetland Times, Sat 2 Dec 1922, p4 (art. 095)**: his address to the electors of Cunningsburgh,
  signed "Thomas Johnston, Lower Leog, Lerwick, 28th November, 1922".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19221202&page=0004&article=095
- **Shetland Times, Sat 15 Jul 1922, p4 (art. 051)**: "Mr John Johnston, younger son of Mr Thomas
  Johnston, Lower Leog", won a university bursary. Tammy (b. 1894) could not have a student son.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19220715&page=0004&article=051
- **Shetland Times, Sat 8 May 1920, p4 (art. 092)**: "Mrs Johnston, Lower Leog" appointed assistant
  teacher at Lerwick Central School (the solicitor's wife was a teacher).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19200508&page=0004&article=092

### 4. Tammy's first seat was Gulberwick, 1925
- **Shetland Times, Sat 14 Nov 1925, p4 (art. 092)**: nominations: Gulberwick, T. J. Anderson
  (sitting member) "and Thomas Johnston, North Roadside, Lerwick"; Cunningsburgh, M. L. Manson and
  Rev. Simson Wallace (no sitting member: the solicitor retired).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19251114&page=0004&article=092
- Supports: (ii)'s ZCC service starts at Gulberwick in 1925. The Cunningsburgh 1922–25 box on his
  wiki page, and the disambiguation page's "first for Cunningsburgh", are wrong.

## James A. Smith: one man, "Ooie Jeemie", James Smith (i)

- **Shetland Times, Sat 25 Nov 1922, p5 (art. 096)**: nominations: "Yell (south)—Mr T. R. Manson,
  Newtown, Westsandwick; and Mr James A. Smith, Hope Villa, Lerwick."
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19221125&page=0005&article=096
- **Shetland Times, Sat 28 Nov 1931, p4 (art. 068)**: court report, witness "James A. Smith,
  ex-Convener of the County, Hope Villa, St. Olaf Street, Lerwick", who "belonged to Mid-Yell" and
  came to Lerwick about ten years before.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19311128&page=0004&article=068
- **Shetland Times, Sat 3 Nov 1934, p4 (art. 045)**: Town Council candidates: "James A. Smith,
  Hope Villa, 90 St. Olaf Street".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19341103&page=0004&article=045
- Supports: the Yell South member (1922–32), the 1928–29 Convener and the 1934 Town Council
  candidate (later Provost) are the same Mid Yell man at Hope Villa. Born Mid Yell, moved to
  Lerwick after WWI, as his profile says. No change. The Bressay 1933 by-election was not checked
  separately; nothing suggests another man.
