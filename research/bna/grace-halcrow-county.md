# Grace Halcrow on Zetland County Council

Researched 2026-09-27 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times, Fridays). Click
**Articles** in the viewer toolbar for the OCR text.

Question: her profile says County Councillor for Cunningsburgh "between 1955 and 1961". The ST
preview of 17 Apr 1964 (p4 art. 054, in `ltc-1955-1965.md`) says one three-year term, so 1955–58?

The ZCC data already had Joan McLeod returned unopposed for Cunningsburgh in May 1958 (election
779), and McLeod again in 1961, 1964 and 1967. So the profile was the only thing wrong.

## Evidence

- **ST Fri 25 Apr 1958, p4 (art. 062)**: "Six County Council elections". The sitting member for
  Cunningsburgh, Miss Grace Halcrow, "is being opposed by Mrs Joan McLeod", the first time a
  division had an all-female choice.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19580425&page=0004&article=062
- **ST Fri 9 May 1958, p4 (art. 051)**: "County elections". "Tuesday first is polling day in the
  county." Halcrow "has withdrawn from Cunningsburgh", leaving McLeod returned unopposed.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19580509&page=0004&article=051

**Edit**: `fix_newspapers.py` #9: intro "between 1955 and 1961" → "between 1955 and 1958". No
ledger or election change. Logged in `corrections-log.md`.

## Also found

"Tuesday first is polling day in the county" (9 May 1958) puts the 1958 ZCC poll on **Tue 13 May
1958**. The DB has 1958-05-12, a Monday: the same slip as the LTC dates 1949–64. The ZCC generals
1949–64 are all on Mondays in the DB (1955 is on a Thursday). Queued.
