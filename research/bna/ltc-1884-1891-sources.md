# LTC 1884–1891: row sources for the 1884–89 vacancies

Researched 2026-10-10 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times). Queue item "1884–1891
row sources".

Question: source the rows sitting through the 1884–89 size-short runs that weren't blocked by an
open question: the two John Robertsons' 1884 seats (start), and the 1888 cohort's ends.

Findings:
- **Nov 1884 general**: polled Tue 4 Nov; the winners and their votes match the DB. Both John
  Robertson rows confirmed. Harrison's start now sourced (his end is still an open question).
- **1891 retiring list**: Anderson, Leisk, Goudie and Laing. Anderson didn't re-stand, which
  matches the 1891 oath report (Leisk, Goudie, Laing, Tulloch). Leisk's 1889 row confirmed.
  The 1888 rows (Laing, Goudie, Anderson) now cite both ends but stay unconfirmed: their start
  waits on the 1888 date question.
- Issues cleared: none. Each size-short run still has a row blocked by an open question
  (Harrison, Duncan 1885, Mitchell, the 1888 date; Daniel for 1961).

## Evidence

### 1. Nov 1884 general (election 33)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 8 Nov 1884, p3 (art. 043)**: "Municipal Election". The poll was on
  Tuesday in the Town Hall, for four vacancies. Result: Bailie Hay 87, John Robertson jr 74,
  Bailie Robertson 71, John Harrison 71, William Duncan 44. Charles Robertson's and Dr Pole's
  figures are lost in the OCR (DB 22 and 17, not checked).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18841108&page=0003&article=043
- **Ledger**: `john-robertson-i@1884-11-04` and `john-robertson-ii@1884-11-04` confirmed (end:
  ST 29 Oct 1887). `john-harrison-i@1884-11-04` start sourced, still unconfirmed.

### 2. Nov 1891 retiring list
- **Shetland Times, Sat 24 Oct 1891, p2 (art. 038)**: leader on the coming nominations. The
  four retiring are Bailie J. B. Anderson, John Leisk, J. M. Goudie and A. L. Laing; "the
  first-named gentleman does not intend to seek re-election".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18911024&page=0002&article=038
- **Ledger**: `arthur-laing`, `james-goudie` and `john-anderson@1888-11-08` end sourced, still
  unconfirmed (1888 date). `john-leisk@1889-11-05` confirmed (start: ST 9 Nov 1889).
