# LTC 1920–1923: retiring lists

Researched 2026-09-28 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times). Every link opens the
BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** in the toolbar for the OCR text.

Question: resolve the George Duffin overlap (elected 1920 and again 1922) (queue item "1922").

## Evidence

### 1. October 1920: four rotation seats
- **Shetland Times, Sat 16 Oct 1920, p1 (art. 009)**: election notice for four councillors "in
  the place of" Peter S. Goodlad, Robert D. Ganson, Alexander Ratter and William Sinclair, "who
  retire by rotation".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19201016&page=0001&article=009
- So Ratter, W. Sinclair, Ganson and Duffin (elected 2 Nov 1920) all took rotation seats, not ad
  interim ones.

### 2. October 1921: six vacancies
- **Shetland Times, Sat 8 Oct 1921, p5 (art. 115)**, "The Greetin' Meetin'": the Clerk announced
  the retiring councillors as J. Smith, R. H. Ramsay, J. T. J. Sinclair and R. Ollason "in the
  ordinary course", J. M. Goodlad as an ad interim member, and Bailie Sinclair "in virtue of his
  resignation".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19211008&page=0005&article=115
- **Shetland Times, Sat 15 Oct 1921, p4 (art. 121)**: election notice for six councillors, same
  names.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19211015&page=0004&article=121
- **Ledger**: `john-sinclair`, `robert-ollason`, `robert-ramsay` 1919-11-04 (ending 1921)
  confirmed.

### 3. October 1922: Duffin retires by rotation
- **Shetland Times, Sat 14 Oct 1922, p4 (art. 066)**: local news: the retiring councillors are
  James Laing, M. L. Manson, James Morrison and G. H. Duffin (OCR garbled on the last name;
  confirmed by the notice below).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19221014&page=0004&article=066
- **Shetland Times, Sat 14 Oct 1922, p4 (art. 083)**: election notice: four councillors in place
  of Laing, Magnus Laurence Manson, Morrison and George Herbert Duffin, "who retire by rotation".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19221014&page=0004&article=083
- **Shetland Times, Sat 4 Nov 1922, p5 (art. 283)**: "No Poll": the four retiring councillors were
  renominated and returned unopposed.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19221104&page=0005&article=283
- **Ledger**: `george-duffin` 1920-11-02 now ends 1922-11-07 (was 1923-11-06), which clears the
  overlap. `james-laing`, `james-morrison`, `magnus-manson` 1919-11-04 confirmed.

### 4. October 1923: A. S. Manson and Murray retire
- **Shetland Times, Sat 6 Oct 1923, p4 (art. 058)**: the four who retire by rotation are "Provost
  Ganson, Bailie Ratter, and Councillors A. S. Manson and D. Murray".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19231006&page=0004&article=058
- **Shetland Times, Sat 13 Oct 1923, p4 (art. 094)**: election notice, same four.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19231013&page=0004&article=094
- **Ledger**: `alexander-manson` 1921-11-01 now ends 1923-11-06 (was 1922-11-07); `david-murray`
  1921-11-01, `alexander-ratter` and `robert-ganson-i` 1920-11-02 confirmed.

## Not settled

- **Why Duffin went a year early.** He was elected in 1920 to a rotation seat, but retired "by
  rotation" in 1922, while both of the lowest 1921 winners (A. S. Manson and Murray) sat until
  1923. The Provost (Ganson) was in the 1923 list, so this isn't the usual Provost swap. It may
  be how the council allocated the two extra 1921 seats (Goodlad's ad interim seat, due 1922, and
  W. Sinclair's, due 1923). The dates are settled by the lists either way. The October 1921 or
  1922 meeting report might give the reason.
- `james-goodlad` 1921-06-11 (by-election start) and `william-sinclair` 1920-11-02 (resignation,
  ledger 1921-10-01) left unconfirmed: dates not checked.
