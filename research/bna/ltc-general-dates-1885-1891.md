# LTC general election days 1885–1891

Researched 2026-09-28 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times). Every link opens the
BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** in the toolbar for the OCR text.

Question: the Nov 1888 and Nov 1889 generals are dated Thu 8 Nov 1888 and Sat 9 Nov 1889, off
the first-Tuesday pattern of the other years (queue item "LTC general dates 1885–1891").
Result: 1889 moved to Tue 5 Nov. 1888 left at 8 Nov and raised in open-questions. 1885–87 and
1890–91 are already on the first Tuesday, and what was found for 1890–91 fits.

The pattern: nominations closed on the **Thursday**, and the poll (if needed) was on the
**Tuesday** after, the first Tuesday of November. When nominations didn't exceed the vacancies
there was no poll. In 1885 the Clerk still dated the unopposed return to the Tuesday ("the annual
election on 3d instant", ST 21 Nov 1885, in `ltc-1885-1889.md`).

## Evidence

### 1. November 1889: polled Tuesday 5 November
- **Shetland Times, Sat 2 Nov 1889, p2 (art. 078)**: Burgh of Lerwick notice of the persons
  nominated, and "municipal election on Tuesday next" (5 Nov).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18891102&page=0002&article=078
- **Shetland Times, Sat 2 Nov 1889, p2 (art. 082)**, "Next Week": "Municipal Election Voting
  Tuesday, in the Burgh Court Room".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18891102&page=0002&article=082
- **Shetland Times, Sat 2 Nov 1889, p3 (art. 097)**: a committee formed to re-elect Halcrow,
  [Harrison: OCR garbled] and Leisk and to elect Kay and Hunter. A contest (the wiki has the votes).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18891102&page=0003&article=097
- **Shetland Times, Sat 9 Nov 1889, p2 (art. 052)**: "The municipal fight is over"; Halcrow,
  Harrison, Leisk, Hunter and Kay returned. This is the 9th in the wiki: the day it was printed.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18891109&page=0002&article=052
- **Edit**: election 41 date 1889-11-09 → **1889-11-05** (`fix_newspapers.py` #17). Ledger: the
  three 1886 rows ending then and the five 1889 rows starting then moved (8 dates); source added
  to the 1889 rows, still `confirmed=0` (end dates unchecked). The Oct–Nov 1889 vacancy row now
  ends 5 Nov.

### 2. November 1888: nominations Thursday 1 November, no poll
- **Shetland Times, Sat 3 Nov 1888, p2 (art. 044)**: "The Annual Municipal Election here, as
  elsewhere, fell upon Thursday"; at the close of nominations Mitchell, Anderson, Goudie and
  Laing were nominated for the vacancies, so "there will be no polling required".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18881103&page=0002&article=044
- **Shetland Times, Sat 17 Nov 1888, p2 (art. 081)**: statutory meeting "on Monday noon"
  (12 Nov), with Goudie and Laing present. Doesn't date the election.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18881117&page=0002&article=081
- No Burgh election notice found for 1888: `municipal election`, `election november`,
  `commissioners election`, `tuesday november` and `burgh lerwick`, Oct–Nov 1888.
- **Edit**: none. The 8th fits neither reading. 1 Nov (the paper's "election") or Tue 6 Nov (the
  1885 convention) is James's call: see open-questions.

### 3. 1890 and 1891: fit the pattern
- **Shetland Times, Sat 25 Oct 1890, p2 (art. 066)**: "Thursday first is the last day for
  nominating" (30 Oct); retiring Bailie Robertson (Chief Magistrate), Bailie C. Robertson,
  John Robertson jun. and Porteous. Poll day would be Tue 4 Nov, as in the ledger.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18901025&page=0002&article=066
- **Shetland Times, Sat 31 Oct 1891, p2 (art. 040)**: nominations "On Thursday" (29 Oct).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18911031&page=0002&article=040
- **Shetland Times, Sat 7 Nov 1891, p2 (art. 073)**: Leisk, Goudie, Laing and Tulloch took the
  oath at the Council Chamber on Thursday (5 Nov).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18911107&page=0002&article=073
- **Edit**: none; 1890-11-04 and 1891-11-03 are the Tuesdays after nomination day.
