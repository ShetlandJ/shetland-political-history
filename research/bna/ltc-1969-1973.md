# LTC 1969–1973: retiring lists, the Provosts who stayed on, and Adair's co-option

Researched 2026-09-28 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times, published on Fridays).
Every link opens the BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** in the toolbar for the OCR text.

Question: clear the overlaps for William Smith (iv) (1969), William Peterson (1972) and Eric Gray
(1973). The draft ledger gave every winner a 3-year term. From 1969 the rotation shifted a year at a
time: Provost Eric Gray stayed on in 1969 and Provost W. A. Smith in 1972, each time someone else
retired in their place, and the knock-on moved seats between cohorts.

## Evidence

### 1969: W. A. Smith retires in place of Provost Gray
- **ST Fri 14 Mar 1969, p1 (art. 002)**: retiring are "Messrs M. M. Shearer, A. Morrison, J. R.
  Smith (all elected in 1966) and W A. Smith (least successful candidate in 1967)". Eric Gray,
  also elected in 1966, is Provost and not in the list.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19690314&page=0001&article=002
- Ledger: `william-smith-iv` 1967 ends 1969-05-06 (was 1970); `eric-gray` 1966 runs to 1971-05-04
  (was 1969); Morrison, Shearer (ii) and J. R. Smith 1966 confirmed.

### 1970: Halcrow retires by rotation; J. R. Smith resigns; Adair co-opted
- **ST Fri 13 Mar 1970, p9 (art. 196)**: "Both Lerwick's bailies are due to retire by rotation":
  Bailies Robertson and Taylor, G. W. Blance and Miss G. M. T. Halcrow.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19700313&page=0009&article=196
- **ST Fri 17 Apr 1970, p1 (art. 002)**: seven candidates for four seats; Blance and Taylor
  re-stand, Robertson and Halcrow "did not lodge papers" (she blamed the winter's weather).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19700417&page=0001&article=002
- **ST Fri 17 Apr 1970, p1 (art. 003)**: "on Tuesday night a letter from Mr John R. Smith was
  read, intimating his resignation" (Tue 14 Apr 1970). The council "will be one member short when
  the result is declared".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19700417&page=0001&article=003
- **ST Fri 15 May 1970, p11 (art. 097)**: Adair co-opted "at Friday's statutory meeting" (Fri 8 May
  1970) as "the unsuccessful candidate with the highest number of votes".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19700515&page=0011&article=097
- Ledger: `grace-halcrow` 1968 ends 1970-05-05 (was 1971); Blance, Taylor, Robertson 1967
  confirmed; `john-smith-ii` 1969 ends 1970-04-14, resigned (was 1970-08-08, "replaced");
  `robert-adair` starts 1970-05-08 (was 1970-08-08). `fix_newspapers.py` #11 moves the
  "By-Election May 1970" to 8 May and adds a note; #5 now ends Halcrow's intro "to 1970".
- The 14 Apr – 8 May 1970 gap now shows as an 11-member row on /data-review: a genuine vacancy.

### 1971: five retiring, unopposed
- **ST Fri 12 Mar 1971, p8 (art. 061)**: "Five members ... are due to retire in May": Provost Eric
  Gray, Hon. Treas. Harry Gray, R. P. Cumming, W. P. Thomson and R. Adair.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19710312&page=0008&article=061
- **ST Fri 16 Apr 1971, p8 (nominations report; the search hit was art. 050, "NOTES", on the same
  page)**: all returning except W. P. L. Thomson. The 1972 and 1973 reports ("second" and "third
  year in succession" with no election) confirm 1971 was unopposed.
- Ledger: Adair 1970, Cumming, H. Gray and Thomson 1968 end 1971 as drafted.

### 1972: Tait and Peterson retire a year early; Provost W. A. Smith stays on
- **ST Fri 17 Mar 1972, p9 (art. 090)**: retiring before the 2 May election are "Mr Alex.
  Morrison, Mr J. J. Paton, Mr W. Peterson and Mr A. J. R. Tait". Peterson and Tait were elected in
  1970; W. A. Smith (1969) is Provost and not in the list.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19720317&page=0009&article=090
- **ST Fri 14 Apr 1972, p1 (art. 001)**: no election "for the second year in succession"; four
  nominations (Laurenson, and retiring Morrison, Paton, Peterson). Provost W. A. Smith regretted
  Tait was not standing.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19720414&page=0001&article=001
- Ledger: `andrew-tait` and `william-peterson` 1970 end 1972-05-02 (were 1973); Morrison and
  Paton 1969 confirmed; `william-smith-iv` 1969 runs to abolition (was 1972).

### 1973: Eric Gray and Butler had two-year seats from 1971
- **ST Fri 13 Apr 1973, p1 (art. 002)**: "For the third year in succession" no poll: "only the four
  retiring councillors had been nominated". The list is Blance, Butler, and (OCR cut off) the other
  two; the 1973 winners are Blance, Butler, E. Gray and Taylor.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19730413&page=0001&article=002
- Ledger: `eric-gray` and `john-butler` 1971 end 1973-05-01 (were 1975 and 1972); Blance and Taylor
  1970 confirmed.

## Not settled

- **How the 1971 seats were split.** Five were elected unopposed for three 3-year seats and two
  2-year ones. Eric Gray and Butler got the short ones, but no report of a draw or of the reason was
  found (`town council lots`, Apr–Jul 1971). No ledger edit depends on it.
- **1968 polling day.** The DB has the May 1968 general on Thursday 2 May; every other year from
  1949 was a Tuesday. The 1968 rows (Cumming, H. Gray, Thomson, Halcrow) have their end dates
  sourced but are left `confirmed=0` until the date is checked. Added to the queue.
- Rows ending at abolition (1975-05-15) are left `confirmed=0`, with sources for their starts:
  the abolition date itself is still unresolved (CLAUDE.md, "Council end dates").
