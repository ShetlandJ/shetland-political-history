# James Laing's Town Council service: the 1933–37 break

Question (2026-11-10): the biography says he "served on the Town Council between 1906 and his
retirement in 1945". The ledger has him out from Nov 1933 to Nov 1937. The 1945 end (retiral
from 29 Nov 1945) is already sourced in `ltc-1936-1945.md` §6.

### 1. Defeated at the Nov 1933 general (election 92)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 11 Nov 1933, p4 (art. 061)**: "Three Ex-Provosts Defeated". The retiring
  Councillors were Ollason and ex-Provosts Laing, J. T. J. Sinclair and W. Sinclair; "only one of
  the retiring Councillors was returned".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19331111&page=0004&article=061
- **Shetland Times, Sat 11 Nov 1933, p4 (art. 065)** "The Result" (read from the page image; the
  OCR gives "244"): Halcrow 1,074, Ollason 958, C. A. Manson 908, Mrs Nicol 880, W. Sinclair 678,
  J. T. J. Sinclair 609, **J. Laing 544**, Mrs Sutherland 532. Matches the DB.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19331111&page=0004&article=065
- Ledger: `james-laing` 1930-11-04 ends 1933-11-07 (unchanged).

### 2. Not a candidate, and not co-opted, 1934–36
- 1934: the nine candidates (ST 3 Nov 1934 p4 a044) don't include him.
- **1935: Shetland Times, Sat 2 Nov 1935, p4 (art. 078)**, "Seven Candidates for Four Seats":
  Duffin, Ganson, A. Manson, M. L. Manson, Mrs Nicol, T. A. Sinclair, John Sutherland. No Laing.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19351102&page=0004&article=078
- 1936: unopposed; the four candidates were Halcrow, Irvine, Shearer and T. A. Sinclair (ST 31 Oct
  1936 p4 a074).
- Co-options Nov 1933–Nov 1937 (all confirmed ledger rows): T. A. Sinclair 7 Jul 1936, Irvine
  4 Aug 1936 and Clausen 10 Nov 1936. None was Laing.

### 3. Returned at the top of the poll, Tue 2 Nov 1937 (election 99)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 6 Nov 1937, p4 (art. 068)** "The Candidates": Laing (ex-Provost) with the
  Labour party representatives, "all but Mr Laing seeking municipal honours for the first time".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19371106&page=0004&article=068
- ST 6 Nov 1937 p4 a072 "The Result": James Laing first, 979 (already on file).
- Ledger: `james-laing` 1937-11-02 (unchanged).

### Conclusion
He sat 1906–1933 and 1937–1945, not continuously from 1906. The person-page sidebar already
shows this ("Served Lerwick Town Council 1906–1933, 1937–1945"). The 1946 on the page is his death
(31 Aug 1946). The biography sentence is wrong.
