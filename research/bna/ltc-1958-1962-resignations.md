# LTC resignations: James Tait (1958) and James Daniel (1962)

Researched 2026-09-27 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times, published on Fridays in
these years). Click **Articles** in the viewer toolbar for the OCR text.

Question: when did Tait and Daniel resign, so their 1958 and 1960 terms can end on the right
date with `end_reason=resigned`? Follows on from `ltc-1955-1965.md`.

## Evidence

### James W. Tait, 1958
- **ST Fri 6 Jun 1958, p4 (art. 050)**: News in brief. Tait, who topped the poll in May and was
  made Bailie and Dean of Guild, "has had to intimate his resignation": the G.P.O. was
  transferring him to Edinburgh.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19580606&page=0004&article=050
- **ST Fri 20 Jun 1958, p2 (art. 021)**: "Bailie J. W. Tait's departure". His formal resignation
  was submitted "at last Tuesday's Council meeting" (Tue 17 Jun 1958), leaving the Council to fix
  the operative date. The Council "decided not to appoint a successor until next Tuesday", so he
  "left Shetland as a Bailie" (on Sat 14 Jun). The town clerk said a resignation normally took
  effect after three weeks, or earlier if the notice gave a date.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19580620&page=0002&article=021
- Johnson was co-opted at the special meeting of Tue 24 Jun 1958 (ST 27 Jun 1958, already in
  `ltc-1955-1965.md`).

**Ledger edit**: `james-tait` 1958-05-06: `end_reason` replaced → resigned, `confirmed=1`. End
date kept at 1958-06-24: the Council held the seat open until then and he stayed Bailie, so there
was no vacancy before the co-option.

### James A. Daniel, 1962
- **ST Fri 10 Aug 1962, p6 (art. 090)**: Magnus Shearer, "top of the unsuccessful candidates in
  the May municipal poll", co-opted at Tuesday night's meeting (Tue 7 Aug 1962) to replace
  Daniel, "who recently submitted his resignation". The councillors were unanimous for co-option.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19620810&page=0006&article=090
- Daniel was still active on the Joint County Council committees in June (ST 15 Jun 1962, p2
  art. 024 and p7 art. 115) and was gymkhana treasurer in July (ST 6 Jul 1962, p4 art. 078).
- **Not found**: the resignation itself. Searched `daniel resignation` (11 May–17 Aug 1962),
  `daniel council` (11 May–3 Aug), `daniel` (29 Jun–3 Aug) and `resignation councillor`
  (1 Jun–10 Aug). Only the 10 Aug article mentions it.

**Ledger edit**: `james-daniel` 1960-05-03: `end_reason` replaced → resigned, with a source, but
left `confirmed=0`. The end date stays at the co-option (1962-08-07) because the resignation date
isn't known. It was probably between late June and early August; the July monthly meeting
(Tue 3 Jul 1962) report would be the place to look.

### James A. Daniel, 1962: second search (2026-09-28)
- **ST Fri 6 Jul 1962, p2 (art. 023)**: the Town Council report of the July meeting (Tue 3 Jul)
  covers the Planning Committee (the Post Office engineering centre site). The OCR has no mention
  of Daniel, a resignation or a vacancy.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19620706&page=0002&article=023
- **Not found**, again: `town council` (6 Jul–3 Aug 1962, 15 results; none about Daniel),
  `resignation` (1 Jun–10 Aug, 12 results) and `resigned` (29 Jun–10 Aug, 4 results). The only
  report is still ST 10 Aug 1962 ("recently submitted his resignation").

**Ledger edit**: none. The row stays at 1962-08-07, unconfirmed. Only the minute book is left.
