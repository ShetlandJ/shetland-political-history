## James J. Aitken (1895–1956), Provost 1946–50

Researched 2026-10-21. The wiki page had no biography. Bayanne not consulted.

- **ST Fri 3 Aug 1956, p4 (art. 058)**, "Death of former Provost": died "yesterday morning" (Thu
  2 Aug) in the Gilbert Bain Hospital; manager of the Pearl Assurance district office until
  ill-health forced him to retire more than a year before.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19560803&page=0004&article=058
- **ST Fri 10 Aug 1956, p6 (art. 075)**, obituary and funeral: of 18 St Olaf Street; only son of
  the late Mr and Mrs John Aitken, Dunrossness, born there 61 years ago; brought to Lerwick as an
  invalid when his father found work in town; part-time work with T. J. Anderson, his father's
  employer; a Pearl Assurance agent towards the end of the First War, district manager "from 1938
  until last November"; Shetland Football Association president; Boys' Brigade and Scouts; County
  Council Welfare Committee chairman and hospitals board; elected on a Labour candidature 1937,
  Provost 1946–50, housing committee chairman; "a total abstainer and a staunch Rechabite"; sister
  Miss Mary Aitken. Funeral on Saturday at St Olaf and St Ringan's Church; buried in Dunrossness
  Churchyard.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19560810&page=0006&article=075
- **ST Fri 10 Aug 1956, p6 (arts. 076–077)**, "Town Council tribute": Provost Conochie at a special
  meeting on Thursday (2 Aug), the morning he died; joined the council Nov 1937, Bailie March 1942,
  Provost Nov 1946 to May 1950, served to May 1953 when failing health kept him from re-standing;
  chairman of General Purposes and Housing; 178 houses built and occupied in his term, 80 more
  planned with the Scottish Special Housing Association; County Council Welfare Committee chairman
  (Viewforth purchase), Education Committee, Board of Management of Shetland Hospitals; president of
  the Shetland County Scout Council. Meeting adjourned half an hour.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19560810&page=0006&article=076

Agrees with the DB: born Dunrossness (61 in Aug 1956 fits 3 Apr 1895), died 2 Aug 1956, Lerwick.
"T. J. Anderson" isn't linked: none of the Thomas Andersons in `people` is clearly him.

## Magnus Shearer (i) (2026-11-08)

Prompted by his Find a Grave memorial (303430900), whose stone reads "Convenor of Zetland County
Council 1929-35".

- **Shetland Times, Sat 21 Dec 1929, p7 (art. 156)**, "The Re-Constituted County Council": first
  meeting "on Tuesday" (17 Dec). J. A. Smith moved Shearer, Vice-Convener of the old council, as
  chairman "to become and be called Convener" in May; ballot Shearer 17, Bailie W. Sinclair 15.
  Ruled "elected Chairman and Convener up till 15th May". So the stone's 1929 is the chairmanship;
  leadership_roles keeps 1930 (the Convenership, as the obituary has it). → biography (#104).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19291221&page=0007&article=156
- **Shetland Times, Fri 10 Mar 1961, p4 (art. 036)**, short notice: died "on Wednesday afternoon"
  (8 Mar, agrees with the DB). Funeral notice same page (art. 033): Lerwick New Cemetery, Sat 11 Mar.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19610310&page=0004&article=036
- **Shetland Times, Fri 17 Mar 1961, p7 (art. 086)**, full obituary (also p6 art. 075, a cut-off
  duplicate): died "at his home, Bayview, King Harald Street, Lerwick" → `death_place` (#104).
  Born at Clate, July 1890, second son of Robert Shearer and Catherine Hughson; cook on the
  'Reindeer', cooper under his father, then A. Wood & Sons at Lerwick and Shields; recalled from
  Shields in 1914; commissioned 1916, 9th Gordons; set up J. & M. Shearer with his uncle James;
  stations in Whalsay, Cullivoe, Lerwick and East Anglia; West Dock ice plant; County Council 1924,
  Education Authority; Whalsay seat given up in 1936, retaken 1947 "until his death"; convener
  1930–35, Provost 1941–46; Education Committee vice-chairman 1933–35, chairman to 1953; Territorial
  command 1938, Military Control Officer from 1941; TD, Lt-Col.; OBE 1937; JP 1949; Order of Vasa;
  chairman of the local Unionist Association; adopted for Orkney and Shetland "ten years ago";
  widow "formerly Miss Flora Stephen" (since 1922), son Magnus, daughters Mrs Noelle Gordon and Miss
  Margaret Shearer, brothers William, Thomas and Arthur. → intro and biography (#104).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19610317&page=0007&article=086

The marriage date (28 Dec 1922, Find a Grave) isn't in the paper; the biography gives the year only.
