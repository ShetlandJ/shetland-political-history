# LTC 1895–1901: retiring lists

Researched 2026-09-28 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times). Every link opens the
BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** in the toolbar for the OCR text.

Question: resolve the overlaps for Robert Hunter (i) (1896), Francis Halcrow (1897) and
George Kay (1900) (queue item "1896–1900").

## Evidence

### 1. October 1895: the four by rotation
- **Shetland Times, Sat 19 Oct 1895, p2 (art. 069)**: the Clerk said the retiring Councillors
  were "Messrs Hunter, Harrison, Halcrow, and Kay". Charles Robertson had died in July 1895, so
  the Nov 1895 general filled five seats: these four and his (1893 cohort, due out in 1896).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18951019&page=0002&article=069

### 2. October 1896: Hunter had Robertson's seat
- **Shetland Times, Sat 24 Oct 1896, p2 (art. 029)**: four retire in November: "Bailie Porteous,
  Messrs John Robertson, P. S. Goodlad, and R. B. Hunter". All four stood again.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18961024&page=0002&article=029
- So Hunter, not Stove, took the short seat in 1895 (the generator had it the other way round).
  Stove sat to 1898 (see 4).
- **Ledger**: `robert-hunter-i` 1895-11-05 ends 1896-11-03; `alfred-stove` 1895-11-05 ends
  1898-11-01.

### 3. October 1897: Halcrow out instead of Leisk
- **Shetland Times, Sat 16 Oct 1897, p4 (art. 091)**: retiring "by rotation": Bailie Goudie,
  Garriock, Halcrow and Laing. **Leisk**, the fourth of the 1894 cohort, is not on the list.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18971016&page=0004&article=091
- **Shetland Times, Sat 30 Oct 1897, p4 (art. 077)**: nominations: the four retiring councillors
  (Goudie, Garriock, Halcrow, ...) nominated again.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18971030&page=0004&article=077
- Neither says why. Leisk was Provost by 1898 (see 4), so it looks like the "Provost stays on"
  rule (1912, 1932, 1937), with Halcrow retiring a year early in his place. That reading is an
  inference; the ledger edit rests only on the two lists.
- **Ledger**: `francis-halcrow` 1895-11-05 ends 1897-11-02; `john-leisk` 1894-11-06 ends
  1898-11-01.

### 4. October 1898
- **Shetland Times, Sat 8 Oct 1898, p5 (art. 080)**: retiring at the next election: "Provost
  Leisk, Councillors Harrison, ..." (OCR breaks off).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18981008&page=0005&article=080
- **Shetland Times, Sat 29 Oct 1898, p4 (art. 055)**: "The four retiring Commissioners were
  Provost Leisk, Messrs Gilbert Harrison, G. G. Kay, and A. S. Stove."
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18981029&page=0004&article=055

### 5. October 1899
- **Shetland Times, Sat 28 Oct 1899, p4 (art. 101)**: retiring: Bailie Porteous, Goodlad,
  R. B. Hunter, John Robertson and S. Johnson (the May 1899 by-elected member, re-standing).
  Five seats: the 1896 four and Garriock's (died Feb 1899).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18991028&page=0004&article=101

### 6. October 1900: Kay volunteers
- **Shetland Times, Sat 13 Oct 1900, p8 (art. 132)**: council meeting. The Clerk said only three
  Commissioners retired in rotation, so one of the others had to be chosen "to complete the full
  third"; "Mr Kay volunteered to retire".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19001013&page=0008&article=132
- **Shetland Times, Sat 13 Oct 1900, p4 (art. 058)**: retiring: Bailie Goudie, Kay, Halcrow,
  Laing.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19001013&page=0004&article=058
- Sinclair Johnson is not in the 1900 third, so his Nov 1899 seat didn't end in 1900 (the draft
  had it ending there, in Garriock's 1897 cohort).
- **Ledger**: `george-kay` 1898-11-01 ends 1900-11-06.

### 7. October 1901: Johnson in the 1898 third
- **Shetland Times, Sat 5 Oct 1901, p5 (art. 063)**: the Clerk: the four retiring members are
  "the Provost and Councillors Harrison, Stove and Johnson".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19011005&page=0005&article=063
- **Shetland Times, Sat 5 Oct 1901, p4 (art. 046)**: same list: Provost Leisk, G. Harrison,
  A. S. Stove, S. Johnson.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19011005&page=0004&article=046
- So Johnson took the place in the 1898 third that Kay left. Stove died on 13 Oct 1901, after
  the list.
- **Ledger**: `sinclair-johnson` 1899-11-07 ends 1901-11-01.

## Result

Council at 12 throughout 1895–1901, apart from the gaps after the deaths of Charles Robertson
(Jul–Nov 1895), Garriock (Feb–May 1899) and Stove (Oct–Nov 1901), which are left as genuine
vacancies. term_issues 49 → 46.

Confirmed (both ends from an election and a retiring list): the 1892, 1893, 1894, 1895, 1896
and 1897 cohorts, and Kay, Harrison and Leisk from 1898, Johnson from 1899 (26 rows). Left
unconfirmed: the death-ended rows (Charles Robertson, Garriock, Stove: death dates not checked
here) and the 1899/1900 cohorts' later ends.
