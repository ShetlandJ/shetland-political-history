## Gulberwick, October 1936: Rev. George Smith co-opted Tue 20 Oct
- **Shetland News, Thu 22 Oct 1936, p5 (arts. 079, 088)**: the statutory meeting "on Tuesday"
  (20 Oct). "NEW MEMBER FOR GULBERWICK": a petition for Rev. George Smith, United Free Church,
  Lerwick, "in succession to Mr Thomas Johnston"; "unanimously co-opted".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0003210%2F19361022&page=0005&article=088
- The Shetland Times report of the same meeting (24 Oct 1936 p5 a107) doesn't mention it; he was
  absent on 17 Nov and welcomed as "the new member" at the statutory meeting of 22 Dec (ST 26 Dec
  1936 p5 a108).
- Johnston's resignation letter (ST 26 Sep 1936 p5 a076) made it "effective from to-day".
- **Edit**: election 633 dated 20 Oct 1936 (`fix_newspapers.py` #83; was 1 Oct).

## Unst South, 1936: the Order
- **ST Sat 11 Jan 1936, p4 (art. 066)**: new elections ordered for Unst South, where there was no
  nomination in Dec 1935. "Nominations have to be lodged by 21st January, intimations of
  withdrawals by 28th January, and, in the event of polls being necessary, voting will take place
  on 11th February."
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19360111&page=0004&article=066
- Manson withdrew and Clark was returned unopposed (ST 1 Feb 1936). Which day to use is back on
  the open questions page.

## Aithsting, Dec 1892: no contest
- **ST Sat 3 Dec 1892, p2 (art. 038)**: Grierson "was not aware of any intention" to nominate
  him for Aithsting, "and the [seat] will thus revert to Mr James MacCullie". Grierson sat for
  Sandness, where he was returned unopposed.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18921203&page=0002&article=038

## Balfour Spence, 1823
- **Sequestrated in August 1821**: "Balfour Spence, merchant in Lerwick" in the Scots
  sequestrations (BNA 0002370, 25 Aug 1821 p8 a045), creditors to meet in John's Coffee House,
  Edinburgh, 26 Sep 1821 (BNA 0002131, 1 Sep 1821 p4 a004).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0002370%2F18210825&page=0008&article=045
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0002131%2F18210901&page=0004&article=004
- Minute book (`~/projects/town_council_minutes`): present 1820–21 (p22–25, last on 1 Oct 1821),
  absent 12 Aug 1822 and 2 Sep 1823, and never present from the 1823 election to 1826; on the
  burgess rolls of 1823 (p28) and 1826 (p34) only. The 1823 "Councillor Spence" is William.
- No list of the 1823 council was found in the BNA (search "balfour spence", all titles,
  1815–1845: 8 results, none 1822–1826).
