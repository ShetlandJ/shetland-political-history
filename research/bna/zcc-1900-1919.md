# ZCC 1900–1919: citations for the generals and by-elections

Researched 2026-09-28 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times). Every link opens the
BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** for the OCR text. The page images don't render,
so vote figures lost in the OCR aren't checked.

Question: find a source for each Zetland County Council election 1900–1919 (citations batch 2,
second run), and check polling days and votes against the DB.

Findings:
- **Dec 1901 general**: polled Tue 3 Dec 1901, as in the DB. Nominations agree with the DB's
  winners; no nomination in Unst South, Fetlar or Yell South (hence the Feb 1902 by-elections).
  Holborn of Foula was nominated for Tingwall and withdrew. Most figures are lost in the OCR;
  the two that survive (Northmavine North majority 35; Nesting within two) agree.
- **Feb 1902 (Unst South, Fetlar, Yell South)**: winners agree, no poll. The day the Secretary
  for Scotland fixed (in January) is lost in the OCR; they were reported elected at the Council
  meeting of Thu 6 Feb, so the DB's 8 Feb (and Fetlar's 1 Feb) can't both be right. Left alone;
  in open-questions.
- **Sandness Jul 1902**: Holbourn was the new member by 2 Aug; the appointment meeting wasn't
  found, and the wiki's Thu 24 Jul fits. Not changed.
- **Delting South Apr 1903**: J. A. Adie appointed Thu 16 Apr, not 1 Apr; **Northmavine North
  Sep 1903**: Haldane appointed Thu 17 Sep, not Sat 19th (`fix_newspapers.py` #32).
- **Dec 1904 general**: polled Tue 6 Dec 1904, as in the DB, in four divisions; the new-council
  list agrees with the DB's winners. **Delting North's loser was James Inkster** (the sitting
  member), not Arthur White, who was returned unopposed for Northmavine South; the wiki table
  has White 13 (`fix_newspapers.py` #33; votes not checked, OCR lost them). J. P. Henderson was
  nominated for Lerwick Central and withdrew.
- **By-elections 1905–1907**: Walls Feb 1905 was fixed for Tue 14 Feb (DB 1 Feb, wiki 21 Feb);
  Yell North was Thu 15 Feb 1906, not March; Dunrossness South Thu 15 Aug 1907, not the 1st
  (`fix_newspapers.py` #32). Dunrossness North Jun 1907: Budge appointed at a June meeting whose
  day is lost in the OCR (the wiki's "Thursday 16th June" was a Sunday); left at 1 Jun, in
  open-questions.
- **Dec 1907 general**: polled Tue 3 Dec 1907, as in the DB, in five divisions; winners agree.
  Walls: Thomason 69 of 104 votes, as in the DB. **Sandwick was unopposed**: the wiki's 1907
  Sandwick table (276, 161, Smith 91, Thomson 70) is a copy of 1904's (`fix_newspapers.py` #34).
  George Laurence withdrew in Yell South, as the DB says. (The DB also has him withdrawing in
  1904, from the wiki, but the paper's 1904 "complete list" has only Williamson: possibly another
  copy. Left alone, since he has no votes and no seat either way.)
- **By-elections 1909–1910**: all at Council meetings on a petition. Tingwall Thu 18 Mar 1909,
  Burra Thu 12 Aug 1909 (the DB had Lennie's death, 27 Jun), Whalsay Thu 21 Jul 1910: the days
  the wiki text gives, which the parser dropped (`fix_newspapers.py` #32).
- **Dec 1910 general**: polled **Tue 6 Dec 1910**, not Sat 3rd (`fix_newspapers.py` #35), in
  four divisions (Nesting, Sandsting, Gulberwick, Lerwick North), as in the DB. Nominations agree
  with the DB's winners; Bowie (Aithsting), Small (Sandsting) and Williamson (Yell South)
  withdrew. The result figures weren't legible (the page OCR mixes columns), so the votes aren't
  checked. Dunrossness South, Sandwick and Unst North aren't in the nominations list read (the
  first lines of the list weren't read), which fits the Jan 1911 [unfilled seat] by-elections.
- **Jan 1911 (Dunrossness South, Sandwick, Unst North)**: the only nominations, handed in Tue
  10 Jan, so no contests; winners agree. The election day isn't given; the wiki's Thu 12 Jan is
  kept (open question).
- **Dec 1913 general**: polled Tue 2 Dec 1913, as in the DB ("Tuesday first", ST 29 Nov; the
  22 Nov preview's "Tuesday, 6th December" is a slip). Five contests, as in the DB; the votes
  that survive agree (Bressay 48–26, Burra 36–26, Aithsting 31–31, Walls Thomason 53). The
  Aithsting tie went to Grierson on the Returning Officer's casting vote, on the Secretary for
  Scotland's instruction. Mouat (Tingwall) and McDougall (Delting South) were nominated and
  withdrew. The previews put the Grierson–Bowie contest in "Sandsting", but the results say
  Aithsting, as the DB does.
- **By-elections 1914–1919**: all Council appointments. Dates: Whiteness Thu 18 Jun 1914,
  Nesting Thu 24 Dec 1914 (wiki 17 Dec), Aithsting Thu 19 Aug 1915 (wiki "Thursday 15 August",
  a Sunday), Fetlar Thu 19 Jul 1917, Cunningsburgh Thu 20 Feb 1919 (wiki "Thursday 23
  February", a Sunday), Dunrossness South and Tingwall Thu 19 Jun 1919 (`fix_newspapers.py`
  #32). The DB showed petition signatures and Council ballots as votes (Whiteness 1914
  "Henderson 22, Sinclair 67" are petitions; the Council voted 10–7): now text
  (`fix_parse_errors.py` #12). The ballots agree with the paper (16–3, 12–4, 5–3; petition of 29).
  About 18 later ZCC by-election pages have the same kind of table (queued).
- **Dec 1919 general**: polled Tue 2 Dec 1919, as in the DB, in eight divisions; the votes
  agree (the five country results exactly; the Lerwick and Burra totals, with spoiled papers,
  fit the DB's figures). Two wards were wrong: **Lerwick South was James Laing**, unopposed after
  C. B. Stout withdrew (the DB and wiki had Stout; Laing sat at the January 1920 meeting), and
  **Delting North was unopposed** (Peterson withdrew; the wiki's "Hay 29, Peterson 15" echoes
  1913's Hay 29 and Henderson 15). `fix_newspapers.py` #36 (with Laing's intro: now "between
  1919 and 1929"), `fix_parse_errors.py` #13.

  Tuesday" (3 Dec), 8 a.m. to 8 p.m.
