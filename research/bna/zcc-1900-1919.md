# ZCC 1900–1919: citations for the generals and by-elections

Researched 2026-09-28 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times). Every link opens the
BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** for the OCR text. The page images don't render,
so vote figures lost in the OCR aren't checked.

Question: find a source for each Zetland County Council election 1900–1919 (citations batch 2,
second run), and check polling days and votes against the DB.

Findings:
- **Dec 1901 general**: polled Tue 3 Dec 1901, as in the DB. Nominations agree with the DB's
  winners; no nomination in Unst South, Fetlar or Yell South (hence the Feb 1902 by-elections).
  Holborn of Foula was nominated for Tingwall and withdrew. Most figures are lost in the OCR;
  the two that survive (Northmavine North majority 35; Nesting within two) agree.
- **Feb 1902 (Unst South, Fetlar, Yell South)**: winners agree, no poll. The day the Secretary
  for Scotland fixed (in January) is lost in the OCR; they were reported elected at the Council
  meeting of Thu 6 Feb, so the DB's 8 Feb (and Fetlar's 1 Feb) can't both be right. Left alone;
  in open-questions.
- **Sandness Jul 1902**: Holbourn was the new member by 2 Aug; the appointment meeting wasn't
  found, and the wiki's Thu 24 Jul fits. Not changed.
- **Delting South Apr 1903**: J. A. Adie appointed Thu 16 Apr, not 1 Apr; **Northmavine North
  Sep 1903**: Haldane appointed Thu 17 Sep, not Sat 19th (`fix_newspapers.py` #32).
- **Dec 1904 general**: polled Tue 6 Dec 1904, as in the DB, in four divisions; the new-council
  list agrees with the DB's winners. **Delting North's loser was James Inkster** (the sitting
  member), not Arthur White, who was returned unopposed for Northmavine South; the wiki table
  has White 13 (`fix_newspapers.py` #33; votes not checked, OCR lost them). J. P. Henderson was
  nominated for Lerwick Central and withdrew.
- **By-elections 1905–1907**: Walls Feb 1905 was fixed for Tue 14 Feb (DB 1 Feb, wiki 21 Feb);
  Yell North was Thu 15 Feb 1906, not March; Dunrossness South Thu 15 Aug 1907, not the 1st
  (`fix_newspapers.py` #32). Dunrossness North Jun 1907: Budge appointed at a June meeting whose
  day is lost in the OCR (the wiki's "Thursday 16th June" was a Sunday); left at 1 Jun, in
  open-questions.
- **Dec 1907 general**: polled Tue 3 Dec 1907, as in the DB, in five divisions; winners agree.
  Walls: Thomason 69 of 104 votes, as in the DB. **Sandwick was unopposed**: the wiki's 1907
  Sandwick table (276, 161, Smith 91, Thomson 70) is a copy of 1904's (`fix_newspapers.py` #34).
  George Laurence withdrew in Yell South, as the DB says. (The DB also has him withdrawing in
  1904, from the wiki, but the paper's 1904 "complete list" has only Williamson: possibly another
  copy. Left alone, since he has no votes and no seat either way.)
- **By-elections 1909–1910**: all at Council meetings on a petition. Tingwall Thu 18 Mar 1909,
  Burra Thu 12 Aug 1909 (the DB had Lennie's death, 27 Jun), Whalsay Thu 21 Jul 1910: the days
  the wiki text gives, which the parser dropped (`fix_newspapers.py` #32).
- **Dec 1910 general**: polled **Tue 6 Dec 1910**, not Sat 3rd (`fix_newspapers.py` #35), in
  four divisions (Nesting, Sandsting, Gulberwick, Lerwick North), as in the DB. Nominations agree
  with the DB's winners; Bowie (Aithsting), Small (Sandsting) and Williamson (Yell South)
  withdrew. The result figures weren't legible (the page OCR mixes columns), so the votes aren't
  checked. Dunrossness South, Sandwick and Unst North aren't in the nominations list read (the
  first lines of the list weren't read), which fits the Jan 1911 [unfilled seat] by-elections.

## Evidence

### 1. County Council Election December 1901 (elections 275–301)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 23 Nov 1901, p4 (art. 063)**: "NOMINATION OF CANDIDATES", closed
  Tuesday (19 Nov). One nomination for each division except Tingwall (W. R. Duncan, George
  Leslie jun., J. M. S. Holborn), Nesting, Lerwick North and both Northmavines. Unst South,
  Fetlar and Yell South "No Nomination". Every name agrees with the DB.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19011123&page=0004&article=063
- **Shetland Times, Sat 30 Nov 1901, p4 (art. 058)**: polling "on Tuesday first"; "The only
  withdrawal" is Holborn's, so Tingwall is Duncan v Leslie.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19011130&page=0004&article=058
- **Shetland Times, Sat 7 Dec 1901, p4 (art. 064)**: the contested divisions "took place on
  Tuesday" (3 Dec), 8 a.m. to 8 p.m.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19011207&page=0004&article=064
- **Shetland Times, Sat 7 Dec 1901, p4 (art. 071)**: "THE RESULT": Tulloch, Duncan, Robertson
  (majority 35), Anderson and Small elected. In Nesting two spoiled papers, correctly marked,
  "there would have been an equality" (DB Small 35, Johnson 33). Other figures lost.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19011207&page=0004&article=071

### 2. By-elections 1902–1903 (elections 302–307)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 4 Jan 1902, p5 (art. 075)**: "NEW ELECTIONS": an Order by the Secretary
  for Scotland "fixing [day lost] instant" for Unst South, Fetlar and Yell South.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19020104&page=0005&article=075
- **Shetland Times, Sat 18 Jan 1902, p4 (art. 066)**: nominations Sandison (Unst South), Sir

- **Shetland Times, Sat 8 Feb 1902, p8 (art. 155)**: monthly meeting "Thursday at noon" (6 Feb):
  the Clerk reported the three elected. Williamson was present.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19020208&page=0008&article=155
- **Shetland Times, Sat 9 Aug 1902, p4 (art. 062)**: Holborn of Foula, "the new representative
  in the County Council" of the northern ward of Walls, visited Papa Stour on Sat 2 Aug.
  Bankart's death: ST 17 May 1902 (p4 art. 082).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19020809&page=0004&article=062
- **Shetland Times, Sat 21 Feb 1903, p4 (art. 053)**: "RESIGNATION OF A COUNCILLOR": W. J. Adie,

  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19030221&page=0004&article=053
- **Shetland Times, Sat 18 Apr 1903, p5 (art. 072)**: meeting "Thursday at noon" (16 Apr): James
  A. Adie appointed for South Delting "in place of Mr W. J. Adie". → #32.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19030418&page=0005&article=072
- **Shetland Times, Sat 19 Sep 1903, p5 (art. 112)**: adjourned general meeting "on Thursday at
  noon" (17 Sep): Ollaberry petition dated 4 Sep; Haldane appointed unanimously. → #32.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19030919&page=0005&article=112

### 3. County Council Election December 1904 (elections 308–334)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 26 Nov 1904, p4 (art. 041)**: "County Council Nominations", a complete
  list. Delting North: "Mr James Inkster, merchant, Brae, and Mr Wm. Pole, merchant, Mossbank".
  Northmavine South: Arthur White alone. Lerwick Central: Jamieson and J. Percy Henderson.
  Walls: no nomination. Yell South: Williamson alone.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19041126&page=0004&article=041
- **Shetland Times, Sat 10 Dec 1904, p4 (art. 104)**: "Polling ... took place Tuesday last"
  (6 Dec); counted by Thursday. Results: Haldane v Loggie, "Pole J. Inkster", P. Anderson v
  H. J. Robertson, W. Smith v J. Thomson (figures lost). "THE NEW COUNTY COUNCIL" lists every
  division; all agree with the DB. → #33.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19041210&page=0004&article=104

### 7. County Council Election December 1910 (elections 369–395)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 19 Nov 1910, p4 (arts. 063, 067)**: the official list of nominations,
  made known on Wednesday; by division from Fetlar to Dunrossness North. Contests possible in
  Yell South (Hoggan, Williamson), Nesting (Loggie, McDougall, Small), Sandsting (Robertson,
  Small, Bowie, Scott), Aithsting (Grierson, Bowie), Gulberwick (Shand, Fordyce), Lerwick North

  Nesting, Sandsting, Gulberwick and Lerwick North; "election takes place on Tuesday, [6th]
