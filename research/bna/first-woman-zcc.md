# First woman on the Zetland County Council (2026-10-03)

The question: was Selina Garriock (Tingwall, Dec 1935) the first woman on the ZCC, or did a woman sit for a five-member "Lerwick Landward" ward in 1932 (James remembered Rose Sutherland)?

**Answer.** The five-member body in 1932 was the **Lerwick (Landward) District Council**, not the County Council, and the woman on it was **Mrs Elizabeth Tait, Gremista, Quarff**. Rose Sutherland wasn't on it: she stood for **Lerwick Town Council** in Nov 1933 and lost. The first woman to sit on the County Council was **Charlotte Nicol**, from Nov 1933, as a Lerwick Town Councillor (burgh member). **Selina Garriock** (Dec 1935) was the first woman **elected** to it for a county division. The site intros already say this.

## Evidence

- **ST Sat 19 Nov 1932, p4 (article 029)**: "Lerwick (Landward) District Council". Five nominations for five seats, so no poll: Robert A. Johnson, Rose Villa, South Road; J. G. Williamson, Lower Langton, Gulberwick; John Taylor, Hamnavoe, Burra Isle; Boyd H. Morgan, Schoolhouse, Burra Isle; "and Mrs Elizabeth Tait, Gremista, Quarff".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19321119&page=0004&article=029
  The district councils were the 1929 Act's successors to the parish councils (ST 25 Jan 1930, p5, "District Councils—successors to Parish Councils"). The 1932 district elections were a separate set of nominations from the County Council election held on the same day (ST 26 Nov 1932, p4, art. 021, "District Council Elections").
- **ST Sat 21 Oct 1933, p4 (article 053)**: the Lerwick branch of the British Women's Temperance Association put forward its president, Mrs Sutherland, Lystina House, "the first lady to seek municipal honours in Lerwick".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19331021&page=0004&article=053
- **ST Sat 4 Nov 1933, p4 (articles 083, 084)**: eight candidates for four Town Council seats, among them the Ratepayers' Association nominees "Mrs C. Nicol, Mrs Rose Sutherland, Captain A. Halcrow and Mr Charles A. Manson". Nomination: "Mrs ROSE SUTHERLAND, Lystina House". Poll Tue 7 Nov.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19331104&page=0004&article=084
- **ST Sat 11 Nov 1933, p4 (article 065)**: Nicol was "the first lady elected to Lerwick Town Council. She will also be the first lady member of the Zetland County Council". The defeated Town Councillors had sat on the County Council "in virtue of being Town Councillors". Rose Sutherland is not among the winners.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19331111&page=0004&article=065
- **ST Sat 25 Nov 1933, p5 (article 086)**: at the County Council meeting (Clerk J. N. Sinclair), "the Convener … extend a cordial welcome to the new members — Mrs Nicol, Captain Halcrow and Mr Charles Manson".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19331125&page=0005&article=086
- **ST Sat 14 Dec 1935, p5 (article 099)**: Scalloway Notes. Mrs L. J. Garriock, candidate for Tingwall, held an election meeting; "the news of Mrs Garriock's success in the election" followed. (Her thanks to the electors of Tingwall are on p1, art. 007.)
