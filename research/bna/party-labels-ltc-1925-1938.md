# Party labels: Lerwick Town Council, 1925–1938

Researched 2026-09-30 (queue: Labels 3). Same method as `party-labels-ltc-1890-1913.md`. BNA link
pattern: `https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2FYYYYMMDD&page=NNNN&article=NNN`.

From 1925 the contests were fought by named sides: Labour against "Constitutional" (1925–29),
Labour and others against the Lerwick Ratepayers' Association (1932–37), and Labour against
Independents (1938). The paper names each side's candidates, so almost every existing label is
now confirmed from a named source.

## 1925 (election 83)

- ST 24 Oct 1925 p4 a073 (preview): the three retiring Labour members (M. L. Manson, A. S. Manson,
  Morrison) and Duffin re-standing; Labour's new candidate Sandison; Campbell, Cogle and Inkster
  the new candidates trying "to oust the three Labour candidates".
- ST 31 Oct 1925 p1 a193: meeting notice, "SPEAKERS—THE LABOUR CANDIDATES: Alex. S. Manson,
  Magnus L. Manson, James Morrison, Laurence Sandison".
- ST 7 Nov 1925 p4 a080 "Labour Loses Two Seats": "four of the candidates standing as
  'Constitutional' and four as Labour"; the ballot paper grouped by party.
- **Labels:** Labour for the four (Sandison new; the other three confirmed); **Constitutional**
  for Campbell, Duffin, Cogle and George Inkster (all new).

## 1926 (election 84)

- ST 6 Nov 1926 p4 a075: Ganson, J. W. Irvine, John Manson and Ratter "sought election as the
  'Constitutional' or 'Moderate' party representatives"; Gray, Murray, Sandison and Spence "the
  representatives of Labour".
- **Labels confirmed:** the DB's "Constitutional/Moderate" and Labour. (Open question: the same
  side is "Constitutional" in 1925 and 1929.)

## 1927, 1928, 1930, 1931: unopposed, no labels

1927 was left uncontested by agreement: a letter for the Lerwick Labour Party (ST 22 Oct 1927 p4
a061, and 5 Nov p5 a080) argues over it, but no candidate is labelled in the nominations or
result.

## 1929 (election 87)

- ST 2 Nov 1929 p1 a205: address asking support for "the Labour Candidates, Alexander S. Manson,
  Laurence Sandison, and William Sinclair".
- ST 9 Nov 1929 p4 a128 "Three Labour Candidates Top the Poll": Ganson, Irvine and Ratter "stood
  together as 'Constitutional' candidates and issued a circular soliciting block votes".
- **Labels confirmed:** Labour three, Constitutional three.

## 1932 (election 91)

- ST 29 Oct 1932 p5 a039 and 5 Nov p4 a025: nine candidates, "the four retiring Councillors"
  (Bruce, A. S. Manson, Morrison, Sandison), "the nominees of the Ratepayers' Association"
  (Conochie, R. J. H. Ganson, Henderson, William Robertson) and Thomas Johnston, "who stood as an
  independent candidate".
- ST 29 Oct 1932 p4 a026: the four retiring councillors' joint address, with a specimen ballot. No
  party name.
- **Labels confirmed:** Ratepayers Association four, Johnston Independent. The DB's "Socialist"
  for the four retiring councillors isn't in the paper (open question).

## 1933 (election 92)

- ST 28 Oct 1933 p4 a073–a079: the Ratepayers' Association's first annual meeting chose its four
  candidates. ST 4 Nov 1933 p4 a083: the four retiring councillors (W. Sinclair, Ollason, J.
  Laing, J. T. J. Sinclair) and "the four nominees of the Ratepayers' Association, Mrs C. Nicol,
  Mrs Rose Sutherland, Captain A. Halcrow and Mr Charles A. Manson".
- **Labels confirmed:** the four Ratepayers. The retiring four had no label.

## 1934 (election 93)

- ST 3 Nov 1934 p4 a044: "the five candidates put forward by the Ratepayers' Association—Messrs
  Peter Dalziel, L. Laurenson, J. W. Robertson, Jas. A. Smith and A. I. Tulloch"; Cogle, Duffin,
  M. L. Manson and Blance unlabelled. ST 10 Nov p4 a066: "Sweeping Victory for Ratepayers'
  Association".
- **Labels confirmed:** the five.

## 1935 (election 94)

- ST 9 Nov 1935 p4 a059: of the two new candidates, T. A. Sinclair and John Sutherland, "the
  latter as a Ratepayer's Association nominee". No other labels in the nominations (2 Nov p4 a078)
  or result.
- **Label confirmed:** Sutherland. The DB's labels for Ganson, Duffin and Nicol (Ratepayers) and
  T. A. Sinclair (Labour) aren't in this year's reports: neither confirmed nor contradicted.

## 1936 (election 97): unopposed

Nominations (ST 31 Oct 1936 p4 a074) unlabelled. The DB's Halcrow (Ratepayers) and T. A. Sinclair
(Labour) are unconfirmed.

## 1937 (election 99)

- ST 30 Oct 1937 p4 a099: "Five retiring Councillors are seeking re-election as a combined group"
  (Nicol, Clausen, Dalziel, Laurenson, J. W. Robertson); six "put forward as representatives of
  the Transport and General Workers' Union and the local Labour party" (Laing, Aitken, Anderson,
  Linklater, W. G. Smith, Williamson); "one independent candidate, Mr Andrew M. Mouat".
- ST 30 Oct 1937 p4 a103: the five's joint address: "Four of us are adherents of the Ratepayers'
  Association, one is not a member."
- ST 6 Nov 1937 p4 a066 "Ratepayers' Association Candidates Defeated. Five Seats Won by Labour
  Party": the defeated included "four of the nominees originally sponsored by the Ratepayers'
  Association". The four defeated of the group were Dalziel, Nicol, Laurenson and Robertson, so
  Clausen (elected) was the non-member.
- **Labels:** Labour six confirmed; Ratepayers four confirmed; **Independent** for Mouat (new).

## 1938 (election 101)

- ST 29 Oct 1938 p4 a083: "Messrs Ganson, Irvine, Shearer and Mouat are standing as independent
  candidates, while Messrs Dalziel, Garriock, Johnson, Sinclair and Williamson are standing as
  Labour party candidates". p4 a084: Mouat's address, "I am standing as an Independent
  Candidate", opposed to "any Political Parties on the Council".
- **Labels:** Labour five confirmed (Dalziel, a Ratepayers' nominee in 1934 and 1937, now Labour);
  **Independent** for Ganson, Irvine, Shearer and Mouat (new).

## Council co-options 1932–38

Morrison 1932, Sinclair and Irvine 1936, Clausen 1936, Dalziel and Williamson 1938 were
appointments by the Council, not contests. The DB labels on two (Sinclair 1936, Williamson 1938,
both Labour) come from the wiki and weren't checked.
