# ZCC 1890–1899: citations for the generals and by-elections

Researched 2026-09-28 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times). Every link opens the
BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** for the OCR text.

Question: find a source for each Zetland County Council election 1890–1899 (citations batch 2,
first run), and check polling days and votes against the DB.

Findings:
- **Feb 1890 general**: polled Tue 4 Feb 1890, as in the DB; every vote figure agrees. Two
  electorates differ from the DB (below); the page image wouldn't render, so not changed.
- **Dec 1892 general**: polled Tue 6 Dec 1892, as in the DB; the votes agree. Aithsting was
  contested (Grierson and McCullie nominated; McCullie "had practically a walk over"), but the
  DB shows McCullie alone with no votes. No figures printed, so nothing changed.
- **Dec 1895 general**: polled Tue 3 Dec 1895, as in the DB; Tingwall (the only contest) agrees.
  Every other nomination matches the DB. Aithsting's only nomination fell through, hence the
  Jan 1896 by-election.
- **Dec 1898 general**: polled Tue 6 Dec 1898, as in the DB, in three divisions (Sandsting,
  Burra, Nesting); the new-council list agrees with the DB's winners. The vote figures are lost in
  the OCR, so the DB's aren't checked. Dunrossness North was nominated as a contest (Rev. Charles
  Whyte, the sitting member, v Isbister) but wasn't polled; the DB has Isbister alone, which fits.

## Evidence

### 1. County Council Election February 1890 (elections 150–176)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 8 Feb 1890, p3 (art. 061)**: the full list of 27 divisions, with
  electors (men and women), numbers polled and votes. Fetlar, Walls North and Walls South had no
  nomination (hence the [unfilled seat] by-elections of March 1890). Delting North: Hoseason 9,
  Pole 5. Sandwick: Duncan 106, Bruce 16. Northmavine East: Hay 43, Laurenson 38, Sinclair 32,
  Hawick 5. All agree with the DB.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18900208&page=0003&article=061
- **Shetland Times, Sat 8 Feb 1890, p3 (art. 062)**: "The polling for Eastern Division of this
  parish took place" at Ollaberry on Tuesday (4 Feb). The Dunrossness count was declared on
  Wednesday, the rest on Friday 7 Feb (same page, first article; and the leader, p2 art. 054).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18900208&page=0003&article=062
- **Shetland Times, Sat 1 Feb 1890, p3 (art. 069)**: preview. In North Delting "Mr Hoseason
  intimates that he is ineligible, and Pole will be duly returned". He was returned anyway (9 to
  5), and his seat went to the May 1890 by-election.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18900201&page=0003&article=069
- Electorates that differ from the DB (OCR; image not checked): Whiteness and Weisdale "M. 86,
  F. 30—Total 116" (DB 126; the paper's figures add up to 116); Nesting and Lunnasting "Total 93"
  (DB 83; the men's figure is lost). Walls North OCR "63", but M. 50 + F. 18 = 68, as in the DB.

### 2. County Council Election December 1892 (elections 184–210)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 26 Nov 1892, p3 (art. 045)**: "The Nominations". At the close on
  Tuesday (22 Nov), nineteen seats were unopposed, five had two candidates, and three had none
  (Burra, where Rev. D. Gray stood down; Walls, where Georgeson stood down; and Fetlar), hence
  the Feb 1893 by-elections. Aithsting: James Anderson retired; J. C. Grierson and J. McCullie
  nominated, and Grierson was also returned unopposed for Sandness.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18921126&page=0003&article=045
- **Shetland Times, Sat 10 Dec 1892, p2 (art. 038)**: "The polling for the contested divisions
  took place on Tuesday" (6 Dec). Unst North Sandison 71, Edmondston 28; Delting North Inkster
  17, Hanton 2; Sandwick Duncan, Tulloch 21 (Duncan's figure lost in the OCR); Tingwall Hay 80,
  Downes 79. In Aithsting McCullie "had practically a walk over". All agree with the DB.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18921210&page=0002&article=038

### 3. County Council Election December 1895 (elections 214–240)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 23 Nov 1895, p3 (art. 056)**: the nominations, one per division except
  Tingwall (A. J. Hay and W. P. Harcus). Sinclair Johnson had been nominated for Aithsting but
  wasn't on the roll, so "his nomination falls through, and a new election will have to be
  ordered". Sandness is "Mr H. F. Anderson" in the OCR (DB Herbert Anderton; probably OCR).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18951123&page=0003&article=056
- **Shetland Times, Sat 7 Dec 1895, p2 (art. 042)**: the Tingwall election "took place on
  Tuesday" (3 Dec); counted the next day: Hay 95, Harcus 84. Agrees with the DB.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18951207&page=0002&article=042

### 4. County Council Election December 1898 (elections 247–273)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 26 Nov 1898, p4 (art. 056)**: "Nomination of Candidates", with new
  candidates starred. Contests: Sandsting (A. J. Garriock, the sitting member, against Alex.
  Inkster, W. P. Harcus and Alex. Mitchell jun.), Nesting (Small v Hunter), Burra and Quarff
  (Henry Henderson, "the sitting member", v Charles Lennie) and Dunrossness North (Rev. Charles
  Whyte v R. H. Isbister). "In South Delting no nomination has been made" (hence Feb 1899).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18981126&page=0004&article=056
- **Shetland Times, Sat 10 Dec 1898, p4 (art. 045, with 047)**: "The voting for County
  Councillors for Sandsting, Burra Isle, and Nesting took place on Tuesday" (6 Dec). Mitchell
  well ahead of Garriock; Lennie beat Henderson; Hunter had told Nesting he wouldn't stand. Then
  "The New County Council", a complete list, which matches the DB's winners (Isbister for
  Dunrossness North). The figures are lost in the OCR.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18981210&page=0004&article=045
