# ZCC 1920–1939: citations for the generals and by-elections

Researched 2026-09-29 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times). Every link opens the
BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** for the OCR text.

Question: find a source for each Zetland County Council election 1920–1939 (citations batch 2,
third run), and check polling days and votes against the DB.

Findings:
- **Dec 1922 general**: polled Tue 5 Dec 1922, as in the DB, in eight divisions. The votes agree
  in the seven the DB has as contests. Two wards were wrong:
  - **Dunrossness North was a contest**: J. R. White 60, A. Irvine jun. 36 (the wiki has White
    unopposed). `fix_newspapers.py` #37.
  - **Aithsting and Sandsting were not combined.** The wiki says the election "recombined" them,
    and `fix_parse_errors.py` #8 had hidden the Sandsting row as a duplicate. But the nominations
    list the two wards separately, with R. A. Sutherland alone for Sandsting, and he sat at the
    new Council's first meeting. #8 now keeps both rows: Aithsting Leslie 110, Clark 89;
    Sandsting Sutherland unopposed, so his seat runs 1922–25 (his intro said 1919–22). The result
    report does call the Leslie–Clark contest "Sandsting and Aithsting", which is presumably where
    the wiki's note came from.
- **Dec 1925 general**: polled **Tue 1 Dec 1925**, not Sat 5th (`fix_newspapers.py` #38). The
  nominations and the candidates' adverts give "Tuesday, 1st December"; the results report's
  "took place on Monday" is taken as a slip. Seventeen unopposed and ten contests, all as in the
  DB, and the votes agree. Two not checked: Sandsting's third candidate (OCR 13, DB 18; the page
  image doesn't render) and Aithsting (Leslie v C. F. Irvine), whose result isn't in the OCR.
- **Dec 1928 general**: polled **Tue 4 Dec 1928**, not Wed 5th (`fix_newspapers.py` #39; the
  wiki's "Tuesday 5 December" has the wrong date). Three contests (Aithsting, Sandness, Walls), as
  in the DB, after three withdrawals; the votes agree. **Walls electorate 389**, not 192, which is
  Sandness's (#40).
- **Dec 1929 general** (the re-constituted Council under the 1929 Act, no Lerwick wards): polled
  Tue 3 Dec 1929, as in the DB. Twelve contests and twelve unopposed, all as in the DB; every
  vote agrees. Sandness was a tie, 45–45: the Returning Officer's casting vote went to Ganson, the
  sitting member ("the first occasion" it was needed in a County Council election). The new
  Council took full office only after 15 May 1930, and "The present County Council will continue
  to function till May". The DB ends the 1928 terms at the Dec 1929 poll (open question).
- **Dec 1932 general**: polled Tue 6 Dec 1932, as in the DB. Three contests (Sandsting, Yell
  North, Yell South); votes and electorates agree. **Two by-elections were missing** from the wiki
  and the DB (`fix_newspapers.py` #41): no nomination was lodged for Sandwick, and James A.
  Jamieson, returned unopposed for Sandness, couldn't sit because he held a minor appointment
  under the Council. New elections were fixed for Tue 14 Feb 1933, and both went unopposed:
  William Jamieson (the former member) for Sandwick and James A. Jamieson again for Sandness. His
  1932 win is in `data/not_seated.csv`. The wiki's Sandwick page says the ward "was incorporated
  into Cunningsburgh and Dunrossness North" in 1932, which the paper contradicts. Sandwick had
  shown as empty 1932–35.
- **Dec 1935 general**: polled Tue 3 Dec 1935, as in the DB. Five contests (Tingwall,
  Northmavine North, Nesting, Walls, Sandwick) and seventeen unopposed, as in the DB (Burra's
  second nomination, John Sutherland, must have withdrawn); no nomination in Unst South. The
  votes agree except **Walls: Halcrow had 4, not 85** (the wiki took the majority as his vote,
  `fix_newspapers.py` #42). William Jamieson was the sitting member for Sandwick and retired, so
  he sat 1933–35 (his intro now says 1919–1935, #43).
- **Dec 1938 general**: polled Tue 6 Dec 1938, as in the DB (a winner's thank-you: "the Poll on
  Tuesday, December 6th"; the 26 Nov preview's "Tuesday first" must mean the 6th, since
  candidates were still advertising on 3 Dec). Six contests, not five: **Yell South** was Manson
  166, Leask 145 (the wiki has Manson unopposed, and puts Yell South's electorate and turnout
  under Yell North, which had 257 electors and 158 votes), `fix_newspapers.py` #45. **Whalsay:
  Hay had 32, not 121** (the majority again, #44). The other votes agree.
- **By-elections 1920–1938**: all appointments by the Council at a meeting, usually on a
  petition; none was a poll except Unst South 1936 (below). The Council met on **Thursdays** up to
  1929 and on **Tuesdays** from the re-constituted Council of May 1930. Nineteen dates moved
  (`fix_newspapers.py` #46; two more from the wiki text, `fix_parse_errors.py` #15), mostly from
  the 1st of the month to the meeting day. Wrong days in the wiki: Lerwick North 1920 was Thu 19
  Feb (not 18 Mar, when Mouat thanked the Council); Sandness and Walls 1921 were Thu 15 Sep (not
  20 Oct, when their acceptance was announced); Cunningsburgh 1927 Thu 17 Feb ("Thursday 15
  February" was a Tuesday); Northmavine South 1927 Thu 21 Jul (not Tue 19th); Sandsting 1930 Tue
  16 Sep (not "Thursday 18"). Petition counts and Council ballots had been shown as votes in ten
  of them (`fix_parse_errors.py` #14, from the wiki text). **Aithsting 1932**: the ballots were
  10–9–3, then 10–9 (Shetland News; the wiki has an extra 8–8–3 round), #48. **Unst South 1936**
  was an election: Manson withdrew and Clark was returned unopposed (the wiki: "Unanimously
  appointed", "Rejected"), #47.
- Not settled (open-questions): Walls May 1930 (garbled; a second petition, for William Hales);
  Unst South 1936's day; Gulberwick Oct 1936 (Rev. George Smith's appointment not found).
- Petition figures that differ in the OCR and weren't changed: Aithsting 1921 Clark 58 (wiki 88),
  Gulberwick 1924 Anderson 34 (wiki 54), Sandsting 1930 White 80 (wiki 89). The paper names the
  1921 Aithsting winner "William Clark", but A. D. Clark sat from then (ST 29 Oct 1921), so the
  DB's Andrew Clark stands. The Sandness 1921 item says Harrison replaced "the late Mr T. A.
  Anderson", a slip for David Sutherland (Anderson was Aithsting's).
