# ZCC 1920–1939: citations for the generals and by-elections

Researched 2026-09-29 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times). Every link opens the
BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** for the OCR text.

Question: find a source for each Zetland County Council election 1920–1939 (citations batch 2,
third run), and check polling days and votes against the DB.

Findings:
- **Dec 1922 general**: polled Tue 5 Dec 1922, as in the DB, in eight divisions. The votes agree
  in the seven the DB has as contests. Two wards were wrong:
  - **Dunrossness North was a contest**: J. R. White 60, A. Irvine jun. 36 (the wiki has White
    unopposed). `fix_newspapers.py` #37.
  - **Aithsting and Sandsting were not combined.** The wiki says the election "recombined" them,
    and `fix_parse_errors.py` #8 had hidden the Sandsting row as a duplicate. But the nominations
    list the two wards separately, with R. A. Sutherland alone for Sandsting, and he sat at the
    new Council's first meeting. #8 now keeps both rows: Aithsting Leslie 110, Clark 89;
    Sandsting Sutherland unopposed, so his seat runs 1922–25 (his intro said 1919–22). The result
    report does call the Leslie–Clark contest "Sandsting and Aithsting", which is presumably where
    the wiki's note came from.
- **Dec 1925 general**: polled **Tue 1 Dec 1925**, not Sat 5th (`fix_newspapers.py` #38). The
  nominations and the candidates' adverts give "Tuesday, 1st December"; the results report's
  "took place on Monday" is taken as a slip. Seventeen unopposed and ten contests, all as in the
  DB, and the votes agree. Two not checked: Sandsting's third candidate (OCR 13, DB 18; the page
  image doesn't render) and Aithsting (Leslie v C. F. Irvine), whose result isn't in the OCR.
- **Dec 1928 general**: polled **Tue 4 Dec 1928**, not Wed 5th (`fix_newspapers.py` #39; the
  wiki's "Tuesday 5 December" has the wrong date). Three contests (Aithsting, Sandness, Walls), as
  in the DB, after three withdrawals; the votes agree. **Walls electorate 389**, not 192, which is
  Sandness's (#40).

## Evidence

### 1. County Council Election December 1922 (elections 468–492, 1242, 1243)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 25 Nov 1922, p5 (art. 096)**: "County Council Representation", the final
  nominations. "There are to be eight contests in all." Aithsting: Leslie and A. D. Clark;
  "Sandsting—Mr Robert A. Sutherland, Sand"; Dunrossness North: J. R. White and A. Irvine jun.
  "The election takes place on 5th December." Every other name agrees with the DB.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19221125&page=0005&article=096
- **Shetland Times, Sat 9 Dec 1922, p4 (art. 078)**: "Polling took place on Tuesday" (5 Dec).
  Nesting Ganson 83, Hunter 73; Cunningsburgh Johnston 80, Anderson 56.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19221209&page=0004&article=078
- **Shetland Times, Sat 16 Dec 1922, p4 (art. 087)**: final results. Delting South 90/43/10/6;
  White "topped the poll with 60 votes, Mr A. Irvine, jun." 36 (OCR heading "Dunrossness
  (South)", but W. Leslie was the only South nomination); Leslie 110, Clark 89; Yell North 61/15;
  Yell South 106/28; Unst South 59/49. All as in the DB. → #37.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19221216&page=0004&article=087
- **Shetland Times, Sat 30 Dec 1922, p5 (art. 084)**: the new Council's first meeting ("on
  Thursday", 21 Dec, per ST 23 Dec p4 art. 084): those present include "R. A. Sutherland". → #8.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19221230&page=0005&article=084

### 2. County Council Election December 1925 (elections 497–523)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 14 Nov 1925, p4 (art. 092)**: nominations for the election "which takes
  place on Tuesday, 1st December".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19251114&page=0004&article=092
- **Shetland Times, Sat 21 Nov 1925, p4 (art. 077)**: "WITHDRAWALS OF CANDIDATES", lodged by 4 p.m.
  Tuesday. Lists the 17 unopposed and the ten contests. Clark withdrew from Aithsting, leaving
  Leslie v C. F. Irvine; J. W. Robertson unopposed in Northmavine South after Hay withdrew.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19251121&page=0004&article=077
- **Shetland Times, Sat 28 Nov 1925, p4 (art. 108)**: a Sandsting candidate's address: the poll
  "on TUESDAY FIRST, 1st December" (the Northmavine North address says the same). → #38.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19251128&page=0004&article=108
- **Shetland Times, Sat 5 Dec 1925, p4 (art. 106)**: "KEEN CONTESTS". "Polling took place on
  Monday" in ten divisions. Lerwick North 366/138, Gulberwick 201/116, Whiteness 79/51, Nesting
  115/50, Sandwick 115/64.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19251205&page=0004&article=106
- **Shetland Times, Sat 12 Dec 1925, p5 (art. 085)**: the rest. Northmavine North Tulloch (majority
  10) v Garriock 23; Dunrossness North Irvine 74, White 51; Walls 44/37/36; Sandsting Bowie 145,
  Hobbin 67, Anderson 13 in the OCR (majority 78).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19251212&page=0005&article=085

### 3. County Council Election December 1928 (elections 526–552)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 17 Nov 1928, p5 (art. 135)**: "SIX PROBABLE CONTESTS", the nominations,
  sitting members starred. Every name agrees with the DB. R. J. H. Ganson moved from Delting South
  to Sandness; E. P. Adie nominated for Delting South.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19281117&page=0005&article=135
- **Shetland Times, Sat 24 Nov 1928, p4 (art. 069)**: "THREE CONTESTS": withdrawals on Tuesday by
  Geo. Leslie (Whiteness), J. T. J. Sinclair (Lerwick Central) and R. D. Ganson (Nesting). "The
  polls in these constituencies take place on Tuesday, 4th December." → #39.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19281124&page=0004&article=069
- **Shetland Times, Sat 8 Dec 1928, p4 (art. 049)**: polls "took place on Tuesday", counted
  Wednesday. Sandness 192 electors, 87 votes: Ganson 48, Moncrieff 39. "In Walls, with an
  electorate of 389, 99 electors voted": Andrew 86, Halcrow 12, one spoiled. Aithsting roll 349,
  194 votes: Anderson 107, Leslie 83, four spoiled. → #39, #40.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19281208&page=0004&article=049
