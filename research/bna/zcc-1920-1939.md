# ZCC 1920–1939: citations for the generals and by-elections

Researched 2026-09-29 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times). Every link opens the
BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** for the OCR text.

Question: find a source for each Zetland County Council election 1920–1939 (citations batch 2,
third run), and check polling days and votes against the DB.

Findings:
- **Dec 1922 general**: polled Tue 5 Dec 1922, as in the DB, in eight divisions. The votes agree
  in the seven the DB has as contests. Two wards were wrong:
  - **Dunrossness North was a contest**: J. R. White 60, A. Irvine jun. 36 (the wiki has White
    unopposed). `fix_newspapers.py` #37.
  - **Aithsting and Sandsting were not combined.** The wiki says the election "recombined" them,
    and `fix_parse_errors.py` #8 had hidden the Sandsting row as a duplicate. But the nominations
    list the two wards separately, with R. A. Sutherland alone for Sandsting, and he sat at the
    new Council's first meeting. #8 now keeps both rows: Aithsting Leslie 110, Clark 89;
    Sandsting Sutherland unopposed, so his seat runs 1922–25 (his intro said 1919–22). The result
    report does call the Leslie–Clark contest "Sandsting and Aithsting", which is presumably where
    the wiki's note came from.
- **Dec 1925 general**: polled **Tue 1 Dec 1925**, not Sat 5th (`fix_newspapers.py` #38). The
  nominations and the candidates' adverts give "Tuesday, 1st December"; the results report's
  "took place on Monday" is taken as a slip. Seventeen unopposed and ten contests, all as in the
  DB, and the votes agree. Two not checked: Sandsting's third candidate (OCR 13, DB 18; the page
  image doesn't render) and Aithsting (Leslie v C. F. Irvine), whose result isn't in the OCR.
- **Dec 1928 general**: polled **Tue 4 Dec 1928**, not Wed 5th (`fix_newspapers.py` #39; the
  wiki's "Tuesday 5 December" has the wrong date). Three contests (Aithsting, Sandness, Walls), as
  in the DB, after three withdrawals; the votes agree. **Walls electorate 389**, not 192, which is
  Sandness's (#40).
- **Dec 1929 general** (the re-constituted Council under the 1929 Act, no Lerwick wards): polled
  Tue 3 Dec 1929, as in the DB. Twelve contests and twelve unopposed, all as in the DB; every
  vote agrees. Sandness was a tie, 45–45: the Returning Officer's casting vote went to Ganson, the
  sitting member ("the first occasion" it was needed in a County Council election). The new
  Council took full office only after 15 May 1930, and "The present County Council will continue
  to function till May". The DB ends the 1928 terms at the Dec 1929 poll (open question).
- **Dec 1932 general**: polled Tue 6 Dec 1932, as in the DB. Three contests (Sandsting, Yell
  North, Yell South); votes and electorates agree. **Two by-elections were missing** from the wiki
  and the DB (`fix_newspapers.py` #41): no nomination was lodged for Sandwick, and James A.
  Jamieson, returned unopposed for Sandness, couldn't sit because he held a minor appointment
  under the Council. New elections were fixed for Tue 14 Feb 1933, and both went unopposed:
  William Jamieson (the former member) for Sandwick and James A. Jamieson again for Sandness. His
  1932 win is in `data/not_seated.csv`. The wiki's Sandwick page says the ward "was incorporated
  into Cunningsburgh and Dunrossness North" in 1932, which the paper contradicts. Sandwick had
  shown as empty 1932–35.
- **Dec 1935 general**: polled Tue 3 Dec 1935, as in the DB. Five contests (Tingwall,
  Northmavine North, Nesting, Walls, Sandwick) and seventeen unopposed, as in the DB (Burra's
  second nomination, John Sutherland, must have withdrawn); no nomination in Unst South. The
  votes agree except **Walls: Halcrow had 4, not 85** (the wiki took the majority as his vote,
  `fix_newspapers.py` #42). William Jamieson was the sitting member for Sandwick and retired, so
  he sat 1933–35 (his intro now says 1919–1935, #43).
- **Dec 1938 general**: polled Tue 6 Dec 1938, as in the DB (a winner's thank-you: "the Poll on
  Tuesday, December 6th"; the 26 Nov preview's "Tuesday first" must mean the 6th, since
  candidates were still advertising on 3 Dec). Six contests, not five: **Yell South** was Manson
  166, Leask 145 (the wiki has Manson unopposed, and puts Yell South's electorate and turnout
  under Yell North, which had 257 electors and 158 votes), `fix_newspapers.py` #45. **Whalsay:
  Hay had 32, not 121** (the majority again, #44). The other votes agree.

## Evidence

### 1. County Council Election December 1922 (elections 468–492, 1242, 1243)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 25 Nov 1922, p5 (art. 096)**: "County Council Representation", the final
  nominations. "There are to be eight contests in all." Aithsting: Leslie and A. D. Clark;
  "Sandsting—Mr Robert A. Sutherland, Sand"; Dunrossness North: J. R. White and A. Irvine jun.
  "The election takes place on 5th December." Every other name agrees with the DB.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19221125&page=0005&article=096
- **Shetland Times, Sat 9 Dec 1922, p4 (art. 078)**: "Polling took place on Tuesday" (5 Dec).
  Nesting Ganson 83, Hunter 73; Cunningsburgh Johnston 80, Anderson 56.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19221209&page=0004&article=078
- **Shetland Times, Sat 16 Dec 1922, p4 (art. 087)**: final results. Delting South 90/43/10/6;
  White "topped the poll with 60 votes, Mr A. Irvine, jun." 36 (OCR heading "Dunrossness
  (South)", but W. Leslie was the only South nomination); Leslie 110, Clark 89; Yell North 61/15;
  Yell South 106/28; Unst South 59/49. All as in the DB. → #37.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19221216&page=0004&article=087
- **Shetland Times, Sat 30 Dec 1922, p5 (art. 084)**: the new Council's first meeting ("on
  Thursday", 21 Dec, per ST 23 Dec p4 art. 084): those present include "R. A. Sutherland". → #8.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19221230&page=0005&article=084

### 3. County Council Election December 1928 (elections 526–552)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 17 Nov 1928, p5 (art. 135)**: "SIX PROBABLE CONTESTS", the nominations,
  sitting members starred. Every name agrees with the DB. R. J. H. Ganson moved from Delting South
  to Sandness; E. P. Adie nominated for Delting South.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19281117&page=0005&article=135
- **Shetland Times, Sat 24 Nov 1928, p4 (art. 069)**: "THREE CONTESTS": withdrawals on Tuesday by
  Geo. Leslie (Whiteness), J. T. J. Sinclair (Lerwick Central) and R. D. Ganson (Nesting). "The
  polls in these constituencies take place on Tuesday, 4th December." → #39.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19281124&page=0004&article=069
- **Shetland Times, Sat 8 Dec 1928, p4 (art. 049)**: polls "took place on Tuesday", counted
  Wednesday. Sandness 192 electors, 87 votes: Ganson 48, Moncrieff 39. "In Walls, with an
  electorate of 389, 99 electors voted": Andrew 86, Halcrow 12, one spoiled. Aithsting roll 349,
  194 votes: Anderson 107, Leslie 83, four spoiled. → #39, #40.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19281208&page=0004&article=049

### 4. County Council Election December 1929 (elections 553–576)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 23 Nov 1929, p5 (art. 135)**: "Re-Constituted County Council". Withdrawals
  to 4 p.m. Tuesday: McDougall from Nesting (still standing in Dunrossness South), Leslie from
  Aithsting. "12 contests in the 24 divisions", and the 12 unopposed listed. "The election in the
  12 contested divisions takes place on Tuesday, 3rd December", but the new Council "only takes
  over full duty after 15th May next".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19291123&page=0005&article=135
- **Shetland Times, Sat 7 Dec 1929, p4 (art. 116)**: "Eight Mainland District Results", counted
  Wednesday. Dunrossness South 95/42, Cunningsburgh 69/27, Bressay 42/23, Whiteness 87/47.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19291207&page=0004&article=116
- **Shetland Times, Sat 7 Dec 1929, p4 (art. 124)**: "A TIE". Sandness Ganson 45, Thomas Sutherland
  45; the casting vote "in favour of the member of the present Council". Sandsting 102/77, Nesting
  91/63/17, Northmavine North 68/38.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19291207&page=0004&article=124
- **Shetland Times, Sat 14 Dec 1929, p4 (art. 081)**: "NORTH ISLES ELECTION RESULTS", counted on
  Saturday. Unst North 126/92, Unst South 83 (majority 63), Yell North 110/18, Fetlar 37/23.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19291214&page=0004&article=081

### 5. County Council Election December 1932 (elections 582–604) and the Feb 1933 by-elections
- **Shetland Times, Sat 19 Nov 1932, p4 (art. 028)**: "Three Probable Contests". One nomination
  in every district except North Yell, South Yell and Sandsting, "and Sandwick from which no
  nomination was lodged". The list (retiring members starred) agrees with the DB. Polling "on
  Tuesday, 6th December". Robert Smith (Yell North) took his seat "at the October meeting".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19321119&page=0004&article=028
- **Shetland Times, Sat 10 Dec 1932, p4 (art. 015)**: polling "took place on Tuesday". Sandsting:
  Arthurson 132, Aston 91; electorate 420, 224 voted.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19321210&page=0004&article=015
- **Shetland Times, Sat 17 Dec 1932, p7 (art. 040)**: Yell North (307 electors, 137 voted): Robert
  Smith 93, C. S. Nicholson 40. "NEW MEMBER FOR YELL SOUTH" (554, 287): Manson 153, J. A. Smith 131.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19321217&page=0007&article=040
- **Shetland Times, Sat 14 Jan 1933, p4 (art. 055)**: the Scottish Office ordered new elections in
  Sandwick ("no nominations") and Sandness ("the unopposed candidate is not eligible to sit").
  Nominations by Tue 24 Jan; "The elections take place on Tuesday, 14th February." → #41.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19330114&page=0004&article=055
- **Shetland Times, Sat 28 Jan 1933, p4 (art. 092)**: both filled by one nomination each, William
  Jamieson for Sandwick and James A. Jamieson for Sandness ("not eligible to sit owing to his
  holding a minor appointment under the Council. This appointment he resigned"). "There will be
  no poll." → #41, `not_seated.csv`.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19330128&page=0004&article=092

### 6. County Council Election December 1935 (elections 608–631)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 16 Nov 1935, p5 (arts. 098, 108)**: "MOST OF SITTING MEMBERS RETURNED
  UNOPPOSED", nominations closed Tuesday (art. 108 carries the text; the OCR is poor). No
  nomination for Unst South; "the sitting member, Mr William Jamieson, Central House, Sandwick,
  has definitely retired from the Council and has not sought nomination". Contests as nominated
  in Northmavine North, Walls, Nesting, Tingwall, Burra (Henderson and John Sutherland) and
  Sandwick. Seventeen "returned unopposed", as in the DB. → #43.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19351116&page=0005&article=108
- **Shetland Times, Sat 7 Dec 1935, p4 (art. 061)**: "Polling in the five districts ... took place
  on Tuesday", counted Wednesday. Tingwall Garriock 228, Mouat 107 (336 votes); Northmavine North
  Johnson 85, Tulloch (majority 39); Nesting Sutherland 111, Anderson (majority 62), Hay 13; Walls
  "John G. Williamson ... 89; *Andrew Halcrow ... 4—majority 85, Total electorate 328, total votes
  cast 93, or 28.3 per cent"; Sandwick Thomson 138, Dingwall 127. → #42.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19351207&page=0004&article=061
