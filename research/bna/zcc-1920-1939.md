# ZCC 1920–1939: citations for the generals and by-elections

Researched 2026-09-29 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times). Every link opens the
BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** for the OCR text.

Question: find a source for each Zetland County Council election 1920–1939 (citations batch 2,
third run), and check polling days and votes against the DB.

Findings:
- **Dec 1922 general**: polled Tue 5 Dec 1922, as in the DB, in eight divisions. The votes agree
  in the seven the DB has as contests. Two wards were wrong:
  - **Dunrossness North was a contest**: J. R. White 60, A. Irvine jun. 36 (the wiki has White
    unopposed). `fix_newspapers.py` #37.
  - **Aithsting and Sandsting were not combined.** The wiki says the election "recombined" them,
    and `fix_parse_errors.py` #8 had hidden the Sandsting row as a duplicate. But the nominations
    list the two wards separately, with R. A. Sutherland alone for Sandsting, and he sat at the
    new Council's first meeting. #8 now keeps both rows: Aithsting Leslie 110, Clark 89;
    Sandsting Sutherland unopposed, so his seat runs 1922–25 (his intro said 1919–22). The result
    report does call the Leslie–Clark contest "Sandsting and Aithsting", which is presumably where
    the wiki's note came from.

## Evidence

### 1. County Council Election December 1922 (elections 468–492, 1242, 1243)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 25 Nov 1922, p5 (art. 096)**: "County Council Representation", the final
  nominations. "There are to be eight contests in all." Aithsting: Leslie and A. D. Clark;
  "Sandsting—Mr Robert A. Sutherland, Sand"; Dunrossness North: J. R. White and A. Irvine jun.
  "The election takes place on 5th December." Every other name agrees with the DB.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19221125&page=0005&article=096
- **Shetland Times, Sat 9 Dec 1922, p4 (art. 078)**: "Polling took place on Tuesday" (5 Dec).
  Nesting Ganson 83, Hunter 73; Cunningsburgh Johnston 80, Anderson 56.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19221209&page=0004&article=078
- **Shetland Times, Sat 16 Dec 1922, p4 (art. 087)**: final results. Delting South 90/43/10/6;
  White "topped the poll with 60 votes, Mr A. Irvine, jun." 36 (OCR heading "Dunrossness
  (South)", but W. Leslie was the only South nomination); Leslie 110, Clark 89; Yell North 61/15;
  Yell South 106/28; Unst South 59/49. All as in the DB. → #37.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19221216&page=0004&article=087
- **Shetland Times, Sat 30 Dec 1922, p5 (art. 084)**: the new Council's first meeting ("on
  Thursday", 21 Dec, per ST 23 Dec p4 art. 084): those present include "R. A. Sutherland". → #8.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19221230&page=0005&article=084
