# James Inkster of Delting North: the merchant at Brae, not the poet

Researched 2026-10-01 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times) and Bayanne. Raised while
cross-checking the first County Council's by-elections against the 1890 election chapter.

Question: the DB links every Delting North County Council candidacy 1890–1904 to `james-inkster`
(id 232), the poet and Lerwick bookseller (Bayanne I89755, 1850–1927). Is that right?

Finding: **no**. The Delting North councillor was **James Thomas Anderson Inkster, merchant,
Brae** (Bayanne I90168; b. 23 Dec 1854 Brae, d. 30 Mar 1907 Brae). Every nominations list that
gives an address calls him "James Inkster, Brae", and in 1898 and 1904 "merchant, Brae". The poet
was at Greenbank, North Yell, from about 1882 (his children's births) until 1896, then in Lerwick,
and his obituary says nothing of the County Council. The merchant's obituary says he was elected
when the Council "came into being" and sat for several terms.

Open points:
- The 1907 obituary says he represented "South Belting" [Delting]; every nominations list puts him
  in **North** Delting. Probably the obituary's slip (he was on Delting South's Parish Council).
- The obituary says he "did not seek re-election five years ago", but the 1904 nominations list
  him for Delting North against William Pole, and the DB has him losing in Dec 1904 (13 votes). Not
  checked against the 1904 result.
- The Lerwick Town Council candidacy of Nov 1903 (140 votes) is the poet's: the paper calls the
  candidate a member of the Lerwick School Board (ST 31 Oct 1903, p4 art. 061), as the poet's
  obituary says he was. The nominations give no address.

Fixed 2026-10-01 (`fix_parse_errors.py` #23): new person `james-t-a-inkster`, the six Delting North
candidacies moved to him, and "County Councillor" taken out of the poet's intro, biography and
categories. The poet now has only the 1903 candidacy, so he is a candidate-only person with a
page; whether he keeps it is James's call.

## Evidence

**Shetland Times, Sat 24 May 1890, p3 (art. 043)**: the Council appoints "Mr James Inkster, Brae"
for North Delting, vacant by Hoseason's resignation.
https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18900524&page=0003&article=043

**Shetland Times, Sat 26 Nov 1892, p3 (art. 045)**: "In North Delting J. Inkster, Brae, who has
proved himself a useful member", opposed by T. Hanton; the list: "North Delting James Inkster, Brae".
https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18921126&page=0003&article=045

**Shetland Times, Sat 23 Nov 1895, p3 (art. 056)**: "Delting—North Mr James Inkster" (no address).
https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18951123&page=0003&article=056

**Shetland Times, Sat 10 Dec 1898, p4 (art. 048)**: Parish Council, South Delting: "Mr James
Inkster, merchant, Brae".
https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18981210&page=0004&article=048

**Shetland Times, Sat 23 Nov 1901, p4 (art. 064)**: Parish Council nominations, Delting South:
"James Inkster, Brae".
https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19011123&page=0004&article=064

**Shetland Times, Sat 26 Nov 1904, p4 (art. 041)**: County Council nominations: "Delting North—Mr
James Inkster, merchant, Brae, and Mr Wm. Pole, merchant, Mossbank".
https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19041126&page=0004&article=041

**Shetland Times, Sat 6 Apr 1907, p4 (art. 070)**: death notice: "At Brae, Shetland, on the 30th
March, James T. A. Inkster, merchant."
https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19070406&page=0004&article=070

**Shetland Times, Sat 6 Apr 1907, p5 (art. 099)**: "Death of Mr James Inkster, Brae": died at his
residence on Saturday evening (30 Mar), aged 52. "After the County Council came into being, Mr
Inkster was elected member of that body", on the Council, the Road Board and the Mainland District
Committee "for a number of years"; a regular attender. Chairman of the School Board at his death;
on the Parish Council.
https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19070406&page=0005&article=099

**Shetland Times, Sat 31 Oct 1903, p4 (art. 061)**: leader on the Town Council candidates: "Mr Inkster is a docile member of the party who ... 'plays the game,' at the Lerwick School Board". The nominations (same issue, art. 070): "James Inkster—Proposed by Mr T. J. Anderson", no address.
https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19031031&page=0004&article=061

**Shetland Times, Sat 15 Jan 1927, p4 (art. 087)**: "Death of Mr James Inkster" (the poet): born
at Sandvoe, North Roe; manager of Pole, Hoseason & Co.'s business at Greenbank, North Yell, "until
1896", then Lerwick; Reid Tait's outfitting department; writer of Mansie's Röd; "associated with
public work in Yell" and the Lerwick School Board. No mention of the County Council.
https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19270115&page=0004&article=087

Bayanne: I89755 (the poet: children born Cullivoe and Greenbank, North Yell, 1882–1894; 1901
census 3 Albany Street, Lerwick) and I90168 (James Thomas Anderson Inkster, "Also Known As Anderson
Inkster", merchant; 1881 census Brae; son of James Inkster, 1819–1872, and Willa Morrison Anderson).
