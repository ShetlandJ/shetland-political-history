# LTC 1908–1911: election results (starts of the rows sitting through Oct 1912)

Researched 2026-09-28 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times). Every link opens the
BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** in the toolbar for the OCR text.

Question: the Oct–Nov 1912 size-short row is MacDougall's dated vacancy (resigned Tue 8 Oct
1912, `ltc-1912-1914.md`). It only clears once every row sitting through it is confirmed. The
unconfirmed ones were the 1909, 1910 and 1911 cohorts; their ends were already sourced in
`ltc-1912-1914.md` (the 1912, 1913 and 1914 retiring lists), so what was missing was the starts.
The 1908 cohort's rows (1908–11) were done at the same time.

## Evidence

### 1. November 1908: the four retiring members returned
- **Shetland Times, Sat 7 Nov 1908, p4 (art. 149)**, "Declaration of the Poll": Provost Porteous
  read the result, "the return of the former candidates" received with applause.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19081107&page=0004&article=149
- **Shetland Times, Sat 7 Nov 1908, p1 (art. 020)**: Goodlad thanks the electors for returning him.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19081107&page=0001&article=020
- The four who retired (Ratter, Goodlad, Ganson, W. Sinclair; `ltc-1908-1910.md`) are the DB's
  four winners, ahead of Brown and Allison.
- **Ledger**: `alexander-ratter`, `peter-goodlad`, `robert-ganson-i`, `william-sinclair`
  1908-11-03 confirmed (end from §4).

### 2. November 1909: the four retiring members returned
- **Shetland Times, Sat 6 Nov 1909, p4 (art. 070)**: the vote on Tuesday returned "the four
  retiring Councillors", and the new candidate (Loggie) was left out.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19091106&page=0004&article=070
- **Shetland Times, Sat 6 Nov 1909, p1 (art. 006)**: thanks notice signed Arthur L. Laing, John
  Smith and R. Stout for "again electing us" (the snippet; the rest of the signatures not read).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19091106&page=0001&article=006
- **Ledger**: `james-laing`, `john-smith-i`, `robert-stout-i` 1909-11-02 confirmed (ends: ST 5
  and 12 Oct 1912, `ltc-1912-1914.md` §2).

### 3. November 1910: MacDougall, A. Smith, W. S. Smith, Loggie elected
- **Shetland Times, Sat 5 Nov 1910, p4 (art. 077)**, "Lerwick Town Council. Election": count on
  the Tuesday night, "The first four were therefore declared elected". The result table itself
  isn't in the OCR, but the analysis says "only ten votes separating the second and fifth", which
  matches the DB (A. Smith 268, Groat 258). The same article reports the Friday meeting electing
  Bailie Laing Provost in place of ex-Provost Porteous, with Loggie and W. S. Smith present.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19101105&page=0004&article=077
- **Ledger**: `andrew-smith`, `william-smith-ii` 1910-11-01 confirmed (ends: ST 11 Oct 1913,
  `ltc-1912-1914.md` §4).

### 4. November 1911: the four retiring by rotation returned unopposed
- **Shetland Times, Sat 7 Oct 1911, p4 (art. 108)**, "Retiring Councillors": Bailie Goodlad and
  Councillors Ganson, Sinclair and Ratter.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19111007&page=0004&article=108
- **Shetland Times, Sat 14 Oct 1911, p1 (art. 004)**: election notice for four councillors in place
  of Ratter, W. Sinclair, R. D. Ganson and Bailie P. S. Goodlad, "who retire by rotation".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19111014&page=0001&article=004
- **Shetland Times, Sat 11 Nov 1911, p4 (art. 093)**: meeting on Thursday to swear in "the
  newly-elected Councillors"; present include Goodlad, Ratter, W. Sinclair, Ganson.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19111111&page=0004&article=093
- Unopposed already established in `ltc-uncontested-generals.md`.
- **Ledger**: `alexander-ratter`, `robert-ganson-i`, `william-sinclair` 1911-11-07 confirmed
  (ends: ST 24 and 31 Oct 1914, `ltc-1912-1914.md` §5). Goodlad's 1911 row was already confirmed.

## Result

12 rows confirmed; the 8 Oct–5 Nov 1912 row cleared as a fully confirmed genuine vacancy
(term_issues 17 → 16), no new issues. Nothing contradicted the wiki or the ledger.

Not checked: John Irvine (iii)'s 1907 row, which ends at the Jan 1910 by-election unconfirmed
(his departure isn't sourced). It doesn't sit through any open issue.
