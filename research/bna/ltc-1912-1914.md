# LTC 1912–1914: retiring lists and council size

Researched 2026-09-27 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times). Every link opens the
BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** in the toolbar for the OCR text.

Question: resolve the Goodlad (1911/1913) and John Smith (i) (1912/1914) overlaps, and decide
the 1912–14 council size (CLAUDE.md "Current state").

## Evidence

### 1. MacDougall's April 1912 resignation was withdrawn
- **Shetland Times, Sat 6 Apr 1912, p4 (art. 076)**: the Provost read a letter from Councillor
  Macdougall resigning as Town Councillor.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19120406&page=0004&article=076
- **Shetland Times, Sat 6 Apr 1912, p4 (art. 060)**: by Friday morning he "had reconsidered his
  decision to resign".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19120406&page=0004&article=060

### 2. October 1912: MacDougall still sitting; retiring list
- **Shetland Times, Sat 5 Oct 1912, p4 (art. 068)**: monthly meeting (Tue 1 Oct 1912). Present
  include Provost Laing, Bailies Stout and Goodlad, Councillors Ganson, Loggie, **Macdougall**,
  Sinclair.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19121005&page=0004&article=068
- **Shetland Times, Sat 5 Oct 1912, p4 (art. 066)**: retiring in November: Bailie Stout,
  Councillors John Smith, James Laing, J. A. Loggie. **Provost (Arthur) Laing is not on the
  list**, although his 1909 cohort was due out.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19121005&page=0004&article=066
- **Shetland Times, Sat 2 Nov 1912, p4 (art. 074)**: election preview. Vacancies are caused by the
  retiral of Stout, John Smith, James Laing and Loggie, and "the resignation of Councillor
  W. Macdougall".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19121102&page=0004&article=074
- **Shetland Times, Sat 12 Oct 1912, p4 (art. 101)**: "Resignation of a Councillor". The
  automatic retirals were Stout, John Smith, James Laing and Loggie. Loggie told the paper on
  Tuesday he wouldn't seek re-election. "On Tuesday" (8 Oct 1912) MacDougall resigned, and the
  article says Loggie was then withdrawn from retiring. The later 2 Nov preview and the Nov 1912
  attendance show Loggie did go out after all, so that arrangement didn't hold.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19121012&page=0004&article=101
- Letter, **Sat 19 Oct 1912, p4 (art. 093)**: one side withdrew a candidate, so the election went
  uncontested.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19121019&page=0004&article=093

### 3. After the Nov 1912 general: Arthur Laing still Provost; 12 members
- **Shetland Times, Sat 9 Nov 1912, p4 (art. 078)**: first meeting after the election, "Provost
  Laing presiding"; Stout re-appointed Bailie.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19121109&page=0004&article=078
- **Shetland Times, Sat 16 Nov 1912, p4 (art. 061)**: adjourned meeting (Mon 11 Nov 1912).
  Present: Provost Laing, Bailies Stout and Goodlad, Councillors R. D. Ganson, W. S. Smith,
  A. Smith, R. H. Ramsay, J. Smith, W. Sinclair, A. Ratter, J. Laing. That's 11, and Grierson (a
  Nov 1912 winner) makes 12.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19121116&page=0004&article=061

- **Shetland Times, Sat 11 Oct 1913, p4 (art. 062)**: "Retiring Councillors": Provost Laing,
  Bailie Goodlad, Councillors Andrew Smith and W. Spence Smith.

  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19131101&page=0004&article=054

### 5. November 1914: four retiring, including John Smith; returned unopposed
- **Shetland Times, Sat 24 Oct 1914, p4 (art. 063)**: election on Tue 3 Nov. Four retiring
  councillors, starting with Bailie Ganson.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19141024&page=0004&article=063
- **Shetland Times, Sat 31 Oct 1914, p4 (art. 059)**: the four retiring members, Bailie Ganson,
  Councillors Alex. Ratter, Wm. Sinclair and John Smith, were renominated with no new candidates,
  so they were declared elected.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19141031&page=0004&article=059

## What it shows

- **Arthur Laing** (Provost) did not retire in 1912. Loggie retired in his place (the
  "Provost stays on" rule). Laing sat until the Nov 1913 general and did not re-stand.
- **MacDougall** sat until Tuesday 8 October 1912, not April.
- **Goodlad's** 1911 seat ran only to Nov 1913, and **John Smith (i)'s** 1912 seat only to Nov
  1914. The paper doesn't say why. The effect is 4 retirements a year: 1913 = the 1910 cohort
  remnant (A. Smith, W. S. Smith) + A. Laing + Goodlad; 1914 = Ganson, Ratter, Sinclair + J. Smith.
- **Council size 1912–14: 12 throughout.** That settles the open question: the council was not
  at 11.
