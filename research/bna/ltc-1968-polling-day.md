# LTC May 1968 polling day

Queue item: the baseline dates the May 1968 Lerwick Town Council election Thursday 2 May 1968.
Every other year from 1949 to 1973 polled on the first Tuesday. Checked 2026-09-28.

**Result: Tuesday 7 May 1968.** `fix_newspapers.py` #13 moves the election date. In the ledger,
the eight rows starting or ending on the 1968 general move to 1968-05-07, and the four 1968 winners
(Cumming, H. Gray, Thomson, Halcrow) are confirmed, since their end dates were already sourced.
Issues: 41 → 41.

## Evidence

- **ST Fri 19 Apr 1968, p4 (art. 059)**: "Eight candidates for four seats". Nominations closed on
  Tuesday. Paton (Lab.), Halcrow, Cumming and H. Gray (Ind.) seek re-election; the new candidates
  include W. P. L. Thomson and Robert Adair (Lab.) and Tom Moncrieff (Ind.), now eligible because
  the town's seats on the County Council were cut from twelve to nine. "Polling takes place in
  Lerwick on 7th May."
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19680419&page=0004&article=059
- **ST Fri 10 May 1968, p4 (art. 062)**: "The result". Successful: Cumming 791, H. Gray 724,
  W. Thomson 686, Halcrow 577. Unsuccessful: Paton 573, Adair 555, Moncrieff 399, D. Leask 372.
  These are the same figures as the baseline. The council stays eight Independent and four Labour.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19680510&page=0004&article=062
- ST 26 Apr 1968 p3 and p4 (art. 050 and 060): a public meeting of the candidates, sponsored by
  the paper. ST 3 May 1968 p5 (art. 083): Cumming's election address. Neither gives the date.
