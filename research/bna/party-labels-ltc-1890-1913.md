# Party labels: Lerwick Town Council, 1890–1913

Researched 2026-09-30 (queue: Labels 1). Method: manifest sweep of the Shetland Times for the
Saturday two weeks before, the Saturday before and the Saturday after each November general
(Town Council, election and p1 advert articles, OCR read), plus the result and nomination
articles already in `data/citations.csv`, plus two searches. Builds on
`early-socialists-1901-1913.md`, which has the fuller narrative. BNA link pattern:
`https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2FYYYYMMDD&page=NNNN&article=NNN`.

Labels go in `data/candidacy_labels.csv` only when declared (see `queue.md`). Unnamed groups are
recorded here and left blank.

## 1890–1900: no labels

Every general 1890–93, 1895–98 and 1900 was unopposed; no nomination or result report uses a
label. Two contests:

- **Nov 1894** (election 46). ST 10 Nov 1894 p2 a040 "Municipal Election": "the Committee which
  had been formed for the support of Bailie Leisk, Messrs A. J. Garriock, and J. M. Goudie" issued
  a specimen ballot paper and canvassed; "those who favoured the other two candidates" (A. L.
  Laing, David Sutherland) did not. An unnamed committee: no label. Sutherland, 85 votes, stood
  again in 1899 and as a Socialist in 1913.
- **Nov 1899** (election 52). Nothing in the nominations (ST 28 Oct p4 a101) or result (11 Nov
  p5 a117). No label.

## 1901 (election 54)

- ST 9 Nov 1901 p4 a083: James Robertson spoke at his own meeting in the Rechabite Hall, "his
  views as Social-Democrat". **Label: Social-Democrat** (confirms the DB). He is the candidate
  the other session linked to Bayanne I28791 (Dr James Robertson; `fix_newspapers.py` #68).

## 1903 (election 56)

- ST 7 Nov 1903 p4 a058: Robertson addressed a meeting; Ganson, Inkster and Kay issued a joint
  manifesto. No label for anyone this time; Robertson's p1 adverts (24 and 31 Oct) are his
  auctioneer's business. Left blank (open question: Robertson 1903).

## 1904 (election 57): no labels

Nothing in the p1 adverts or the result (ST 5 Nov 1904 p4 a112).

## 1905 (election 59)

- ST 11 Nov 1905 p4 a071: the canvass was strong "especially by the members of the Working Men's
  Association, who were running two candidates"; only those two sent a circular.
- ST 11 Nov 1905 p1 a007: Alexander Ratter and William Sinclair thank the electors for electing
  them, "as Working-Class Representatives".
- **Label: Working Men's Association** for Ratter (DB blank). Sinclair is "Labour" in the DB: a
  conflict, left for James (open question). The WMA was affiliated to the SDF (ST 30 Jun 1906).

## 1906 (election 60)

- ST 10 Nov 1906 p4 a073 "Lerwick Municipal Contest": "The Socialist section was well
  represented" with a systematic canvass and a circular. The candidate isn't named. James Laing
  (nominated by the SDF founders Pottinger and Manson, ST 3 Nov 1906 p4 a071) is the likely one.
  No advert with a label. Left blank (open question).

## 1907 (election 61)

- ST 9 Nov 1907 p4 a063 "Counting the votes": M. L. Manson agent for Charles G. Addison; "the
  Socialist had no fewer than 165 plumpers". **Label: Socialist** for Addison (confirms the DB).
- ST 9 Nov 1907 p1 a016: joint thanks notice signed Irvine, A. Smith, J. Smith, W. S. Smith. No
  group name. The DB gives all four "Ratepayers Association (unofficial)"; the Lerwick
  Ratepayers' Association was only formed in Oct 1908 (below). Not confirmed (open question).

## 1908 (election 62)

- ST 24 Oct 1908 p5 a122: the Lerwick Ratepayers' Association formed at a Town Hall meeting.
- ST 7 Nov 1908 p5 a161: its meeting of Mon 2 Nov was held "for the purpose of hearing the views
  of the candidates for the Town Council": every nominated candidate was invited. A hustings, not
  a slate.
- ST 7 Nov 1908 p4 a148: Manson agent for Sinclair, Pottinger for Ratter; "One enthusiastic voter
  had 'signed' for the Socialists"; 189 voted for Ratter and Sinclair only.
- ST 31 Oct 1908 p5 a099–a100 (letters): the new Association is a "Socialist caucus"; "all the
  forthcoming Socialist candidates" spoke at its first meeting.
- The DB gives Ratter and Sinclair "Ratepayers Association". The paper calls them the
  Socialists; nothing says the Association ran them. Not confirmed (open question).

## 1909 (election 63)

- ST 6 Nov 1909 p4 a070: A. L. Laing, Stout and J. Smith "decided to stand together" with a joint
  circular (no name). James Laing and Loggie stood alone. Loggie's circular said "he stood as an
  independent candidate". **Label: Independent** for Loggie.
- ST 5 Nov 1910 p4 a077, looking back: the plumpers for "the nominee of the local Socialists
  Party" were 126 "Last year, when Mr James Laing was elected". **Label: Socialist** for James
  Laing (confirms the DB).

## 1910 (election 65)

- ST 5 Nov 1910 p4 a077 "A Socialist Defeat": "Mr Groat, the nominee of the local Social-Democrat
  Party", and later "the nominee of the local Socialists Party". The DB has "Socialist/Democrat",
  which is neither form. Open question: alias it to "Social-Democrat", or leave.

## 1911 (election 66): unopposed, no labels

## 1912 (election 67)

- ST 2 Nov 1912 p4 a061 (leader): a ratepayers' committee, "almost all the clergymen in town",
  found candidates "pledged against the opening of unlicensed refreshment places on Sunday":
  Grierson, Ramsay and H. J. Robertson. W. J. Greig came forward "as an independent candidate".
  Greig and Robertson withdrew (p4 a074), so the five were returned unopposed; four of them for
  Sunday closing, James Laing against. The committee isn't named in 1912 (it's the Sunday Closing
  Committee in 1913). Grierson and Ramsay left blank (open question).

## 1913 (election 68)

- ST 8 Nov 1913 p4 a064 "Return of Sunday-Closing Candidates. Socialist Defeat.": one circular
  "signed by Dr Willcock on behalf of the Sunday Closing Committee, favouring the candidature of
  Messrs P. S. Goodlad, L. Laurenson, Andrew Smith, and W. S. Smith, and the other by the two
  Socialist candidates—Messrs L. Sandison and David Sutherland". **Labels: Sunday Closing** for
  the four (DB blank), **Socialist** for Sandison and Sutherland (confirms the DB).
