# Early socialists in Lerwick local government, 1901–1913

Researched 2026-09-30 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times) for a narrative report (artifact "Lerwick's First Socialists"). Working notes, one block per contest or topic. Citations are ST date, page, article; the BNA link pattern is `https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2FYYYYMMDD&page=NNNN&article=NNN`. No ledger edits: all the rows involved were already confirmed. Not yet added to data/citations.csv.


## 1901 Nov LTC general (Tue 5 Nov)
- ST 9 Nov 1901 p4 a083 "Municipal Election". James Robertson nominated an hour before close; poll forced. Meeting Mon night Rechabite Hall 9pm, 300-400 present; "first occasion ... Lerwick ratepayers have been treated to platform oratory on municipal matters". Chair David Sutherland. Robertson spoke ~20 min "his views as Social-Democrat": French Revolution, labour question, electric lighting, public lavatories. Vote of thanks moved S. W. Fordyce. Roll close on 900; interest slight. Result: Irvine 307, Harrison 294, Johnson 269, Leisk 264, Robertson 130.
- ST 9 Nov 1901 p1 a7: thanks notice Harrison, Irvine, Johnson, Leisk.
- ZCC Dec 1901 Lerwick North: Tulloch over Robertson (st-19011207-p4-a071) — check if same James Robertson.

## 1903 Nov LTC general (Tue 3 Nov)
- ST 7 Nov 1903 p4 a058 "The Municipal Election". Retiring members made no effort; opponents did. Mon evening Robertson addressed public meeting Rechabite Hall, large audience, 3/4 hr, vote of thanks. Same evening Ganson, Inkster, Kay manifesto + specimen ballot ("new blood meant business", persistent canvassing). 440 voted: "record poll at Lerwick". Result Kay 266 Ganson 242 Goudie 225 Laing 165 | Robertson 163 Inkster 140 Halcrow 107. Robertson 2 votes short of 4th.

## 1905 Nov LTC general (Tue 7 Nov)
- ST 28 Oct 1905 p8 a147 letter "MUNICIPAL ELECTION": "two working men are to seek election"; working class not represented by own class; councils drawn "almost exclusively from the merchant class"; "in every progressive town" working men returned.
- ST 4 Nov 1905 p4 a082 nominations: Sinclair, baker — proposers John Georgeson clerk, James Houston cooper; assenters incl J. G. Laurenson gardener, F. H. Pottinger commission agent, M. L. Anderson grocer, Goodlad shoemaker, L. Laurenson draper, Porteous chemist, W. Spence Smith draper, J. M. Tulloch merchant.

- ST 11 Nov 1905 p1 a007: joint thanks notice by ALEXANDER RATTER and WILLIAM SINCLAIR "as Working-Class Representatives", dated 9 Nov.
- Result: Porteous 282, Sinclair 276, Ratter 260, Goodlad 248 | Laurenson 227, W. S. Smith 205, T. J. Anderson 171.
- **DB: only Sinclair labelled "Labour"; Ratter unlabelled, though he ran jointly as WMA working-class candidate.** Flag.
- ST 11 Nov 1905 p5 a086 letter "SOCIALISM" signed "Formerly a Socialist": mocks socialists for not answering "the able lecture of the above rev gentleman" reported 28 Oct.
- Oct 1905 debate: ST 7 Oct p8 a161 DRINK AND SOCIALISM; 21 Oct p5 a74 THE FALLACIES OF SOCIALISM; 28 Oct p5 a77-86 lecture (What Socialism is not, etc.)
- ST 21 Oct 1905 p5 a074 letter "THE FALLACIES OF SOCIALISM": asks why socialism "acquired such undue prominence in Lerwick"; "a fierce war upon Socialism raging" in both Shetland papers.
- ST 7 Oct 1905 p8 a161 letter "DRINK AND SOCIALISM": replying to Mr Manson (letters on whether drink is the cause of poverty).
- ST 28 Oct 1905 p5 a076-086: Rev. A. J. Campbell, parish minister, lecture on Socialism at Lerwick Debating Society (Tue 24 Oct); ends: socialism may act "as the extreme left of a reconstructed Toryism" — that's the extent of its future.

## 1906
- School Board election (spring 1906): socialists elected, a minority, "pledged to work together" (letter ST 13 Oct 1906 p5 a090 "WORKINGMEN CANDIDATES AND ELECTION PLEDGES", replying to a correspondent "A Socialist"). Letter: "The same applies to the workingmen Town Councillors" — minority, taking their programme point by point; remedy is workers "returning more men from their own number". Magnus Manson joined School Board 1906 (wiki).
- ST 3 Nov 1906 p4 a071 nominations (Tue 30 Oct): James Laing, stonecutter — proposed F. H. Pottinger & M. L. Manson; assenters John Nicolson photographer, Ernest C. Denton cooper, Thomas Irvine baker, E. S. Reid Tait draper, R. J. Groat baker; 2nd nom by St Magnus St / Hill Lane / Pitt Lane residents. Andrew J. Abernethy, photographer — proposed Adam Halcrow tailor & W. M. Pottinger shopman (Halcrow had nominated Sinclair 1905) — possible 2nd working-men candidate; unconfirmed. Kay and J. T. J. Sinclair not re-standing.
- ST 10 Nov 1906 p4 a073 "Lerwick Municipal Contest": 632 voted, record (table: 1906 632, 1905 581, 1904 559, 1903 449, 1901 433, 1899 106; no contests 1900, 1902). Paper hostile: crowd near polling station "chiefly of the section of working men many of whom [do] not know what work is". "The Socialist section was well represented": systematic canvass, a circular whose "chief characteristics being tall talk and want [of] truth". Others canvassed "in a desultory haphazard fashion".
- Result: Stout 334, A. L. Laing 308, James Laing 289, Ganson 275 | Abernethy 246, Greig 103. James Laing (future first Labour Provost) elected.

## 1908
- ST 10 Oct 1908 p5 a127 "A RATEPAYERS' ASSOCIATION": at the Town Council, petition from 8 ratepayers (R. H. Ramsay, J. Spence, D. Sutherland watchmaker, R. W. Tait, M. Anderson, D. Anderson, Jas Inkster, G. G. Kay) to form the Lerwick Ratepayers Association, open to everyone on the roll, "full and free discussion", asking free use of Town Hall for inaugural meeting Wed 14 Oct. Sinclair: "This does not belong to any of the great political parties." Ratter: "We have nothing to do with them." Ganson objected; Sinclair moved, Ratter seconded. Outcome truncated in OCR.
- ST 17 Oct 1908 p5 a111 letter "THE TOWN COUNCIL AND THE TOWN HALL" (RATEPAYER): council "by a majority" refused free use of Town Hall to ratepayers forming an association; Ganson's warship jibe; Provost's "town horse".
- ST 17 Oct 1908 p5 a112 letter (JEAN): ladies are eligible to sit as Town Councillors — why not one or two lady candidates? (suffragette lecture at the Literary Society)
- ST 17 Oct 1908 p1 a167 notice: public meeting of ratepayers, Town Hall, Wed 21 Oct 1908, to form a Ratepayers' Association for the Burgh; chair Rev. A. J. Campbell (the 1905 anti-socialist lecturer!).
- ST 31 Oct 1908 p5 a099 letter "FORMING RATEPAYERS' ASSOCIATIONS" (SPECTATOR): a "Liberal and Unionist Ratepayers' Association" for the parish, open to all "(except Socialists and certain other malcontents)", has existed "for a considerable time". Mocks the new body — suggests calling it "The Social Democratic Ratepayers' Association"; executive named by "wirepullers"; "two of the Socialistic members of the Town Council are in the 'melting-pot'"; D. Sutherland proposed returning all four retiring councillors unopposed. Attacks: "plenty of talking"; one (burgh surveyor resolution; opposed stair carpets; opposed women's waiting room at Victoria Pier); the other proposed cheap gas at £3000 cost.  Socialism "disastrous ... increasing the rates and driving away trade".
- ST 31 Oct 1908 p5 a100 letter "THE SO-CALLED RATEPAYERS' MEETING" (RATEPAYER): non-ratepayers spoke; "all the forthcoming Socialist candidates making violent electioneering speeches"; elected a former Secretary of the L&U Ratepayers' Association as President; David Sutherland "another Socialist" spoke; Campbell's "supernatural innocence"; "the secret Socialist Society, which for years has been the only municipal body to hold secret meetings in a secret chamber"; "an omnium gatherum Socialist caucus".
- ST 31 Oct 1908 p1 a180 notice: Lerwick Ratepayers' Association meeting, Town Hall Mon 2 Nov 8.30pm, candidates invited to state their views.
- ST 7 Nov 1908 p4 a149 "DECLARATION OF THE POLL": "the biggest crowd that was ever present at the declaration of a poll in a municipal election in Lerwick"; return of the former candidates to loud cheering. Result: Ratter 381, Sinclair 360, Ganson 310, Goodlad 297 | John Brown 283, George Allison 249.
- DB labels Ratter & Sinclair 1908 "Ratepayers Association" — consistent with the new (Oct 1908) association, not the L&U one. So both labels (1905 "Labour"/none, 1908 "Ratepayers Association") describe the same working-class pair.
- ST 31 Oct 1908 p4 a089 nominations: George Allison, King Harald St — nominated by Rev. A. J. Campbell and John Brown; John Brown jun., 37 Burgh Road — nominated by James M. Goudie (ex-Provost) and (2nd) Charles J. Duncan & William Fox.
- ST 7 Nov 1908 p4 a148 "COUNTING THE VOTES": M. L. Manson agent for W. Sinclair, F. H. Pottinger agent for A. Ratter. 657 papers, 17 spoiled. "One enthusiastic voter had 'signed' for the Socialists, by writing his name twice". 189 voted for Ratter and Sinclair only. Ratter 381, Sinclair 360 (DB), Ganson 310, Goodlad 297 | Brown 283, Allison 249.

## 1909 Nov LTC general (Tue 2 Nov)
- ST 6 Nov 1909 p4 a070 (leader/report): contest "forced on" the community; A. L. Laing, Stout, J. Smith stood together with a joint circular; "The other two candidates decided to stand alone": James Laing "put his name to a circular which did a good deal of credit to the imagination of the combination who drew it up" (council "an administrative body, not a legislative assembly"); Loggie as "an independent candidate (whatever that may mean)", School Board member since April. Order: James Laing, Stout, A. L. Laing, J. Smith. On James Laing: "done good work in the Council ... practical knowledge and good common-sense"; "the hand might have been the hand of Esau, but the voice was the voice of — well, most people know" (i.e. Manson's); "neither played the mountebank nor the clown". Result (DB): J. Laing 393, Stout 380, A. L. Laing 349, J. Smith 322 | Loggie 269. Socialist topped the poll.
- ST 13 Nov 1909 p7 a141 "DEFEAT OF A SHETLAND SOCIALIST": James Manson, a Lerwegian, stood as a Socialist in Leith 6th ward v Lindsay; lost by 212, polled 633.

## 1910 Nov LTC general (Tue 1 Nov)
- ST 5 Nov 1910 p4 a077 "Lerwick Town Council Election. Close Poll. A Socialist Defeat.": Loggie, A. Smith, W. S. Smith retiring + Porteous's retiral; new: Wm McDougall of the Commercial Bank and "Robert G. Groat, Commercial Road" (DB: Robert John Groat; OCR "R. G."/"E. (1." — initial unclear). "Mr Groat, the nominee of the local Social-Democrat Party" most active canvasser. Stormy, little excitement. 555 voted (analysis OCR says 665; table 1901 433, 1903 449, 1904 559, 1905 581, 1906 632, 1907 654, 1908 657). Groat's agent M. L. Manson. Only 10 votes between 2nd and 5th — "the narrowest that has ever occurred". Plumpers 170: 123 for Groat. "A feature in the voting for the nominee of the local Socialists Party has always been the number of plumpers. Last year, when Mr James Laing was elected, plumpers numbered 126 ... little difference in the Socialist vote as such. But the candidate this year failed to win the general support". Result MacDougall 308, A. Smith 268, W. S. Smith 267, Loggie 262 | Groat 258.

## 1913 Nov LTC general (Tue 4 Nov)

SOCIALIST DEFEAT.": circular by Dr Willcock for the Sunday Closing Committee; the other by the two Socialists. a065: Socialist circular dealt with housing, sanitation, water-supply, municipal lighting, Sunday trading, and the "load-line". a066 PUBLIC MEETING called by the Socialists, Town Hall Mon evening, all candidates invited, only the two Socialists came; hall well filled; chair E. ## The SDF arrives, 1905–06
- ST 5 Aug 1905 p1 a168 notice: "IMPORTANT ADDRESS TO WORKING MEN. Mr Thos. Kennedy, of THE SOCIAL DEMOCRATIC FEDERATION" — Town Hall Fri 11 Aug 1905 8.30pm, "The Social Revolution"; special allusion to steam drifters in the herring fishing; fishermen invited; questions invited.
- ST 12 Aug 1905 p1 a170: same, "To-Night (Friday)", plus OUTDOOR MEETINGS Sat, Mon, Wed 8pm; Mrs Kennedy to speak too.
- ST 19 Aug 1905 p8 a223 letter "A SOCIALIST FALLACY": at Kennedy's Town Hall meeting he was asked about state control of drink / prohibition; replied "the drink problem would have to settle itself". Starts the drink-and-socialism correspondence (2 Sep p5 a101 "A Socialist Fallacy"; 7 Oct p8 a161 "Drink and Socialism" replying to Mr Manson).
- ST 30 Jun 1906 p1 a008 notice: "LERWICK WORKING MEN'S ASSOCIATION (Affiliated to the S.D.F.)" — George Belt, Socialist parliamentary candidate for Hammersmith, public Socialist lecture, Rechabite Hall Fri 6 Jul 1906; outdoor meetings on the Esplanade every evening from Tue 3 Jul. => the WMA that ran Sinclair & Ratter in 1905 was the SDF branch.

### Identified: Dr James Robertson, M.B., Ch.B. (b. 12 Aug 1873 Brough, Nesting; d. Sun 29 Jan 1911, Nordrach-on-Dee) — Bayanne I28791
- ST 4 Feb 1911 p4 a060 (Deaths): "At Nordrach-on-Dee, Kincardineshire, on the 29th January, JAMES ROBERTSON, M.B., Ch.B., eldest surviving son of Thomas and Grace Robertson, North Ness, Lerwick. Aged 37 years." North Ness = the 1901 candidate's address ("Northness").
- ST 4 Feb 1911 p4 a067 obituary "DEATH OF DR JAMES ROBERTSON, OF LERWICK": died at Banchory "on the afternoon of Sunday last" (29 Jan 1911), aged 37, after a long illness. Born in Nesting; as a boy in the Shetland Times bookshop, then the composing room; shopman at Goudie & Sons, ironmongers; house painter in Lerwick, then Edinburgh, where he fell from a scaffold and broke a leg; back in Lerwick in business for himself; then "the business of auctioneering and fish salesman", "the mainspring in forming an Association of local fishermen, largely composed of Burra Isle men" (line and net fishermen; against trawlers). Dr Munro (formerly Scalloway, now Leith) urged him to study medicine; Edinburgh University; health broke down, sat exams in bed; home July 1909(?)–May 1910, then finished his finals and "was duly capped"; straight to Nordrach-on-Dee Sanatorium; stomach operation end of 1910. "For a short period ... a member of the Lerwick School Board ... the 'stormy petrel' of that body." Obituary does not mention socialism or his council candidacies.
- So the Social-Democrat of 1901/1903, the auctioneer of the 1901 County nomination and the 1903 advert, and the diarist of Shetland Archives D1/524 (house-painter, socialist reading, School Board, TB) are one man.
- **Correction to the notes above:** "JAMES ROBERTSON, Auctioneer" adverts to Jun 1912 and the fish salesman listed in 1913 postdate his death; they are another James Robertson (or the business trading on under the name). Not him.
- **Bayanne** gives death 21 Jan 1911; the ST notice and obituary give Sunday 29 Jan 1911.
- Shetland Archives: Series D1/524, Diaries of James Robertson [1873-1911].

## Who was Rev. A. J. Campbell? (looked up 2026-09-30)
The Very Rev. **Andrew James Campbell** D.D. (c.1875–1 May 1950), parish minister of Lerwick Mar 1902–autumn 1909, later Moderator of the General Assembly (1945). Not related to the ZCC Campbells (Rev. James Archibald Campbell, Fetlar, and his son Capt. John A. Campbell). No person row: he never stood for the Town or County Council, only the School Board.
- ST 30 Nov 1901 p4 a055: "Rev. A. J. Campbell, B.A., of East Church, Aberdeen", first on the short leet, to preach at Lerwick Parish Church.
- ST 1 Feb 1902 p4 a062: Lerwick Presbytery fixes his ordination for Fri 14 Mar 1902.
- ST 1 Mar 1902 p4 a051: presentation at St Mary's Chapel, Aberdeen, after 19 months as East Parish assistant.
- ST 11 Sep 1909 p4 a118: elected assistant and successor to Dr David Millar, St John's Parish Church, Glasgow (234 for, 16 against); "Parish Minister at Lerwick since March, 1902".
- Aberdeen Press and Journal 2 May 1950 p6 a082 (BL/0000578): died in Edinburgh "yesterday", in his 75th year; son of John Campbell, Ardallie; Fettes and Cambridge; St John's Glasgow 20 years; Clerk to Glasgow Presbytery from 1929 for seven years; then Evie, Orkney; Moderator 1945.
- Shetland News 4 May 1950 p5 a079 (BL/0003210) obituary "THE LATE DR. A. J. CAMPBELL": born Crathie, schoolmaster's son; St John's College, Cambridge (Classics); chaplain 52nd Division, Gallipoli and Egypt; author of *Two Centuries of the Church of Scotland*; DD Glasgow 1933; on the Union Committee to 1929; retired from Evie May 1948. In Lerwick: helped build St Clement's; a leading member of the School Board, often opposed to F. H. Pottinger; a member of the Lerwick Debating Society. Second wife Anna Mary Robertson M.A., daughter of William Robertson, Commercial Road, Lerwick.
- ST 5 May 1950 p4 a062: "parish minister in Lerwick from 1902-1909", died in Edinburgh on Monday.
- Discrepancies between the obituaries: SN says St John's came "eight years later" and Evie in "1926" (OCR, probably 1936); P&J says seven years in Lerwick. The ST of 1909 settles the Lerwick dates.
