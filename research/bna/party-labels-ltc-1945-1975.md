# Party labels: Lerwick Town Council, 1945–1975

Researched 2026-09-30 (queue: Labels 4). Method: manifest sweep of the Friday papers two weeks
before, the week before and the week after each general (the paper was a Friday one from 1944),
the cited result reports, and a few searches. BNA link pattern:
`https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2FYYYYMMDD&page=NNNN&article=NNN`.

**Summary.** From 1945 to the last election in 1973 every contest was Labour against
non-Labour. Labour candidates always stood as a named team ("Lerwick Labour Party nominees",
"the undersigned Labour candidates", "The Labour Group"), so their labels are confirmed nearly
every year. The paper usually calls them "Socialists"; the candidates' own adverts say Labour, so
the DB's "Labour" stands. The other side's name moved about: "Independents" (1945–49, 1953–73),
"Moderate" (1950–52, and in the Shetland News in 1958), sometimes only "independently". The
labels don't stop: in 1972 the paper still counted the council as "six Labour, six Independent".

Rows are confirmations unless marked new. 151 rows; 3 new (Lamont, Paton and Mouat in 1945).

## 1945 (election 110)

- ST 2 Nov 1945 p2 a034: "Lerwick Labour Party nominees were Messrs R. A. Anderson, Arthur
  Johnson, Alex Morrison, and Thomas A. Sinclair, all former members with newcomers in Messrs.
  Peter Lamont and James Paton"; Gray, Ollason, Peterson and L. W. Smith "standing as
  Independents". p1 a103: Mouat "again nominated as an independent member".
- **New:** Lamont and Paton Labour, Mouat Independent (all blank in the DB).

## 1946 (election 113)

- ST 25 Oct 1946 p5 a087, 1 Nov p4 a051: the five "representing Labour's interests" (Aitken,
  Brownlie, Dalziel, Johnson, Thomason); five Independents "standing jointly" (Bain, Burgess,
  Coghill, J. D. Williamson and one more), W. Anderson "an Independent candidate".
- ST 8 Nov 1946 p5 a070: result table tagging every candidate (Lab.), (Ind.) or, for Reid,
  (Workers). ST 15 Nov p1 a128: Reid "stood as a WORKERS' CANDIDATE". The DB has "Workers Party"
  (open question).

## 1947 (election 114)

- ST 24 Oct 1947 p4 a082: Labour's four (Anderson, Morrison, Thomason, and Paton selected when
  J. R. Smith wasn't on the roll); three Independents (J. D. Williamson, J. N. Inkster, G.
  Fordyce). 31 Oct p1 a029: Mouat "again nominated as an Independent Candidate".

## 1949 (election 115)

- ST 1 Apr 1949 p3 a039: "The Labour Party have announced the names of four members": John
  Anderson, J. R. Davidson, R. B. Blance, Arthur Johnson.
- ST 29 Apr 1949 p4 a050: "FOUR Socialist and FOUR Independent". The four others' joint address
  (p1 a008) has no party name, so their rows are `inferred`.

## 1950 (election 117)

- ST 21 Apr 1950 p4 a047: Groat, Aitken, Johnson, Blance "(all Socialist)"; Burgess and J. D.
  Williamson "(Moderate)"; Mouat "(Independent)". ST 12 May p1 a023: the four thank the electors
  as those "who stood as Labour candidates". Mouat's advert (28 Apr p1 a010): "Independent".

## 1951 (election 119)

- ST 27 Apr 1951 p1 a012: Blance and Morrison's address headed "SHETLAND LABOUR PARTY". Inkster
  and Ollason's joint address beside it has no party name; the paper calls them Independents on
  13 Apr (p4 a047) and "Moderates" on 4 May (p4 a039). Their "Moderate" is left for James (open
  question). Mouat: no advert found; left blank.

## 1952 (election 120)

- ST 2 May 1952 p5 a077: W. Anderson, Conochie, Peterson and A. H. Robertson, "having been
  nominated as Moderate candidates". The DB has all four as **Independent** (open question;
  corrections log). p8 a108: "four Moderate and four Socialist aspirants".
- ST 9 May 1952 p1 a009: thanks headed "LERWICK LABOUR PARTY" from Davidson, Johnson, More and
  Tait. Labour confirmed.

## 1953 (election 121)

- ST 1 May 1953 p4 a044: four "standing as a Labour team" (Anderson, Gair, Sandison, Strachan);
  H. Gray and Eunson "going forward together"; Burgess and Williamson "contesting the election
  independently". Eunson, a year later: "he stood as an Independent candidate" (ST 23 Apr 1954
  p5 a075). Gray, Burgess and Williamson's "Independent" is unconfirmed.

## 1954 (election 122)

- ST 23 Apr 1954 p4 a056: "four Socialists and four Independents". ST 30 Apr p5 a086: "The Lerwick
  Labour Party presents The Four Labour Candidates", with photographs of Blance, Morrison, Gair
  and Strachan (the OCR is garbled; names read from the page image). The Independents are the
  other four (`inferred`); Halcrow and Sutherland advertised together, as did Inkster and Ollason,
  with no party name.

## 1955 (election 123)

- ST 29 Apr 1955 p5 a090: Johnson, McKay, Nicolson and Tait "nominated by the Lerwick Labour
  Party" (for the Town and County Councils). p2 a165 (repeated on every page): "VOTE INDEPENDENT
  CONOCHIE X DANIEL X".

## 1956, 1957 (elections 125, 126): unopposed, no labels in the reports

## 1958 (election 127)

- ST 18 Apr 1958 p4 a056: "seven Independents and six Socialists" (A. C. Sandison later
  withdrew); the retiring Burgess and Daniel and "the five newcomers to the Independents' ranks"
  (Bennet, Eric Gray, Kerr, Johnston, Sandison).
- ST 25 Apr 1958 p3 a039: Johnston and Sandison: "We are Independents in the true sense"; the
  Shetland News had renamed the other five "Moderates".
- ST 2 May 1958 p5 a080: the Labour address, "A team selected and elected by the Lerwick Labour
  Party".

## 1959 (election 129)

- ST 1 May 1959 p8 a120: "the undersigned Labour candidates" Anderson, Nicolson, Johnson, McKay,
  Strachan. The other side (Conochie, H. Gray, Bennet) is only "the Independents" in the result
  (8 May p5 a058): unconfirmed.

## 1960 (election 131)

- ST 29 Apr 1960 p8 a125: "vote for the THREE INDEPENDENT CANDIDATES" Daniel, Eric Gray, Harry
  Gray. ST 13 May p1 a012: thanks from "the undersigned Labour Candidates".

## 1961 (election 132)

- ST 28 Apr 1961 p1 a125: "The Lerwick Labour Party have nominated candidates": Adair, Johnson,
  Johnston, Strachan. p1 a119: P. S. W. Ollason "As an independent candidate". ST 5 May p5 a066:
  "The bloc of four Independents ... won all four vacant seats".

## 1962 (election 134)

- ST 4 May 1962 p4 a086: result table tagging each candidate (Lab.) or (Ind.).

## 1963 (election 136)

- ST 3 May 1963 p8 a113: Adair and Morrison, "the undersigned Labour Party candidates". ST 10 May
  p8 a119: "Mr A. D. Bennet, standing as a Conservative". Gray, Shearer and Peterson unlabelled in
  the reports (unconfirmed).

## 1964 (election 137)

- ST 17 Apr 1964 p4 a054: Alger "to stand on his own as an Independent"; the "official"
  Independent group. ST 1 May p4 a087: "We are standing as members of the Labour Party"; p4 a188:
  Anderson, Halcrow, Robertson and Taylor, "be sure to vote Independent".

## 1965 (election 138): unopposed

ST 16 Apr 1965 p5 a056: the four retiring members, "three Labour and one Independent". Consistent
with the DB; no rows added.

## 1966 (election 139): unopposed after Adair withdrew

- ST 15 Apr 1966 p5 a079: "Messrs Adair and Morrison are Labour men, as is the fifth candidate
  to enter the fray, Mr John R. Smith". The DB has John Smith (ii) as **Independent** (open
  question; corrections log). Morrison confirmed.

## 1967 (election 140)

- ST 14 Apr 1967 p4 a059: Johnson "one of four Labour nominees", in a bloc with Laurenson, Leask
  and W. A. Smith; ST 28 Apr p3 a045: "The following Labour Party members". The six others
  aren't labelled in the reports (unconfirmed).

## 1968 (election 143)

- ST 3 May 1968 p5 a157: "The following Labour Party members" Adair, Leask, Paton, Thomson. ST 10
  May p4 a062: result table tagging each (Lab.) or (Ind.).

## 1969 (election 144)

- ST 2 May 1969 p3 a154: advert of "The Labour Group", Morrison, Paton, J. R. Smith, W. A. Smith.
  p2 a018: Furnival "as an Independent Candidate"; p2 a024: W. A. Moncrieff, "INDEPENDENT".

## 1970 (election 145)

- ST 17 Apr 1970 p1 a002: Blance and Taylor "seek re-election under the Independent label";
  Peterson and Carter "new candidates under the Independent tag"; "Labour have three candidates
  forward": Adair, Butler, Tait.

## 1971–1973 (elections 147–149): unopposed

ST 14 Apr 1972 p1 a001: Laurenson "has three times previously stood ... under the Labour ticket";
the council stays "six Labour, six Independent". No rows added; the DB labels are consistent.
