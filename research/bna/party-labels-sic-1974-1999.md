# Party labels: Shetland Islands Council, 1974–1999

Researched 2026-10-12 (queue: Labels 7). Method: a manifest sweep of the nominations, notice-of-poll
and result issues of each general, and of the issues either side of each by-election, reading the
OCR of every election heading and the candidates' adverts. From 1978 the notice of poll prints each
candidate's **description** (the ballot-paper label, if any), and from 1986 the paper printed the
full nominations list with descriptions, so most labels below are the candidate's own. The
Shetland Times in the BNA ends with 2000, so 2003 isn't covered. BNA link pattern:
`https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2FYYYYMMDD&page=NNNN&article=NNN`.

**Result.** 110 label rows: 18 new, 1 renamed (Tulloch 1980: "Shetland Ratepayers' Association"),
91 confirming the wiki. Five generals and one by-election had the wrong polling day
(`fix_newspapers.py` #84), and three candidates were misnamed (#85), one of them a wrong person:
Leonard Groat did not stand in Lerwick North Central in 1999; James Wiseman did. Four conflicts
are in open-questions. A DB "Independent" with no row here is the wiki's default, not seen in the
paper: about 30 candidates gave no description (listed under each year).

## 1974 (7 May; DB date right)

- ST 3 May 1974 p12 a305, advert: "LERWICK needs LABOUR representation TO JOIN ALEX MORRISON and
  WILLIAM SMITH ... BUTLER for BREIWICK PATON for TWAGEOS". **Labour (new): Morrison (Lerwick
  North), W. Smith (Lerwick West), Butler (Breiwick), Paton (Twageos).**
- ST 10 May 1974 p1 a004: "Only four of the candidates — all in Lerwick — carried an official
  Labour tag"; the contest "virtually non-political".
- ST 3 May 1974 p12 a307: Cumming, Breiwick, "I stand as an Independent". **Independent (new).**
- ST 3 May 1974 p12 a303: a Lerwick Central address, "always stood as an Independent Candidate",
  sixteen years on the Town and County Councils; the name is lost in the OCR. Eric Gray (Wishart
  was a newcomer). **Independent (new, inferred).**

## 1978 (2 May; DB date right)

- ST 14 Apr 1978 p1 a010: "three candidates in Lerwick mentioned in their papers their Labour Party
  associations, and one in Sound declared himself to be an Ind/Progressive."
- ST 21 Apr 1978 p12 a123–a124, notice of poll (2 May): W. A. Smith "Labour"; Butler "Shetland
  Labour Party"; Sutherland "Independent/Progressive". All three confirm the wiki. p13 a385,
  uncontested: Morrison, Lerwick North, "Member, Shetland Labour Party". **New.**
- ST 28 Apr 1978 p17 a372–a374 (page image), advert "Vote for the LABOUR GROUP": Butler, Breiwick;
  Christie, Harbour; Paton, Twageos; Smith, Central; Gair, Sound. **Labour (new): Christie,
  Paton, Gair.** p17 a371, Gair's own: "a member of a strong Labour Group ... VOTE GAIR — VOTE
  LABOUR".
- ST 28 Apr 1978 p17 a376: "Candidate — ALEX D. ARTHUR Party: INDEPENDENT." **New.**
- ST 5 May 1978 p1 a005, results: Cluness "one of three members of the 'Shetland Group' to stand
  in the election. The Shetland Group is the all-party enclave which favours increased autonomy";
  Playfair the second, Dr T. M. Y. Manson the third (lost Sandwick). **Shetland Group (new):
  Cluness, Playfair, Manson.** The same report: "five Lerwick candidates indicated at a late stage
  they were standing as a Labour group — three of them were returned" (only Smith and Butler of the
  advert's five won; the third is probably Morrison, unopposed).

## 1982 (Thursday 6 May; DB had Tuesday 4th)

- ST 16 Apr 1982 p1 a005 "Scant interest in SIC elections": "the Shetland Islands Council's
  elections on 6th May". "Although no candidate has stated on his nomination papers that he is
  standing as a member of the Shetland Movement, about half of the new council will be Shetland
  Movement members"; Graham "a leading member". "Only sitting members Mr W. A. Smith and Mr Chris
  Dowle have declared themselves as Labour candidates, and Mr R. R. Tulloch ... on behalf of
  Shetland Ratepayers' Association." Confirms all three.
- ST 7 May 1982 p1 a011 (Friday): "polls closed last night". **Date: 6 May** (#84).
- ST 23 Apr 1982 p19 a252: the Shetland Movement lists 23 of 37 candidates who "indicated their
  support for the Manifesto". Support, not a label: not used.
- ST 23 Apr 1982 p12 a229, notice of poll: no party descriptions.
- ST 30 Apr 1982 p7 a048 (page image), advert: "VOTE LABOUR — Vote DOWLE and GROAT" ("Don't vote
  for yet another Shetland Movement sympathiser"). **Groat, Lerwick North: Labour (new).**
- Graham's DB label "Shetland Movement": cleared, James 2026-10-13 "trust the paper" (#86). Delting South Hunter,
  Nesting Hunter, Yell North Johnson "Independent" and Scalloway Anderson "None" are the wiki's.
  Robert Rae Tulloch's 1982 candidacy is unlinked, though the 1980 one is linked to
  `robert-tulloch`.

## 1986 (Thursday 8 May; DB had Sunday 4th)

- ST 18 Apr 1986 p1 a001: "14 face an election on Thursday, 8th May"; "Only seven of the 48
  candidates are standing under the Shetland Movement banner"; Thomason "described himself only as
  a member of the movement, not a movement candidate"; Labour has five. **Date: 8 May** (#84).
- ST 18 Apr 1986 p3 a264, the full list with descriptions. Confirmed: Shetland Movement: Graham,
  T. M. Y. Manson, Cluness, Ellis, Walterson, Cumming, Bray; Labour Party: Groat, W. Smith, J. J.

  Whitehead "Independent Liberal". Thomason "Member, Shetland Movement": kept as Shetland Movement

- No description: Byrne, C. B. Eunson, R. Tait, Fraser, D. Johnson, R. S. Gray, P. Hunter, Irvine,
