# LTC 1936–1945: Halcrow's death, Clausen's resignation, and the rows holding 1936–38 open

Researched 2026-09-28 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times). Every link opens the
BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** in the toolbar for the OCR text.

Question: the Nov 1936, Oct 1937 and Jul 1938 size-short rows were already known to be genuine
vacancies (`ltc-1934-1938.md`), but stayed on /data-review because rows sitting through them were
unconfirmed: Adam Halcrow (i) 1936, Clausen 1937, Dalziel's Feb 1938 co-option, W. G. Smith
(iii) 1937 and James Laing 1937. Check each one's ends.

## Evidence

### 1. Captain Adam Halcrow's death, Dec 1940
- **Shetland Times, Sat 28 Dec 1940, p4 (art. 062)**: "Death of Captain A. Halcrow. Sudden
  passing." He died at home "late on Christmas Eve" (he had been out as usual on Tuesday), and was
  then a member of Lerwick Town Council, the County Council and the Harbour Trust.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19401228&page=0004&article=062
- Ledger: `adam-halcrow-i` 1936-11-03 confirmed (end 1940-12-24 unchanged).

### 2. Williamson co-opted for Halcrow, Jan 1941
- **Shetland Times, Sat 11 Jan 1941, p5 (art. 048)**: "Tribute to Late Captain Halcrow.
  Co-Option of Mr. John A. Williamson", at the Council's "meeting on Tuesday" (7 Jan 1941), Bailie
  Laing presiding.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19410111&page=0005&article=048
- **Same issue, p5 (art. 054)**: "Member co-opted": Williamson was "the next highest on the list of
  candidates unsuccessful at last election".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19410111&page=0005&article=054
- Ledger: `john-williamson-iii` 1941-01-07 start sourced; end (1945-11-05) not checked, so left
  `confirmed=0`.

### 3. Clausen elected Nov 1937 and resigned Jan 1938
- **Shetland Times, Sat 6 Nov 1937, p4 (art. 072)**: "The result" of the 2 Nov 1937 poll (six
  seats): James Laing, J. J. Aitken, Wm. Anderson, J. L. Linklater, a fifth name the OCR garbles
  (the wiki has W. G. Smith), E. J. F. Clausen, then Dalziel and J. A. Williamson. Only the
  snippet was read, not the table itself; the order matches the wiki and Dalziel and Williamson
  being the next two co-opted in 1938.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19371106&page=0004&article=072
- **Shetland Times, Sat 1 Jan 1938, p4 (art. 080)**: Mr and Mrs Clausen to live in Thurso on his
  appointment as superintendent for Wick, Thurso, Orkney and Shetland.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19380101&page=0004&article=080
- **Shetland Times, Sat 8 Jan 1938, p4 (art. 076)**: at the monthly meeting "on Tuesday evening"
  (4 Jan 1938), a letter from Clausen, "as he had taken up duty in the South", resigning. Provost
  Smith said they were sorry to lose him.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19380108&page=0004&article=076
- Ledger: `erling-clausen` 1937-11-02 now ends **1938-01-04**, `resigned` (was 1938-02-03,
  `replaced`), confirmed. The letter's own date isn't given; 4 Jan is the meeting it was read at.

### 4. Dalziel co-opted for Clausen, Feb 1938
- **Shetland Times, Sat 5 Feb 1938, p4 (art. 077)**: "Member co-opted": "in place of Mr E. J. F.
  Clausen, resigned", Captain Halcrow moved and Bailie Laing seconded Peter Dalziel; unanimous. The
  report opens with the monthly meeting held "on Tuesday evening", i.e. **Tue 1 Feb 1938**, not
  Thu 3 Feb.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19380205&page=0004&article=077
- Ledger: `peter-dalziel` 1938-02-03 → **1938-02-01**, confirmed (end at the 1 Nov 1938 general
  is already sourced from the ST preview). Election 100 moved (`fix_newspapers.py` #18).

### 5. W. G. Smith's death, Feb 1942
- **Shetland Times, Sat 7 Mar 1942, p2 (art. 033)**: "Junior Bailie of Lerwick": Bailie William
  G. Smith died "on Saturday of last week" (28 Feb 1942) in hospital in Glasgow, aged 44.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19420307&page=0002&article=033
- Ledger: `william-smith-iii` 1937-11-02 confirmed (end 1942-02-28 unchanged).

### 6. James Laing's retiral, Nov 1945, and Johnston's co-option
- **Shetland Times, Fri 16 Nov 1945, p3 (art. 076)**: "Resignation": a letter from Councillor
  Laing intimating his retiral, which "will come into force on the 29th of November"; Provost
  Shearer said he had been in poor health.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19451116&page=0003&article=076
- **Shetland Times, Fri 14 Dec 1945, p3 (art. 076)**: "Ex-Provost Laing's seat filled": Arthur
  Johnston, highest of the unsuccessful candidates at the recent election, chosen at the meeting
  "on Monday evening" (10 Dec 1945).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19451214&page=0003&article=076
- Ledger: `james-laing` 1937-11-02 now ends **1945-11-29**, `resigned` (was 1945-12-10,
  `replaced`), confirmed. `arthur-johnson` 1945-12-10 confirmed (end already sourced from ST
  4 Oct 1946).
