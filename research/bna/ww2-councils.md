# WW2 council sweep, 1939–45: follow-ups

Researched 2026-09-29 in the British Newspaper Archive, from the meeting-by-meeting sweep of
Lerwick Town Council and Zetland County Council, 3 Sep 1939 – 15 Aug 1945 (published as the
"Shetland's Councils in the Second World War" artifact). Every link opens the BNA viewer; click
**Articles** for the OCR text.

## 1. John Gear left for Lyness in July 1942 but resigned in February 1943

- **ST Sat 11 Jul 1942, p2 (art. 024 and 027)**: the report of "the monthly meeting ... on
  Tuesday evening" (7 Jul) is headed "Resignation of Councillor John Gear", but the body is only
  "CONGRATULATIONS TO COUNCILLOR GEAR" on his appointment as postmaster at Lyness. The Provost was
  sorry "it might take him away from us for a time"; Gear said he was "very sorry indeed to be
  breaking my connection" with the Council. No letter was read and nothing was accepted.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19420711&page=2&article=027
- **ST Sat 6 Feb 1943, p2 (art. 057)**: the Clerk read his letter, "now postmaster at Lyness",
  tendering his resignation; the Provost said there was "nothing left for them but to accept" it,
  as he was living away and could no longer attend (already in `ltc-1940-1945.md` §6).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19430206&page=2&article=057
- He isn't in any Town Council attendance list after 7 Jul 1942, and ST 26 Dec 1942 fills his
  place on the County Roads Committee.
- **Decision**: the ledger's end of **2 Feb 1943** stands. The ledger dates a resignation to the
  letter or its acceptance, and in July he only said goodbye (the Provost expected him back).
  The seven months he held the seat while away go in the source. No ledger edit.

## 2. Henry Mouat died on Saturday 20 May 1944, not 20 March

- **Shetland News, Thu 25 May 1944, p2 (art. 019)**: "DEATH OF MR HENRY MOUAT", ex-County Convener
  and ex-chairman of the Harbour Trust: he "died early on Saturday morning" at Hamarslea, Lerwick,
  in his 69th year; funeral on the Wednesday (24 May).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0003210%2F19440525&page=2&article=019
- The Shetland Times obituary is 26 May 1944 (p2 art. 026); the Town Council's tribute was at its
  meeting of 6 Jun 1944; the Harbour Trust's in ST 16 Jun 1944 (p3 art. 043).
- Edit: `henry-mouat` died_date 1944-03-20 → **1944-05-20** (`fix_newspapers.py` #67). His County
  seat had ended in 1938, so no council term changes.

## 3. Provost James Smith (i)'s end, June 1941

- **ST Sat 7 Jun 1941, p2 (art. 070)** (already cited): his letter resigning as Councillor and
  Provost "three weeks from this date" was read and accepted on Tue 3 Jun 1941. The acting
  chairman had it by the night before (Mon 2 Jun). The Shetland News report (Thu 5 Jun 1941, p3
  art. 038) is too garbled to give the letter's date.
- Precedent: his first resignation, read on Tue 3 Oct 1939, used the same words and was withdrawn
  on 7 Nov 1939 (ST 7 Oct 1939 p4 art. 091; ST 11 Nov 1939 p4 art. 080).
- Edit (James's call, 2026-09-29): `james-smith-i` 1934-11-06 ends **1941-06-23** (was
  1941-07-01), three weeks from a letter dated by 2 Jun. Stays confirmed; the citation link is
  marked `inferred`. Johnson's co-option on 1 Jul is unchanged, so the seat was empty 23 Jun–1 Jul.

## 4. "J. Jamieson" at the County Council, 21 Aug 1945

- **ST Fri 24 Aug 1945, p3 (art. 059)**: the attendance at the August meeting (Tue 21 Aug) ends
  "... L. Laurenson, J. Jamieson and W. Henry".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19450824&page=3&article=059
- The only Jamieson sitting then was **James A. Jamieson (`james-jamieson-ii`)**, Sandness member
  from 1932, re-elected Dec 1938 and Dec 1945. `john-jamieson` wasn't on the Council until the Dec
  1945 general (Whalsay and Skerries), and Whalsay's wartime member, R. Ollason, is in the same
  list. J. A. Jamieson was still living in the district (secretary of the Walls, Sandness etc.
  Nursing Association, ST 21 Jun 1941 p1 art. 105).
- He isn't in the wartime attendance lists read for the sweep (1939–45), which suggests he was
  away for most of the war; not checked. **No missing co-option**: the "J. Jamieson" of 1945 is
  the sitting Sandness member. No edit.
