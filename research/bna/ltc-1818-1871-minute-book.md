first Lerwick Town Council minute book (1818–1877), transcribed at

## 1823: result not in the book

p27 opens the minute of the 4 Sep 1823 election and p28 lists the burgesses present, then the
page stops. p29's top third is ruled but blank (transcript note): the result was never entered.
The first meeting since the election (29 Oct 1823, p29) has Bailies Ogilvy and Duncan and
Councillors W. Spence, Hay, Deans, Greig, Gauden and Paterson, who "accept of their respective
offices". Angus sat on 31 Oct 1823 (p30) and Councillor Ogilvy (James; Charles was Bailie) on
22 Oct 1824 (p31). Andrew Grierson appears only as a Town Trustee (p29, p31).

- 10 of the 11 rows now cite p27 (`inferred`: the meeting happened, result not recorded) and the
  meeting where they sat (`read`).
- **Balfour Spence** (1823 row) is not seen at any meeting from Oct 1823 to Sep 1826 (p29–33).
  His row is left as it was, in open-questions.

## 1844: Gifford of Busta

The burgesses elected Arthur Gifford of Busta Senior Bailie on 5 Sep 1844 (p105). At the meeting
of 27 Sep 1844 (p107) Bailie Leask read Gifford's letter: he "could not accept the office of chief
Magistrate". A new election was deferred to take counsel's opinion. The ledger's 10 rows for 1844
are right (Gifford never sat); the DB has no candidacy for him. In open-questions.
