# LTC 1894–1910: results, retiring lists, and the 1906 Provost rule

Researched 2026-09-28 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times). Every link opens the
BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** in the toolbar for the OCR text.

Question: confirm the unconfirmed rows sitting through the four LTC size-short windows of
1895–1905 (queue Batch 1): Garriock's 1894 seat, Stove's 1898 seat, and the 1899–1904 cohorts.
Also John Irvine (iii)'s 1909 departure. Earlier retiring lists (1895–1901) are in
`ltc-1895-1901.md`.

## Evidence

### 1. November 1894: Garriock the only new member
- **Shetland Times, Sat 10 Nov 1894, p2 (art. 040)**: "MUNICIPAL ELECTION": the official figures
  for Goudie, Garriock, Leisk, Laing and Sutherland.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18941110&page=0002&article=040
- **Same issue, p2 (art. 030)**: leader: "Mr Garriock is the only new member".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18941110&page=0002&article=030
- **Ledger**: `archibald-garriock` 1894-11-06 confirmed (end from the 1897 list, `ltc-1895-1901.md` §3).

### 2. November 1898: unopposed
- **Shetland Times, Sat 29 Oct 1898, p4 (art. 055)**: the four retiring (Leisk, Harrison, Kay,
  Stove) were nominated "and no more", so "the Council therefore consists of the former members".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18981029&page=0004&article=055
- **Shetland Times, Sat 5 Nov 1898, p4 (art. 052)**: on Thursday "the four newly-elected
  Councillors" took the oath before Bailie Porteous.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18981105&page=0004&article=052

### 3. November 1899: Morrison for Hunter
- **Shetland Times, Sat 11 Nov 1899, p5 (art. 117)**: "the five vacancies" polled on Tuesday;
  result John Robertson, D. R. Morrison, P. S. Goodlad, A. A. Porteous (59), S. Johnson, then
  D. Sutherland. "The whole of the old members ... returned", Morrison taking Hunter's seat.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18991111&page=0005&article=117

### 4. November 1900: unopposed
- **Shetland Times, Sat 3 Nov 1900, p4 (art. 088)**: "The four Councillors presently retiring
  having been nominated again ... there will be no contest" (Goudie, Kay, Halcrow, Laing; see the
  13 Oct 1900 list in `ltc-1895-1901.md` §6).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19001103&page=0004&article=088

### 5. October–November 1901: Stove's death, and the poll on Tuesday 5 November
- **Shetland Times, Sat 19 Oct 1901, p5 (art. 044)**: "Death of Mr Alfred S. Stove": he died "On
  Sunday morning last" (13 Oct). Also the County Council tribute (art. 041).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19011019&page=0005&article=044
- **Shetland Times, Sat 2 Nov 1901, p1 (art. 009)**: Burgh notice of the candidates "at the
  Municipal Election on TUESDAY NEXT", i.e. 5 Nov. The DB had Fri 1 Nov.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19011102&page=0001&article=009
- **Shetland Times, Sat 9 Nov 1901, p4 (art. 083)**: "Municipal Election" and "THE RESULT": the
  three retiring (Harrison, Johnson, Leisk) plus John G. Irvine, put forward "with a view to
  filling the vacancy" of the late Mr Stove; James Robertson also stood, forcing a poll. Voting
  began on Tuesday morning; result Irvine, Harrison, Johnson, Leisk elected, Robertson 130.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19011109&page=0004&article=083
- **Ledger**: the 1898/1899 rows ending and the four 1901 rows starting on 1901-11-01 moved to
  1901-11-05 (`fix_newspapers.py` #21 for election 54); `alfred-stove` 1898-11-01 confirmed
  (death 13 Oct 1901).

### 6. October–November 1902: unopposed
- **Shetland Times, Sat 11 Oct 1902, p4 (art. 069)**: "RETIRING COUNCILLORS": Bailie Porteous,
  Councillors Robertson, Morrison and Goodlad.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19021011&page=0004&article=069
- **Shetland Times, Sat 8 Nov 1902, p4 (art. 078)**: the four retiring councillors, with no other
  candidates, were on Tuesday "duly declared elected".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19021108&page=0004&article=078
- **Ledger**: the 1899 cohort (Porteous, Morrison, Robertson, Goodlad) confirmed to 1902.

### 7. October–November 1903: Halcrow defeated, Ganson in
- **Shetland Times, Sat 10 Oct 1903, p5 (art. 067)**: retiring: "Bailie Goudie, and Councillors
  Halcrow, Kay, and Laing".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19031010&page=0005&article=067
- **Shetland Times, Sat 7 Nov 1903, p4 (art. 058)**: the election "took place on Tuesday last"
  (3 Nov). Page 1 (art. 008, 010): thanks from Ganson ("next the highest successful"), Kay, Laing
  and the defeated James Robertson.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19031107&page=0004&article=058
- **Ledger**: the 1900 cohort confirmed to 1903.

### 8. November 1904: Leisk withdraws, J. T. J. Sinclair in
- **Shetland Times, Sat 5 Nov 1904, p4 (art. 112)**: the election on Tuesday filled the vacancies
  "caused by the retiral of Provost Leisk and Councillors Gilbert Harrison, J. G. Irvine and
  Sinclair Johnson"; Leisk withdrew after nomination; six candidates.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19041105&page=0004&article=112
- **Ledger**: the 1901 cohort confirmed to 1904.

### 9. February 1905: John Robertson dies
- **Shetland Times, Sat 4 Feb 1905, p4 (art. 044)**: his death "in the early hours of Thursday"
  (2 Feb) at Victoria Wharf.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19050204&page=0004&article=044
- **Shetland Times, Sat 11 Feb 1905, p5 (art. 057)**: Provost Goudie's tribute at the council.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19050211&page=0005&article=057
- **Ledger**: `john-robertson-ii` 1902-11-04 confirmed (ends 1905-02-02).

### 10. October 1905
- **Shetland Times, Sat 14 Oct 1905, p4 (art. 047)**: retiring: "Bailie A. A. Porteous,
  Councillors P. S. Goodlad, D. R. Morrison, and L. Laurenson" (Laurenson in Robertson's seat).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19051014&page=0004&article=047
- **Ledger**: the 1902 cohort (Porteous, Morrison, Goodlad) confirmed to 1905.

### 11. October 1906: Provost Goudie stays on, J. T. J. Sinclair retires
- **Shetland Times, Sat 6 Oct 1906, p5 (art. 125)**: retiring: Bailie Laing, Ganson, Kay and
  J. T. J. Sinclair. "It was Provost Goudie who should have retired, but on his election to
  Provost, he had to serve a period of three years", so Sinclair retired, having had "the smallest
  number of votes" in 1904.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19061006&page=0005&article=125
- This is the Clerk stating the "Provost stays on" rule outright: a Provost served three years
  from his election as Provost, and the lowest winner of the next cohort retired in his place.
- **Ledger**: `james-goudie` 1903-11-03 now ends 1907-11-05; `john-sinclair` 1904-11-01 now ends
  1906-11-06 (the draft had them the other way round). Laing, Kay, Ganson confirmed to 1906.

### 12. October 1907: Goudie retires
- **Shetland Times, Sat 5 Oct 1907, p5 (art. 113)**: retiring in November: "Provost Goudie,
  Councillors Irvine, Harrison, and Sinclair Johnson".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19071005&page=0005&article=113
- **Shetland Times, Sat 12 Oct 1907, p4 (art. 094)**: Provost Goudie's "decision to retire into
  private life".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19071012&page=0004&article=094
- **Ledger**: the 1904 cohort (Harrison, Irvine, Johnson) confirmed to 1907, and Goudie's 1903 row.

### 13. December 1909–January 1910: Irvine resigns, Loggie appointed
- **Shetland Times, Sat 11 Dec 1909, p4 (art. 078)**: "RESIGNATION OF MR J. G. IRVINE": his
  letter read; the Provost said he had sat since 1901. The report is of the meeting "on Tuesday
  night" (art. 073), i.e. 7 Dec 1909.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19091211&page=0004&article=078
- **Shetland Times, Sat 8 Jan 1910, p4 (art. 071)**: a special meeting "held on Tuesday night,
  before the ordinary meeting" (4 Jan) unanimously appointed James A. Loggie in place of Irvine,
  resigned.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19100108&page=0004&article=071
- **Ledger**: `john-irvine-iii` 1907-11-05 ends 1909-12-07 (resigned), confirmed; `james-loggie`
  starts 1910-01-04 (`fix_newspapers.py` #22 for election 64).

## Result

term_issues 16 → 12: the 1895, 1899, 1901 and 1905 size-short rows are hidden, all genuine
vacancies (Charles Robertson's and Garriock's deaths, Stove's death before the 1901 general,
Robertson's death before the Feb 1905 by-election). The new Dec 1909–Jan 1910 vacancy is hidden
too, since every row sitting through it is confirmed. 28 rows edited (27 newly confirmed).

Left unconfirmed: Charles Robertson (1893, died 21 Jul 1895) and Garriock (1897, died 28 Feb
1899) death-ended rows, Johnson's May 1899 and Laurenson's Feb 1905 by-election start days. None
sits through a window.
