# LTC 1945–1956: the last unconfirmed rows in the short windows

Researched 2026-09-28 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times, published on Fridays in
these years). Every link opens the BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** in the toolbar
for the OCR text. Queue: Batch 2.

## Evidence

### 1. David Gray (ii): elected 6 Nov 1945, died Mon 4 Mar 1946
- **ST Fri 9 Nov 1945, p2 (art. 047)**: "The first six names are the successful candidates". The
  OCR of the table is garbled, but the order is Peterson, Gray ("GRAN"), Ollason, Smith, Anderson
  904, Morrison 903, matching the DB (Gray 1180, second).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19451109&page=0002&article=047
- **ST Fri 8 Mar 1946, p2 (art. 016)**: the council adjourned at its monthly meeting "on Tuesday"
  (5 Mar) in respect to the late Councillor David Gray, "whose passing occurred on Monday evening
  of this week" (4 Mar). Tribute in art. 017; public funeral notice p1 art. 069.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19460308&page=0002&article=016
