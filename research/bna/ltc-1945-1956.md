### 3. James Brownlie: elected 5 Nov 1946, resigned 13 Sep 1949
- Start: third in the Nov 1946 result, 829 votes (ST 8 Nov 1946, p4 art. 061; see 2).
- **ST Fri 16 Sep 1949, p5 (art. 055)**: "Politics Laid Aside for Co-Option". Johnson was invited
  to fill the vacancy "caused by the resignation of Mr James H. Brownlie". His letter, read at the
  same meeting, said he had been "granted six months' leave of absence earlier this year" when
  ill, and was now being transferred south. So the resignation and the co-option were the same
  meeting (Tue 13 Sep 1949, `ltc-election-dates-1949-1964.md`), and the council was never short.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19490916&page=0005&article=055
- Edit: `james-brownlie` 1946-11-05 → 1949-09-13 confirmed, end reason `replaced` → `resigned`.
  Clears the 3 Jun–1 Jul 1947 row (Shearer's dated vacancy): 11 → 10.
