# William Tulloch, German Vice-Consul at Lerwick

Researched 2026-10-13 in the British Newspaper Archive, prompted by an email from a provenance
researcher pointing out his Order of the German Eagle (Third Class), gazetted February 1939.
The biography paragraphs are `fix_newspapers.py` #87.

Findings:
- **Two commissions.** First commissioned by Kaiser Wilhelm at Potsdam on Christmas Day 1905
  (ST 1938) or 1904 (obituary, ST 1943); held to 4 Aug 1914. Re-appointed: reported ST 6 Dec 1924,
  though the 1938 article says 1925.
- **The award** was reported in Lerwick in December 1938, as a local honour with no comment
  against it, and listed in the paper's review of the year next to J. W. Robertson's Danish
  knighthood.
- **Harbour Trust**: he was clerk and treasurer 1915–27, but chairman (from 1938) when decorated.
  The intro's "clerk of the Harbour Trust" is true but undated.
- **Not found**: anything on the consulate at the outbreak of war in Sept 1939. Searched
  `german consulate` (ST 1939–40) and `tulloch consul` (SN Jun 1938–1943).
- **Open question:** the obituary gives his birth as 21 Sept 1858; the DB has 2 Sept 1858
  (from the wiki). James: trust Bayanne, so 2 Sept stays.

### Articles
- **Shetland Times, Sat 6 Dec 1924, p4 (art. 057)**: Local and District News: appointed German
  Vice-Consul; the post "vacant since 1914", and he had held it for seven years before.
  [viewer](https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19241206&page=0004&article=057)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 24 Jun 1933, p4 (art. 072)**: a German fishery cruiser's commander pays
  an official call on the Vice-Consul.
  [viewer](https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19330624&page=0004&article=072)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 11 Jul 1936, p4 (art. 096)**: "Death of a German fisherman": Hermann
  Sikkes, of the *Alma* of Leer, died at the Isolation Hospital; at the funeral (Fri 3 Jul) the
  Vice-Consul attended and "the coffin was covered with the Nazi flag".
  [viewer](https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19360711&page=0004&article=096)
- **Shetland News, Thu 1 Dec 1938, p4 (art. 025)**: "German Consular Honour for Mr W. A. A.
  Tulloch".
  [viewer](https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0003210%2F19381201&page=0004&article=025)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 3 Dec 1938, p4 (art. 089)**: "High Honour for Mr W. A. A. Tulloch":
  the German Eagle, Grade III, from Hitler; "the first occasion" it went to a Vice-Consul; the
  1905 commission and 1914 closure; addressed as chairman of the Harbour Trust.
  [viewer](https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19381203&page=0004&article=089)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 31 Dec 1938, p5 (art. 101)**: review of the year lists the award among
  "honours conferred on Shetlanders".
  [viewer](https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19381231&page=0005&article=101)
- **Shetland Times, Fri 9 Jul 1943, p4 (art. 094)**: "W. A. A. Tulloch dies: 42 years public
  service": Harbour Trust offices by year, School Board 1897–1906, Widows' Homes 38 years, the 1904
  commission, survived by his sister Kate; born "21st September, 1858".
  [viewer](https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19430709&page=0004&article=094)
- **Shetland Times, Fri 16 Jul 1943, p2 (art. 026)**: death notice, at Leog, 2 Jul 1943.
  [viewer](https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19430716&page=0002&article=026)
