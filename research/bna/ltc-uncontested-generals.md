# LTC generals with no votes in the wiki, 1900–1966

Queue item: after May 1954 turned out to be a poll, check every LTC general since 1900 that the
wiki shows as unopposed. Checked 2026-09-28 in the Shetland Times. BNA viewer links below.

**Result: all twelve were unopposed. The wiki is right.** No ledger or correction edits. These
articles confirm the start of each winner's term. They don't confirm the end, so no row is
newly `confirmed=1` (see the open question on confirming rows with one end sourced).

## Evidence

### November 1900
- **ST Sat 3 Nov 1900, p4 (art. 088)**: "Municipal Election". The four retiring councillors were
  re-nominated and there were no fresh candidates, so "there will be no contest". It names Goudie,
  F. H. Halcrow, G. G. Kay and A. L. Laing, with their proposers and seconders.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19001103&page=0004&article=088

### November 1902
- **ST Sat 8 Nov 1902, p4 (art. 078)**: Local and District News. With no additional candidates
  nominated, the retiring members were "duly declared elected" on the Tuesday: Bailie Porteous,
  Goodlad, Morrison and Robertson.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19021108&page=0004&article=078
- **ST Sat 15 Nov 1902, p5 (art. 111)**: the Clerk "intimated the re-election of the four
  retiring Councillors".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19021115&page=0005&article=111

### November 1911
- **ST Sat 28 Oct 1911, p4 (art. 075)**: nominations on Tuesday first for the seats of Bailie
  Goodlad and Councillors Ganson, Ratter and Sinclair, who were retiring.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19111028&page=0004&article=075
- **ST Sat 4 Nov 1911, p3 (art. 113)**: "only the four retiring candidates had been nominated".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19111104&page=0003&article=113
- ST 11 Nov 1911 p4 (art. 094): all four sworn in.

### November 1923
- **ST Sat 3 Nov 1923, p4 (art. 062)**: the four retiring members were re-nominated, with no
  other nominations. (The 27 Nov 1923 poll in the adverts is the Temperance Act poll, not the
  council election.)
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19231103&page=0004&article=062

### November 1924
- **ST Sat 25 Oct 1924, p4 (art. 060)**: nominations "to-day (Friday)". No new candidates had
  been mentioned, and several men who were approached declined.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19241025&page=0004&article=060
- **ST Sat 1 Nov 1924, p4 (art. 091)**: Bruce, Ollason, J. T. J. Sinclair and E. S. Reid Tait were
  nominated. With no new candidates, the four were "declared elected".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19241101&page=0004&article=091

### November 1927
- **ST Sat 22 Oct 1927, p4 (art. 061)**: a letter to the editor complains that "we are to have no
  contest this year".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19271022&page=0004&article=061
- **ST Sat 29 Oct 1927, p4 (art. 073)**: "Retiring members re-nominated. No poll."
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19271029&page=0004&article=073

### November 1928
- **ST Sat 3 Nov 1928, p5 (art. 090)**: "Retiring town councillors re-nominated". No poll for
  the four vacancies.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19281103&page=0005&article=090

### November 1930
- **ST Sat 1 Nov 1930, p4 (art. 070)**: "No poll this year. Four retiring members re-nominated."
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19301101&page=0004&article=070

### November 1931
- **ST Sat 24 Oct 1931, p4 (art. 056)**: those retiring were Bailie John Campbell and
  Councillors M. L. Manson, G. H. Duffin and [Cogle; the snippet is cut off].
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19311024&page=0004&article=056
- **ST Sat 31 Oct 1931, p4 (art. 097)**: "No contest. Retiring councillors nominated."
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19311031&page=0004&article=097

### May 1956
- **ST Fri 13 Apr 1956, p4 (art. 064)**: "No municipal election this year": the first since the
  war. Nominations closed on the Tuesday.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19560413&page=0004&article=064

### May 1965
- **ST Fri 16 Apr 1965, p5 (art. 056)**: "No municipal election", the first for eight years.
  When nominations closed on the Tuesday, only the four retiring members had been nominated.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19650416&page=0005&article=056

### May 1966
- **ST Fri 15 Apr 1966, p5 (art. 079)**: "Only one new council aspirant". There were five
  candidates for four seats: the retiring Senior Bailie Eric Gray, Junior Bailie Robert Adair,
  Dean of Guild Alex. Morrison and Magnus Shearer, plus John R. Smith (Labour, county councillor
  for Bressay). A poll was set for 3 May.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19660415&page=0005&article=079
- **ST Fri 22 Apr 1966, p4 (art. 060)**: "No election". Junior Bailie Adair (Labour) "decided not
  to seek re-election", so the four remaining candidates were returned unopposed.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19660422&page=0004&article=060

This fits the ledger. Adair's 1963 seat ends in 1966, and he came back by co-option in May 1970
in J. R. Smith's place (`ltc-1969-1973.md`). The wiki records 1966 correctly as unopposed; it
doesn't mention the withdrawal.
