# LTC 1915–1916: wartime deaths, resignations and co-options

Researched 2026-09-28 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times, and the Shetland News where
the Times is missing). Every link opens the BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** in the
toolbar for the OCR text.

Question: the three size-short rows (10–27 Apr 1915, 3 Jul–3 Aug 1915, 14 Jul–1 Aug 1916). Are they
genuine vacancies, and can every row sitting through them be confirmed?

**Gap in the Shetland Times:** nothing is digitised between Sat 17 Apr and Sat 19 Jun 1915. The
Shetland News (`BL/0003210`) covers it.

## Evidence

### 1. Provost Robert Stout died Sat 10 Apr 1915
- **Shetland Times, Sat 17 Apr 1915, p4 (art. 086)**: "Death of Provost Stout". As announced in
  the special edition of Saturday last, he "died with startling suddenness".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19150417&page=0004&article=086
- Ledger: `robert-stout-i` 1912-11-05, end 1915-04-10 `died` (unchanged), confirmed.

### 2. J. T. J. Sinclair elected at the special meeting of Tue 27 Apr 1915
- **Shetland News, Sat 1 May 1915, p4 (art. 038)**: special meeting "on Tuesday evening" to appoint
  a new Provost, Junior Bailie and a member for Stout's vacancy. Sinclair (proposed, seconded by
  W. S. Smith) beat David Sutherland "by six votes against two". Goodlad was made Provost.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0003210%2F19150501&page=0004&article=038
- Matches the wiki (Tuesday 27 April, 6–2). Ledger: `john-sinclair` 1915-04-27 confirmed.

### 3. James C. Grierson died Sat 3 Jul 1915
- **Shetland Times, Sat 10 Jul 1915, p4 (art. 074)**, Deaths: at Helendale, Lerwick, on the 3rd.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19150710&page=0004&article=074
- **Same issue, p4 (art. 079)**: monthly meeting (Tue 6 Jul 1915). Present: Provost Goodlad, Bailies
  Ganson and Ratter, Councillors J. T. J. Sinclair, J. Laing, J. Smith, W. Sinclair. The Provost
  refers to "the death of Councillor Grierson".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19150710&page=0004&article=079
- Ledger: `james-grierson` 1912-11-05, end 1915-07-03 `died` (unchanged), confirmed.

### 4. Charles B. Stout elected Tue 3 Aug 1915
- **Shetland Times, Sat 7 Aug 1915, p4 (art. 093 and "Filling a Vacancy", art. 096)**: monthly
  meeting "on Tuesday evening". Proposed by J. Smith and seconded by Bailie Ganson for Grierson's
  vacancy; "no other proposal", so declared elected.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19150807&page=0004&article=093
- Ledger: `charles-stout` 1915-08-03 confirmed.

### 5. Laurence Laurenson (ii) died Fri 14 Jul 1916
- **Shetland Times, Sat 15 Jul 1916, p4 (art. 082)**, Deaths: at Windhouse, Lerwick, at noon on
  Friday 14th, Captain Laurence Laurenson.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19160715&page=0004&article=082
- Ledger: `laurence-laurenson-ii` 1913-11-04, end 1916-07-14 `died` (unchanged), confirmed.

### 6. J. Percy Henderson appointed Tue 1 Aug 1916
- **Shetland Times, Sat 5 Aug 1916, p5 (art. 107, "Appointment of New Member")**: monthly meeting
  "on Tuesday evening". Bailie Ganson moved Henderson for Laurenson's seat; W. Sinclair moved Magnus
  Manson; Henderson won "by a majority". The Provost and Ganson promised Manson "the next vacancy".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19160805&page=0005&article=107
- Ledger: `john-henderson` 1916-08-01 confirmed.

### 7. Andrew Smith resigned; M. L. Manson co-opted Tue 5 Sep 1916
- **Shetland Times, Sat 9 Sep 1916, p4 (art. 075)**, Local News, "Resignation of a Town
  Councillor": Andrew Smith of Islesburgh House resigned; through failing health he had not
  attended recently. "On Tuesday evening" Magnus L. Manson was co-opted in his room.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19160909&page=0004&article=075
- **Same issue, p5 (art. 093 area)**: monthly meeting (Tue 5 Sep 1916), present includes Ramsay.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19160909&page=0005&article=093
- The day of the resignation isn't printed. It came after 1 Aug (when the Provost spoke of Manson
  getting "the next vacancy"). Ledger: `andrew-smith` 1913-11-04, end 1916-09-05, `end_reason`
  `replaced` → `resigned`, confirmed.

### 8. W. Spence Smith resigned Tue 7 Nov 1916 (not 5 Dec)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 11 Nov 1916, p5 (art. 098, "Resignation of a Councillor")**: at the
  monthly meeting (Tuesday evening, 7 Nov) a letter was read from W. Spence Smith, Northness,
  resigning because of his health after nine years; accepted with regret.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19161111&page=0005&article=098
- Ledger: `william-smith-ii` 1913-11-04, end 1916-12-05 → **1916-11-07** `resigned`, confirmed. The
  new 7 Nov–5 Dec 1916 vacancy is fully confirmed, so it doesn't show on /data-review.

### 9. H. J. Robertson appointed Tue 5 Dec 1916
- **Shetland Times, Sat 9 Dec 1916, p4 (art. 110, "The Shops' Hours", sub-head "New Councillor")**:
  monthly meeting "on Tuesday evening"; unanimously agreed to appoint Mr H. J. Robertson (OCR
  garbled) in place of W. Spence Smith, "who had resigned owing to failing health".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19161209&page=0004&article=110
- **Shetland Times, Sat 6 Jan 1917, p5 (art. 113)**: meeting of Tue 2 Jan 1917; present include
  "H. J. Robertson". https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19170106&page=0005&article=113
- Ledger: `hugh-robertson` 1916-12-05 confirmed.

### 10. Nov 1913 result
- **Shetland Times, Sat 8 Nov 1913, p4 (art. 064)**: "The Municipal Election": Goodlad,
  L. Laurenson, Andrew Smith and W. S. Smith returned; the Socialist candidates defeated.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19131108&page=0004&article=064

### 11. The 1919 election notice
- **Shetland Times, Sat 18 Oct 1919, p5 (art. 082)**, "Municipal Election, 1919": poll if needed on
  Tuesday 4 November, to elect seven Councillors in place of James Laing and Robert Henderson
  Ramsay (by rotation) and J. T. J. Sinclair, J. P. Henderson, C. B. Stout, M. L. Manson and
  H. J. Robertson ("elected ad interim").
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19191018&page=0005&article=082
- Confirms the 1919-11-04 ends of those seven rows. Goodlad, Ratter, Ganson, W. Sinclair and
  J. Smith (i) are not on it: the 1913 and 1914 rows end at 1919-11-04 only because the ledger
  splits them into holdover rows there. Their sources say so.

The 1912 and 1914 starts are from `ltc-1912-1914.md` (1912 unopposed; 1914 returned unopposed).

## Result

All three short rows are genuine vacancies between a death and the co-option. 17 rows confirmed;
one end date moved (W. S. Smith, 5 Dec → 7 Nov 1916). term_issues 21 → 18.

Not checked: whether Provost Goodlad left in 1919–20 (ST 18 Oct 1919 art. 055 says he meant to
retire "at the end of the war"; the 9 Oct 1919 report said 8 vacancies "if Goodlad resigns"). His
holdover row to Nov 1920 is unconfirmed.
