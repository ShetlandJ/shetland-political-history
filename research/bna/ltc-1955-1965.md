# LTC 1955–1965: retiring lists, lots and co-options

Researched 2026-09-27 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times, published on Fridays in
these years). Every link opens the BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** in the toolbar
for the OCR text.

Question: clear the 1958–65 size-over row and the overlaps for Arthur Johnson (1958, 1964), Eric
Gray and James Daniel (1960), William Conochie (1961), James Paton (i) (1962) and James Taylor
(1964). The draft ledger gave every winner a 3-year term. From 1955 the rotation was broken by
uncontested elections, so the council **drew lots** for who retired when, and the lowest-placed
winners got the shorter seats.

## Evidence

### 1955: Halcrow resigns, Johnson co-opted
- **ST Fri 13 May 1955, p6**: Johnson, who lost his seat "by a matter of ten votes", was co-opted
  "on Friday night" (6 May 1955) to replace Miss G. M. T. Halcrow, who resigned to become county
  councillor for Cunningsburgh. "Her resignation took effect from 26th April".
  (Found via search; article id not captured.)

### 1956: five retiring
- **ST Fri 16 Mar 1956, p2 (art. 027)**: retiring are Provost Burgess, ex-Provost Anderson,
  Councillors Eunson, Gray and Johnson: "the first four retire by rotation, the last-mentioned as
  a co-opted member". All five were returned unopposed.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19560316&page=0002&article=027
- Conochie became Provost in May 1956 ("Lerwick's new Provost", ST 11 May 1956).

### 1957: the unopposed four were Blance, Morrison, **Nicolson**, Ollason
- **ST Fri 19 Apr 1957, p4 (art. 052)**: "NO ELECTION THIS YEAR": "only the four retiring
  councillors had been nominated": Robert B. Blance, Alexander Morrison, Andrew J. Nicolson,
  Robert Ollason. **Grace Halcrow is not among them** (the wiki has her).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19570419&page=0004&article=052
- **ST Fri 17 May 1957, p6 (art. 080)**: statutory meeting; the Provost thanked "the four
  retiring members" for standing again.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19570517&page=0006&article=080
- **ST Fri 17 Apr 1964, p4 (art. 054)**: Halcrow "was elected to the Town Council ten years ago,
  but served for only one year before resigning" for the county council. Confirms she was not on
  the council 1957–60.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19640417&page=0004&article=054

### 1958: Eunson resigns; lot for retirement
- **ST Fri 17 Jan 1958, p5 (art. 074)**: John W. Eunson's resignation "was accepted by the Council
  at Tuesday night's meeting" (14 Jan 1958).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19580117&page=0005&article=074
- **ST Fri 24 Jan 1958, p5 (art. 070)**: on the Provost's casting vote, a special meeting decided
  to leave Eunson's seat vacant until the May election.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19580124&page=0005&article=070
- **ST Fri 14 Mar 1958, p4 (art. 049)**: Bailie Tait and Councillor Daniel "have been longest on
  the Council, and automatically retire". The other two places were drawn by lot from the five who
  entered unopposed in 1956 (Anderson, Burgess, Eunson, H. Gray, Johnson), Eunson included: "Out
  came G. H. Burgess and A. Johnson". So five vacancies in May.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19580314&page=0004&article=049
- **ST Fri 6 Jun 1958, p4 (art. 050)**: Tait, the poll-topper made Bailie and Dean of Guild, "has
  had to intimate his resignation" (date not given).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19580606&page=0004&article=050
- **ST Fri 27 Jun 1958, p4 (art. 055)**: special meeting "on Tuesday night" (24 Jun 1958): the
  vacancy from "the recent retiral of Bailie J. W. Tait" filled by co-opting Arthur Johnson.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19580627&page=0004&article=055

### 1959: five retiring, Nicolson by lot
- **ST Fri 13 Mar 1959, p5 (art. 059)**: retiring by rotation: Provost Conochie ("four years
  without facing a contest"), Bailie Harry Gray and ex-Provost Anderson ("three years"), Councillor
  A. J. Nicolson ("two years (his place was decided by lot some months ago)"), and Arthur Johnson,
  co-opted after the last election.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19590313&page=0005&article=059
- **ST Fri 14 Aug 1959, p4 (art. 035)**: at the monthly meeting "on Tuesday" (11 Aug 1959) a letter
  from M. J. Sandison resigning (he might be employed by the Education Department).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19590814&page=0004&article=035
- **ST Fri 11 Sep 1959, p6 (art. 073)**: ex-Bailie Harry Gray, top of the unsuccessful in May, "was
  co-opted to the Town Council last Thursday" (3 Sep 1959).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19590911&page=0006&article=073

### 1960: five retiring
- **ST Fri 15 Apr 1960, p4 (art. 060)**: "Eight candidates for five seats". Ollason decided not to
  re-stand; "the other four retiring councillors" (marked *) are Daniel, Eric Gray, Harry Gray and
  Morrison. Blance (Provost) is not retiring.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19600415&page=0004&article=060

### 1961: four retiring; Thomson resigns; Strachan co-opted in June
- **ST Fri 17 Mar 1961, p4 (art. 041)**: due to retire in May: Burgess, Strachan, Conochie, Johnson.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19610317&page=0004&article=041
- **ST Fri 12 May 1961, p7 (art. 099)**: "Newly-elected councillor resigns": the Rev. Kenneth
  Thomson, third in the poll, resigned at Friday's statutory meeting (5 May 1961); he held a
  teaching post and was ineligible.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19610512&page=0007&article=099
- **ST Fri 16 Jun 1961, p3 (art. 057)**: "Co-opted to Town Council": Robert Strachan, who headed
  the unsuccessful candidates, co-opted at "Tuesday's Town Council meeting" (13 Jun 1961) for
  Thomson's vacancy. The wiki's "By-Election May 1961" was this June co-option.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19610616&page=0003&article=057

### 1962: five retiring
- **ST Fri 16 Mar 1962, p4 (art. 066)**: "RETIRING COUNCILLORS": Provost R. B. Blance, elected
  May 1957 and on the council without an election since, "owing to his elevation to the Provost's
  chair in 1959"; Anderson and Nicolson, elected 1959; James J. Paton, "lowest of the successful
  candidates in 1960"; and Robert Strachan, co-opted last June.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19620316&page=0004&article=066
- **ST Fri 10 Aug 1962, p6 (art. 090)**: M. M. Shearer co-opted to replace J. A. Daniel, "who
  recently submitted his resignation" (date not given).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19620810&page=0006&article=090

### 1963: four retiring
- **ST Fri 19 Apr 1963, p5 (art. 079)**: four vacancies. Ex-Provost Conochie is not seeking
  re-election; the other retiring members are Eric Gray, Morrison and Shearer. **Harry Gray is not
  retiring.**
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19630419&page=0005&article=079

### 1964: four retiring
- **ST Fri 17 Apr 1964, p4 (art. 054)**: "All four retiring members": A. I. Anderson, P. Robertson,
  J. J. Taylor and Arthur Johnson.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19640417&page=0004&article=054

### 1965: four retiring, unopposed
- **ST Fri 16 Apr 1965, p5 (art. 056)**: "NO MUNICIPAL ELECTION": only the four retiring members
  were nominated (Anderson, H. Gray, Nicolson, Paton: the four returned).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19650416&page=0005&article=056

## What it shows

- **1957**: the fourth seat was Andrew Nicolson's, not Grace Halcrow's. Nicolson's 1955 seat was a
  two-year one (he re-stood in 1957); Halcrow sat only 1954–55 and again from 1964.
- **Provosts stay on**: Conochie (Provost 1956–59) sat 1955–59; Blance (Provost from 1959) sat
  1957–62.
- **Lots and short seats**: Burgess and Johnson drawn to retire in 1958 (after two years), Nicolson
  in 1959 (two years). Two-year seats also went to E. Gray and Daniel (1958–60), Johnson, Conochie
  and Strachan (1959–61), Paton (1960–62, lowest), Conochie (1961–63), Taylor and Johnson (1962–64).
  Harry Gray's 1960 seat ran five years, to 1965. The papers give the result, not always the reason.
- **Vacancies**: Halcrow 26 Apr–6 May 1955; Eunson 14 Jan–May 1958 (left empty on purpose);
  Tait June 1958; Sandison 11 Aug–3 Sep 1959; Thomson 5 May–13 Jun 1961; Daniel 1962.
- The council is 12 throughout 1955–65 apart from those vacancies.
