# Biographies: Town Councillors of 1818–1850

Researched 2026-10-01 in the British Newspaper Archive. Every link opens the BNA viewer on the
article; click **Articles** for the OCR text.

Question: what can be found for the earliest LTC members with no biography? They all died before
the Shetland Times began (1872), so the search used other titles in the BNA:

- **Orkney & Zetland Chronicle** (`0006124`, Kirkwall, monthly, Jan 1825–Sep 1826, 13 issues). Every
  issue's articles were OCR-swept for councillors' surnames.
- **Orkney and Shetland Journal** (`0005738` 1836–37, `0005737` 1838–39). The 1836–37 run is
  Arthur Anderson's *Shetland Journal*; the 1838–39 run carries much less Shetland news.
- **John o' Groat Journal** (`0000459`, Wick, from 1836) and **Northern Ensign** (`0002561`, Wick):
  Lerwick letters and death notices.
- Scottish and naval papers, through all-title searches with surname + "lerwick" over the weeks
  after each death.

Biographies are in `fix_newspapers.py` #71, for William Copland, James Yorston, Robert Goudie, Archibald Greig,
Charles Ogilvy (ii), John Ogilvy, Gilbert Duncan, Alexander Irvine, Gilbert Paterson, James
Pottinger (i), William Clark (i), James Hunter (i) and David Nicolson. The last six are a sentence
or two each: only a death notice was found.

Findings:
- **James Yorston died at Leith, not Lerwick.** Three naval obituaries give "Paymaster and Purser
  James Yorston (1813)", died at Leith on 10 Feb 1849 of typhus, his wife two days before. The date
  is ours exactly, and the 1825 and 1827 birth notices tie a purser James Yorston to Sound, near
  Lerwick. Death place fixed (#71).
- **Gilbert Paterson died on 8 April 1828, not the 5th** (two papers). Fixed (#71).
- **Archibald Greig died on 12 October 1852, not the 11th** (three papers). Fixed (#71).
- **Three of the early councillors were Royal Navy pursers**: James Pottinger (i), James Yorston and
  Gilbert Duncan (purser and writer).
- **William Copland was Arthur Anderson's man in Lerwick**: Lerwick seller of the Shetland Journal
  and agent for the Peninsular company. With Yorston, he handled Anderson's 1836–37 schemes for
  passages south and the 1837 famine emigration.
- Nothing found for John Hughson, William Angus, Thomas Balfour Nicolson, Gilbert
  Robertson, David Leask, Alexander Nicol or David Burns (`data/searches.csv`). Isaac Cowie
  (surgeon, d. 11 Jun 1847), James Mouat (ii) (d. 28 Feb 1853, aged 45) and Samuel Hunter (d. Feb
  1858, merchant and fishcurer; the John o' Groat obituary is too garbled to use) had notices that
  only confirm what we have. Bayanne not consulted (Cloudflare). James Ogilvy was looked at again
  later the same day (his own section below).

## William Copland

https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0005738%2F18360611&page=0003&article=023

**Shetland Journal, Sat 12 Nov 1836, p4 (arts. 029, 030)**

## James Yorston

https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0002775%2F18130526&page=0003&article=019

At Sound, near Lerwick, 8 Feb 1827, Mrs Yorston, "Lady of James Yorston, Esq. P.R.N.", of a son.

**Hampshire Telegraph, Sat 17 Feb 1849, p5 (art. 025)**
https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000069%2F18490217&page=0005&article=025
"At Leith, James Yorston, Esq., Paymaster and Purser, R.N. (1813.)" He and his wife "died within

https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0002044%2F18490224&page=0002&article=014

## Robert Goudie

**Northern Ensign, Thu 2 Dec 1869, p5 (art. 064)**
https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0002561%2F18691202&page=0005&article=064
"Death of Robert Goudie, Esq.": ironmonger, aged 71, "one of our oldest and most worthy townsmen".
He came to Lerwick nearly sixty years ago and served his apprenticeship with his relatives the
Messrs Sinclair. He was a Free Churchman from the Disruption but gave to other churches too, and
died on the evening of Saturday the 20th after a protracted illness, survived by his widow and family.

**John o' Groat Journal, Thu 2 Dec 1869, p2 (art. 013) and p3 (art. 059)**
https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000459%2F18691202&page=0002&article=013
https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000459%2F18691202&page=0003&article=059
The same obituary (cleaner OCR: "The son of pious parents"), and the death notice: "on the 20th
ult., Mr Robert Goudie, ironmonger, aged 71 years".

## Archibald Greig

**Edinburgh Evening Post, Wed 27 Oct 1852, p3 (art. 027)**
https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0001177%2F18521027&page=0003&article=027
"At Lerwick, on the 12th inst., Archibald Greig, Esq., of Sandsound, Procurator Fiscal of
Zetland." The John o' Groat Journal, Fri 29 Oct 1852, p3 (art. 045), which Bayanne cites (S422)
for its 11 Oct, also has "on the 12th instant ... aged 34 years" (OCR "12tta", "84"):
https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000459%2F18521029&page=0003&article=045
Suggested to Bayanne (I18009) on 2026-10-01. The Morning Chronicle (28 Oct, p8) and Londonderry Standard (28 Oct, p2: "at his house,
Lerwick") also give the 12th.

**Northern Ensign, Thu 28 Oct 1852, p3 (art. 041)**
https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0002561%2F18521028&page=0003&article=041
Lerwick letter on "the sudden demise" of the public prosecutor for Shetland. He had not been in good
health "since the apopletic stroke of last season", and three of the legal profession, "all fiscals", had died in
six months. The OCR is garbled where his age and the letter's date would be; a zoom would recover them.

Supports: `died_date` 1852-10-11 → 1852-10-12 (#71).

## James Ogilvy

Added 2026-10-01: how did he come to die at New Orleans? Not found. The biography (#74) is
what could be found.

**Caledonian Mercury, Mon 10 Feb 1823, p1 (art. 001)**
https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000045%2F18230210&page=0001&article=001
Estate of William Sinclair: inquire at "Mr James Ogilvy, merchant in Lerwick, the trustee". He is
trustee again in the London Gazette (1 Sep 1823, p1461) and Edinburgh Gazette (4 Nov 1824, p200).
No sequestration of his own in either Gazette.

**Caledonian Mercury, Thu 3 Jun 1824, p3 (art. 011)**
https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000045%2F18240603&page=0003&article=011
"Destructive Fire at Lerwick", from a letter dated 15 May. About 2.30 am the dwelling-house, shop and
warehouses of Mr James Ogilvy, merchant, were found on fire; the roof fell in and the walls were
left standing. Furniture, stock, account books and papers all lost. The family were at a small
country house while repairs were done. Cause: a spark from the counting-room fire, or lightning.

**Inverness Courier, Thu 10 Jun 1824, p4 (art. 014)**
https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000446%2F18240610&page=0004&article=014
The same letter, with a clean date: "the morning of the 12th instant".

  of James Malcolmson, Sheriff Substitute; "James, born 29th November, 1794, and died [at] New
  Orleans, 1848". Brother Thomas, Lieutenant R.N., died at Moulmein about 1830. No wife given.
  This is the only source found for New Orleans.
- Bayanne I18310, note from E. S. Reid Tait's notes (Robertson, *Sons and Daughters of
  Shetland*, p144): bought the property at roup 29 May 1821; burnt "23rd May 1824" (wrong: the

## Gilbert Duncan and Alexander Irvine

**Orkney & Zetland Chronicle, 28 Feb 1825, p1 (art. 002)**
https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0006124%2F18250228&page=0001&article=002
General meeting at Lerwick, 2 Feb 1825, of commissioners of supply, landholders and others, to make
up a valuation roll for Zetland and get a declarator of their right to vote at elections for the
Stewartry of Orkney and Zetland. William Mouat of Garth chaired. Those attending
include "Mr Alex. Irvine, merchant, Lerwick, for his father Mr Andrew Irvine" and Gilbert Duncan,
writer, with mandates. Other councillors present: Andrew Grierson of Quendale, Charles Ogilvy of
Seafield, William Hay younger of Laxfirth, Dr A. Edmondston, Charles Ogilvy jun., William Spence,
James Mouat, Gilbert Gauden, James Greig and William Sievwright.

o' Groat Journal notice (8 Mar 1844, p3) adds a garbled line about a messmate.
