# LTC 1884–1935: citations for eleven elections

Researched 2026-09-28 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times, and the Shetland News for
1932). Every link opens the BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** for the OCR text.

Question: find a source for elections 34, 39, 44, 45, 51, 58, 81, 83, 84, 87 and 90 (citations
queue, batch 1), and check their dates and votes against the DB.

Findings:
- **1893 general was Tue 7 Nov**, not Thu 2 Nov (`fix_newspapers.py` #26; 8 ledger dates).
- **Johnson was elected Tue 4 Apr 1899**, not 2 May, when he took his seat (#27).
- **Reid Tait was co-opted Tue 6 May 1924**, not Mon 5th (#28).
- **Duncan's Nov 1884 co-option was before 18 Nov**, not on the 22nd (the paper's date): he sat
  at the adjourned meeting of Tue 18 Nov. The meeting that elected him was after the annual
  meeting of Mon 10 Nov; its day isn't in the report. Open question; nothing changed.
- **Bailie Campbell resigned at the May 1932 monthly meeting** (probably Tue 3 May), not at
  Morrison's June co-option. Ledger end moved to 1932-05-03, unconfirmed.
- The Shetland Times for mid-May to June 1932 isn't digitised; the Shetland News covers it.
- 1925 and 1929 votes match where the OCR is readable; 1926's figures are in a table the OCR lost.
  Ganson's 1929 figure (DB 504) and most of 1925's weren't checked: the page image wouldn't render.
- 17 ledger rows confirmed: the 1886–87 co-options and the 1887 cohort (not Anderson, whose end
  waits on the 1888 date), Johnson 1899, Laurenson 1905, Reid Tait 1924, the 1926 cohort and
  the 1929 Manson, Sandison and Ganson (i) rows.

## Evidence

### 1. Nov 1884 co-option (election 34)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 15 Nov 1884, p3 (art. 042)**: "Election of a Councillor": the first
  business was the vacancy "caused by the resignation of Bailie Hay"; Leisk proposed William
  Duncan, merchant. A sub-article on the same meeting refers to "Monday's meeting" (the annual
  meeting, 10 Nov) and another says Charles G. Duncan (died Monday night) died "since last
  meeting", so this was a later meeting that week.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18841115&page=0003&article=042
- **Shetland Times, Sat 22 Nov 1884, p3 (art. 041)**: adjourned meeting "on Tuesday evening"
  (18 Nov); W. Duncan present.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18841122&page=0003&article=041

### 2. Nov 1887 general (election 39)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 29 Oct 1887, p2 (art. 048)**: nominations on Thursday. Retiring by
  rotation: John Robertson (Chief Magistrate), Charles Robertson (Junior Bailie), John Robertson
  junior, John B. Anderson and A. A. Porteous, all re-nominated, to be declared elected "on
  Tuesday next" (1 Nov).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18871029&page=0002&article=048

### 3. Nov 1892 general (election 44)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 29 Oct 1892, p2 (art. 033)**: nominations on Thursday "for the four
  vacancies"; the retiring councillors were re-nominated and will be declared elected. Names
  not given here (the Town Clerk's notice is art. 025).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18921029&page=0002&article=033

### 4. Nov 1893 general (election 45)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 21 Oct 1893, p2 (art. 027)**: retiring are Provost Robertson, Bailie
  Laurenson, John Robertson and A. A. Porteous; Laurenson won't stand.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18931021&page=0002&article=027
- **Shetland Times, Sat 4 Nov 1893, p2 (art. 058)**: Goodlad nominated for Laurenson's seat;
  "on Tuesday next, the day of election", the four will be declared elected (7 Nov).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18931104&page=0002&article=058

### 5. Apr 1899 co-option (election 51)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 8 Apr 1899, p4 (art. 055)**: the monthly meeting "on Tuesday evening"
  (4 Apr; heading in art. 047) "sat in committee to appoint a Councillor in place of the late
  Mr A. J. Garriock, when Sinclair Johnson was elected".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18990408&page=0004&article=055
- **Shetland Times, Sat 6 May 1899, p8 (art. 140)**: at the monthly meeting of Tuesday (2 May)
  Johnson, "elected at last meeting", took the affirmation and his seat.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18990506&page=0008&article=140

### 6. Feb 1905 co-option (election 58)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 4 Mar 1905, p4 (art. 051)**: "Appointment of a Town Councillor": special
  meeting "on Tuesday at noon" (28 Feb).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19050304&page=0004&article=051

### 7. May 1924 co-option (election 81)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 10 May 1924, p5 (art. 133)**: at the monthly meeting "on Tuesday evening"
  (6 May), E. S. Reid Tait co-opted "to fill the casual vacancy caused through the resignation
  of Mr James Goodlad".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19240510&page=0005&article=133

### 8. Nov 1925 general (election 83)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 7 Nov 1925, p4 (art. 080)**: "Labour Loses Two Seats"; polling on
  Tuesday, "1305 electors from a possible roll of 2099". Result: Campbell 763, Morrison 518
  readable (both match); the rest garbled.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19251107&page=0004&article=080

### 9. Nov 1926 general (election 84)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 6 Nov 1926, p4 (art. 073)**: polling on Tuesday in a gale; order Ganson,
  Ratter, Sandison, Irvine, then J. Manson, Murray, Gray, Spence; "The total poll was 1196"
  (matches). Ganson and Ratter returned; Sandison and Irvine new.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19261106&page=0004&article=073

### 10. Nov 1929 general (election 87)
- **Shetland Times, Sat 9 Nov 1929, p4 (art. 130)**: W. Sinclair 780, L. Sandison 756, A. S.
  Manson 514 (match), R. D. Ganson (OCR "308"; the DB's 504 fits the order), then Irvine and
  Ratter, "retiring members", defeated.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19291109&page=0004&article=130

### 11. Campbell's resignation and Morrison's co-option, 1932 (election 90)
- **Shetland News, Thu 5 May 1932, p5 (art. 049)**: at the monthly meeting (day lost in the OCR)
  a letter from Bailie Campbell tendering his resignation from the Town Council.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0003210%2F19320505&page=0005&article=049
- **Shetland News, Thu 9 Jun 1932, p4 (art. 030)**: Cogle appointed Junior Bailie; "James
  Morrison, a former member of the Council, has been coopted a Councillor in place of Bailie
  Campbell". Day not stated; Tue 7 Jun (the DB date) was the monthly meeting.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0003210%2F19320609&page=0004&article=030
