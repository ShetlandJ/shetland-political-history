# Lerwick Town Council, 1921–1931: retiring lists

Researched 2026-10-21, to confirm the draft ledger rows from Nov 1921 to Nov 1928. Each year's
retiring Councillors are named in the Shetland Times nomination reports. 1924, 1927, 1928, 1930
and 1931 were unopposed. BNA link pattern:
`https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2FYYYYMMDD&page=NNNN&article=NNN`.

## Goodlad's resignation, 1924

- **ST Sat 5 Apr 1924, p4 (art. 089)**, "Resignation of Councillor Goodlad", at the monthly meeting
  on Tuesday evening (1 Apr 1924, Bailie Laing presiding): his letter was read and the resignation
  accepted; he was "not prepared" to reconsider.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19240405&page=0004&article=089
  → `james-goodlad` 1921-11-01 ends 1924-04-01 (resigned), confirmed. Reid Tait was co-opted on
  6 May (ST 10 May 1924).

## Retiring lists

- **1924, ST 1 Nov 1924 p4 a091**: "the four retiring Councillors", Bruce, Ollason, J. T. J.
  Sinclair and Reid Tait. → confirms the 1921 rows of Bruce, Ollason and J. T. J. Sinclair.
- **1925, ST 24 Oct 1925 p4 a073**: "the four retiring Councillors", Bailie M. L. Manson,
  A. S. Manson, Morrison and Duffin. Laing, elected in 1922 with Manson, Morrison and Duffin, is
  not among them: he was Provost. A. S. Manson (elected 1923) retired in his place, a year early;
  the paper gives no reason, but it's the pattern of 1906–07, 1908 and 1937.
  → `james-laing` 1922 runs to 1927-11-01 (not 1925); `alexander-manson` 1923 ends 1925-11-03
  (not 1926). He stood again and lost (ST 7 Nov 1925).
- **1926, ST 30 Oct 1926 p4 a068**: Ganson, Murray and Ratter seek re-election; "the other
  retiring Councillor", Reid Tait, is not standing.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19261030&page=0004&article=068
  → `edwyn-tait` 1924 ends 1926-11-02 (not 1927), a year early; no reason given. Confirms the 1923
  rows of Ganson, Murray and Ratter.
- **1927, ST 29 Oct 1927 p4 a073**: "the four retiring members", Laing, J. T. J. Sinclair, Ollason
  and Bruce, re-elected unopposed. → confirms Laing sat 1922–27 without a break, and the 1924 rows
  of Sinclair, Ollason and Bruce.
- **1928, ST 3 Nov 1928 p5 a090**: the retiring Councillors Campbell, Cogle, Duffin and
  M. L. Manson. → confirms the 1925 rows.
- **1930, ST 1 Nov 1930 p4 a070**: Bruce, Laing, Ollason and J. T. J. Sinclair, "the four retiring
  members". → confirms the 1927 rows.
- **1931, ST 31 Oct 1931 p4 a097**: "the retiring Councillors" Campbell, Cogle, Duffin and
  M. L. Manson. → confirms the 1928 rows.

The council is 12 throughout Nov 1921–Nov 1931 apart from Goodlad's vacancy (1 Apr–6 May 1924).
