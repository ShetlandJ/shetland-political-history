# ZCC 1960–1974: citations for the generals and by-elections

Researched 2026-09-29 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times, a Friday paper by then).
Every link opens the BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** for the OCR text.

Question: find a source for each Zetland County Council election 1960–1974 (citations batch 2,
fifth and last run), and check polling days and votes against the DB.

Findings:
- **Generals**: all five polled on the Tuesday already in the DB (9 May 1961, 12 May 1964, 9 May
  1967, 12 May 1970, 8 May 1973). Counts were on the Wednesday. 1967 had no contests at all
  (Fetlar and Tingwall had no nomination). The 1961, 1964 and 1970 contests agree with the DB.
  The OCR misreads Unst South 1961 (98 for Hunter's 96: 96 + 20 + 2 spoiled = 118 voted), Tingwall
  1964 (172 for Pottinger's 72: majority 102) and Bressay 1970 (179 for Irvine's 79: 79 + 56 + 33
  = 168).
- **Burra and Quarff 1964**: electorate **500**, not 524. The paper gives 66 on the Quarff roll and
  434 on Burra's, and 23.6 per cent (118 of 500). The wiki's 22.5% is 118 of 524 (#53).
- **Aithsting 1973** was a three-cornered contest: **Peter F. M. Tulloch 46** (Caldwell 151,
  Garrick 124, two spoiled, 86 per cent). The wiki leaves Tulloch out; added (#54).
- **Northmavine South 1970–72 was Robert Balfour's seat, not Hugh Sutherland's.** Balfour
  resigned "as member for Northmavine South" in March 1972 after seventeen years, and spoke as
  "the Northmavine representative" in 1970. The wiki gives the May 1970 seat to Sutherland, and its
  intros are built on that (Balfour "1955 and 1967"; Sutherland "finally for Northmavine South").
  Candidacy, 1972 replaced member and both intros fixed (#55). Where Sutherland was 1970–72 isn't
  settled: Delting South went to Rev. William C. Robb in 1970, so he probably didn't stand.
- **Six by-elections were polls**, where the wiki has "Unopposed" or "appointed": Bressay 1960,
  Delting North 1963, Yell South 1966, Bressay 1966, Gulberwick 1970 and Yell North 1974 (#56).
  The Delting North winner was A. W. (Andrew W.) Peterson, the wiki's "Willie Peterson"; he had
  sat for Yell North until 1962.
- **Dates**: every by-election moved from the 1st of the month (or the wiki's day) to the poll,
  the day fixed for an unopposed return, or the co-opting meeting (#57). Wiki days were wrong in
  most cases, and so were several title months: Yell South "March" 1966 was 22 Feb; Fetlar and
  Tingwall "September" 1967 were 25 Jul; Burra and Whiteness "August" 1970 were 14 Jul; Dunrossness
  North "September" 1963 was a co-option on 22 Oct; Dunrossness North "October" 1971 was a
  November poll; Northmavine South "June" 1972 was 16 May; Burra "September" 1972 was 8 Aug; Yell
  North "February" 1974 was 8 Jan. The titles are kept.
- **Dunrossness North 1971** had two by-elections. Mrs Mary T. Fisher, the only nominee, was
  formally elected on Tue 24 Aug, but as a council employee she couldn't take her seat, and
  resigned. The August by-election is added, her win is in `not_seated.csv`, and the November poll
  is a `[voided election re-run]` (#59, James's go-ahead 2026-09-29). The November poll was between
  her and James W. Leask. The day is lost in the OCR ("Tuesday, [..]th November"); dated Tue 9 Nov
  (the wiki's Wed 10 Nov is probably the count). The result wasn't found, so Leask 55, Fisher 15
  is the wiki's.
- **Yell North 1962**: the by-election was fixed for Tue 4 Dec and Sutherland took his seat on Tue
  18 Dec. No nominations report found, so "Unopposed" is the wiki's. Dated 4 Dec.
- **1973 general**: the paper calls Unst North "the one contest in which no retiring member sought
  re-election", with Sandy Cluness. That's Yell North (Cluness beat Manson there; Unst North was
  Thomson v Thomason, as the same article says). A slip in the paper; no change.

## Evidence

### 1. County Council Election May 1961 (elections 805–828)
- **Shetland Times, Fri 21 Apr 1961, p5 (art. 062)**: "ONLY FIVE POSSIBLE COUNTY ELECTIONS"; seventeen
  retiring members returned unopposed. Nominations list.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19610421&page=0005&article=062
- **Shetland Times, Fri 12 May 1961, p5 (art. 065)**, already on file (`zcc-election-dates-1949-1964.md`):
  "Shocks for retiring councillors". Sandwick majority 38, 268 of 611; Aithsting majority 107, 305
  of 379; Unst South 118 of 180, two spoiled; Yell North majority 96, 190 of 323; Whalsay 190 to 47,
  238 of 648. All agree. Now linked to every ward row.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19610512&page=0005&article=065

### 2. County Council Election May 1964 (elections 832–855)

  Cromarty.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19730511&page=0001&article=006
- **Same page (art. 011)** and its sub-articles TINGWALL and BRESSAY: the table. Aithsting Caldwell
  151, Garrick 124, **Peter F. M. Tulloch 46**, two spoiled, 86 per cent → #54; Whiteness 77/67/64;
  Walls 195/49; Northmavine North 46/37; Tingwall 327/260; Bressay 122/34; Dunrossness South 150/87.
  Yell North, Gulberwick, Nesting and Unst North figures aren't in the OCR (Gulberwick "a two-vote
