# LTC 1934–1938: departures and co-options

Researched 2026-09-28 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times). Every link opens the
BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** in the toolbar for the OCR text.

Question: the five size-short rows on /data-review (Sep 1934, Jun 1936, Nov 1936, Oct 1937,
Jul 1938). Are they dated vacancies, and are the departure and co-option dates right?

## Evidence

### 1. Sandison's death, Sep 1934
- **Shetland Times, Sat 6 Oct 1934, p4 (art. 037)**: "Lerwick Town Councillor Killed". His car
  went off the road near Pettawater on Sunday afternoon (30 Sep 1934); the doctor found he had
  probably died instantly.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19341006&page=0004&article=037
- Ledger: `laurence-sandison` 1932-11-01 confirmed (end 1934-09-30 unchanged).

### 2. Johnston's resignation and the Oct 1934 retiring list
- **Shetland Times, Sat 18 Nov 1933, p5 (art. 098)**: Councillor Thomas Johnston's letter asking
  for six months' leave of absence; he had been laid aside by illness.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19331118&page=0005&article=098
- **Shetland Times, Sat 13 Oct 1934, p4 (art. 078)**: the monthly meeting "on Tuesday evening"
  (9 Oct 1934), Provost Ollason presiding.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19341013&page=0004&article=078
- **Same issue, p4 (art. 087)**: letter from Johnston resigning "owing to ill-health"; accepted
  with regret.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19341013&page=0004&article=087
- **Same issue, p4 (art. 088)**: retiring councillors are Bailies Cogle and Duffin and Councillors
  M. L. Manson and **Thomas Johnston**; "The late Councillor Laurence Sandison was also due to
  retire". Five vacancies.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19341013&page=0004&article=088
- **Shetland Times, Sat 3 Nov 1934, p4 (art. 045)**: preview. Vacancies from three retiring by
  rotation, "the resignation of Mr Thomas Johnston" and the death of Sandison.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19341103&page=0004&article=045
- So both Johnston's and Sandison's 1932 seats were two-year ones (due Nov 1934), although
  Johnston was 3rd of 5 in 1932. Why isn't stated.
- Ledger: `thomas-johnston-ii` 1932-11-01 ends **1934-10-09** (was 1934-11-06), confirmed.

### 3. Cogle's resignation, May 1936
- **Shetland Times, Sat 9 May 1936, p4 (art. 073)**: "Ex-Bailie Cogle Resigns from Council". At the
  monthly meeting on Tuesday (5 May 1936), after the water debate, he resigned, "definitely
  refused to reconsider", and left the meeting.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19360509&page=0004&article=073
- Ledger: `laurence-cogle` 1934-11-06 ends **1936-05-05** `resigned` (was 1936-07-07 `replaced`),
  confirmed.

### 4. Duffin's death, Jun 1936
- **Shetland Times, Sat 4 Jul 1936, p5 (art. 106)**: obituary; he "passed away on Sunday morning"
  (28 Jun 1936).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19360704&page=0005&article=106
- Ledger: `george-duffin` 1935-11-05 confirmed (unchanged dates).

### 5. Sinclair co-opted for Cogle, Jul 1936
- **Shetland Times, Sat 11 Jul 1936, p4 (art. 101)**: monthly meeting "on Tuesday evening"
  (7 Jul 1936). T. A. Sinclair co-opted "in room of Mr L. A. Cogle, who resigned"; Duffin's seat
  deferred to August.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19360711&page=0004&article=101
- **Same issue, p4 (art. 106)**: Sinclair chosen as the highest unsuccessful candidate at the last
  election; unanimous.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19360711&page=0004&article=106
- Ledger: `thomas-sinclair` 1936-07-07 confirmed.

### 6. Irvine co-opted for Duffin, Aug 1936
- **Shetland Times, Sat 8 Aug 1936, p4 (art. 068 and 074)**: "Mr Thomas Irvine, Confectioner,
  Co-opted" at the monthly meeting on Tuesday (4 Aug 1936), to the vacancy "caused by the death
  of ex-Bailie Duffin".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19360808&page=0004&article=068
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19360808&page=0004&article=074
- Ledger: `thomas-irvine-ii` 1936-08-04 confirmed.

### 7. Clausen co-opted, Nov 1936
- **Shetland Times, Sat 14 Nov 1936, p4 (art. 119)**: "Mr E. J. F. Clausen Co-opted to Council" at
  the meeting following the statutory meeting, on Tuesday evening (10 Nov 1936).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19361114&page=0004&article=119
- **Same issue, p4 (art. 127)**: Bailie Halcrow: with no contest they "had to get a member" (the
  uncontested 3 Nov general left one of five seats empty).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19361114&page=0004&article=127
- Ledger: `erling-clausen` 1936-11-10 confirmed.

### 8. A. S. Manson's death, Jul 1938
- **Shetland Times, Sat 30 Jul 1938, p4 (art. 083)**: died "very suddenly on Friday afternoon of
  last week" (22 Jul 1938), aged 54.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19380730&page=0004&article=083
- Ledger: `alexander-manson` 1935-11-05 confirmed.

### Also confirmed

  and the 1938 preview).
