# Unsuccessful LTC/ZCC candidates linked to Bayanne (2026-11-02)

Sweep of the 79 LTC/ZCC candidacies with no person row and no Bayanne link: Shetland Times
election articles in the two weeks either side of each election, read for the candidate's
address, then matched on Bayanne. Linked in `fix_newspapers.py` #98 (Gray, Spence 1926) and #99.

## Linked (#99)

- **Robert Wilson, Unst South 1922**: ST 25 Nov 1922 p5 a096, "Mr Robert Wilson, Queen's Hotel,
  Baltasound". https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19221125&page=0005&article=096
  → Bayanne I504758 (b. Stirling 1865, d. Stirling 1939), 1921 census at the Queen's Hotel, Baltasound.
- **Christopher F. Irvine, Aithsting 1925**: ST 21 Nov 1925 p4 a077, "Christopher F. Irvine, Unifirth".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19251121&page=0004&article=077
  → Bayanne I37083, Christopher Fraser Irvine, b. Unifirth, Aithsting, 1876.
- **Thomas Hobbin, Sandsting 1925**: ST 12 Dec 1925 p5 a085, "Thomas Hobbin, Bridge-of-Walls, 67".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19251212&page=0005&article=085
  → Bayanne I69297 (1869–1932); wife died and son born at Bridge of Walls. The only Thomas Hobbin.
- **Laurence S. Reid, Walls 1925**: same article, "Laurence S. Reid, Walls, 36".
  → Bayanne I71249, Laurence Smith Reid, b. Vadlure, Walls, 1888 (the other Walls Laurence Reid,
  I47011, b. 1844, has no middle name). Inferred from the initial.
- **George A. Kerr, Bressay by-election 1960**: ST 8 Apr 1960 p1 a144, thanks to the Bressay voters
  signed "GEORGE A. KERR. 30 Braewick Road, Lerwick".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19600408&page=0001&article=144
  Same address as the 1958 Town Council candidate (ST 25 Apr 1958 p1 a014,
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19580425&page=0001&article=014),
  already linked to Bayanne I236889.
- **James R. Gair, Town Council 1960 and 1964**: already I52866 for 1953, 1954, 1955 and 1958;
  "James R. Gair, 36 St. Sunniva Street" in 1960 (ST 29 Apr 1960 p8 a117).

  Bayanne I23811, Magnus Robert Keith (1881–1968), merchant seaman, d. 1 Baila, Sound. The only
  other Magnus R. Keith (I34544) died in 1928, aged four. Inferred.

  I177705, Robert Henry Tulloch (1912–1999), b. Sound, West Yell, the only Robert H. Tulloch from

  other George Robert Jamieson of Sandwick (I103971) died in 1941. Inferred.
