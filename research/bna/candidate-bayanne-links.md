# Unsuccessful LTC/ZCC candidates linked to Bayanne (2026-11-02)

Sweep of the 79 LTC/ZCC candidacies with no person row and no Bayanne link: Shetland Times
election articles in the two weeks either side of each election, read for the candidate's
address, then matched on Bayanne. Linked in `fix_newspapers.py` #98 (Gray, Spence 1926) and #99.

- **Robert Wilson, Unst South 1922**: ST 25 Nov 1922 p5 a096, "Mr Robert Wilson, Queen's Hotel,

  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19251121&page=0004&article=077
  → Bayanne I37083, Christopher Fraser Irvine, b. Unifirth, Aithsting, 1876.

  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19251212&page=0005&article=085
