# William Duncan (i): biography

Researched 2026-10-10 in the British Newspaper Archive, after a Shetland Family History Society
Facebook post asked whether he was the William Duncan, Principal Extractor in the Court of
Session, whose death was reported in May 1897. He wasn't (see the last section).

## Evidence

- **Shetland Times, Mon 8 Jul 1872, p1 (art. 007)**: advert, "WILLIAM DUNCAN, GROCER AND
  PROVISION MERCHANT", Commercial Street, Lerwick (the number is unclear in the OCR: "9G").
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18720708&page=0001&article=007
- **Shetland Times, Sat 29 Oct 1881, p2 (art. 027)**: nominations, Burgh of Lerwick. "DUNCAN,
  William, Queen's lane", proposed by Laurence Stove, seconded by John Leisk.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18811029&page=0002&article=027
- **Shetland Times, Sat 12 Apr 1884, p3 (art. 099)**: Town Council, erections. Plans "of a
  tenant-house in Quendale Lane, by Mr William Duncan, grocer" approved.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18840412&page=0003&article=099
- His 1886 resignation (ST 17 Jul and 16 Oct 1886) is in `ltc-1886-duncan-harrison.md`. The
  October report names G. Harrison interim Convener of Lighting and Cleansing in his place.
- **Shetland Times, Sat 22 May 1897, p4 (art. 062)**, Deaths: at Marguerite Villa, Richmond Wood
  Road, Bournemouth, "on the 19th inst., William Duncan, Merchant, in his 77th year."
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F18970522&page=0004&article=062
- **Shetland News, Sat 22 May 1897, p4 (art. 050)**, Local Intelligence, "Death of Mr Wm.
  Duncan": "lately grocer in Lerwick"; he had lived in Bournemouth for several years and been in
  ill-health for some time.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0003210%2F18970522&page=0004&article=050
- No obituary in the Shetland Times: every article on pp2–7 of 22 May, 29 May and 5 Jun 1897 was
  OCR-scanned for "Duncan".

Agrees with the DB: died 19 May 1897, Bournemouth; born 1820 (77th year fits 1820/21).

Not used: a William Duncan spoke at the reopening of the Burra chapel (ST 19 Nov 1887) and at a
lecture (ST 1 Sep 1888). Nothing ties either to him.

## Not the Principal Extractor

- **Dumfries and Galloway Saturday Standard, 1 May 1897, p7 (art. 007)**, April chronicle: "19.
  Died, in Edinburgh, William Duncan, S.S.C., principal extractor of the Court of Session", of
  Danevale (Kirkcudbrightshire) and Cairnhill (Peeblesshire), 80 years.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0001735%2F18970501&page=0007&article=007
- He died on 19 April 1897 in Edinburgh, aged 80, a month to the day before our man. The Daily
  News of 29 May 1897 reported the appointment of his successor.
