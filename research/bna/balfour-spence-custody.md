# Balfour Spence: where did "Town Councillor 1823" come from? (sweep in progress)

Started 2026-10-12. Question (James): trace the chain of custody of the claim that Balfour Spence
was elected to Lerwick Town Council in Sept 1823 (ledger row `balfour-spence` 1823-09-04, now
`confirmed=0`; background in `answers-2026-10-12.md`).

## BNA, all titles, "balfour spence" (43 hits; 21 with Lerwick)

Read so far (snippets only, no article OCR yet):
- **1808-10-14** (BNA 0000401 p4 a028): a Shetland society's officers; "Balfour Spence, Esqrs"
  among the members, with Arthur Gifford and Dr Edmondston. Not the council.
- **1817-07-01 to 07-12** (0000547, 0000082, 0001476, 0001325, 0000085): London Gazette, appointed
  Vice Consul for Hanover in Shetland.
- **1821-08-25, 09-01** (0002370 p8 a045; 0002131 p4 a004): sequestrated, "merchant in Lerwick".
- **1843-07-29** (0000860 p2 a014): "the Vice Consul of the Shetland Islands, Mr. Balfour Spence".
- **1871-04-14/18/20** (0001427 p8 a073; 0003483 p3 a021; 0000459 p3 a051): death of Charles
  Ogilvie Spence, "second son of the late J. Balfour Spence, of Lerwick". Note the **"J."**: his
  name may have been J(ohn?) Balfour Spence.
- **1887-12-03** (Shetland News, 0003210 p5 a056): a Lerwick article naming him (OCR garbled;
  not yet read). Could be a history of the burgh, the likeliest place for a councillor claim.
- Post-1850 hits without Lerwick are mostly lists where "Balfour" and "Spence" are two people.
- Nothing yet calls him a councillor or bailie.
