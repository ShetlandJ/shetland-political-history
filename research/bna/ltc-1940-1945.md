# LTC 1940–1945: the wartime departures and co-options

Researched 2026-09-28 in the British Newspaper Archive (Shetland Times: Saturdays to 1941, Fridays
by 1944). Every link opens the BNA viewer on the article; click **Articles** in the toolbar for the
OCR text.

Question: the Dec 1940 size-short row (Halcrow's death to Williamson's co-option) was already a
known vacancy, but it stayed on /data-review because Shearer's 1938 row and Mouat's 1940 co-option
were unconfirmed. Check William Sinclair's 1940 departure, Mouat's co-option, the other wartime
co-optees (none checked before), and their ends at the Nov 1945 general.

## Evidence

### 1. The Nov 1938 general
- **ST Sat 5 Nov 1938, p4 (art. 093)**: "Municipal Election. Four of Retiring Candidates
  Returned." Polled "on Tuesday" (1 Nov). Nine candidates for five seats; the winners were Shearer
  (1260, the most votes ever at a municipal election), Ganson, William Sinclair (828), Dalziel and
  Irvine (760). Mouat (509) topped the losers, ahead of J. A. Williamson (490).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19381105&page=0004&article=093
- Ledger: the starts of `magnus-shearer-i` and `william-sinclair` 1938-11-01 are now sourced.
  Shearer's row is **confirmed**: its end (reported at the 3 Jun 1947 meeting) was already sourced
  (`ltc-1947-shearer-dalziel.md`), and the start was all that held it.

### 2. William Sinclair resigns, May–June 1940
- **ST Sat 25 May 1940, p4 (art. 077)**: special meeting "last evening" (Fri 24 May) on the Police
  Act. Councillor W. G. Smith resigned verbally but agreed to reconsider; "Councillor William
  Sinclair tendered his resignation in writing", and he was also asked to reconsider.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19400525&page=0004&article=077
- **ST Sat 8 Jun 1940, p5 (art. 094)**: "MR W. SINCLAIR ADHERES TO RESIGNATION". Bailie Laing said
  they should "declare the seat vacant", which was agreed. This is part of the report of the June
  monthly meeting (headed "Lerwick Town Council. Progress with Waste Paper Scheme", same page),
  whose day is garbled in the OCR ("on.sl' the day evening"); the first Tuesday was 4 Jun.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19400608&page=0005&article=094
- Ledger: `william-sinclair` 1938-11-01 ends **1940-06-04**, `resigned` (was 1940-08-06,
  `replaced`), confirmed. The seat-declared-vacant date follows the Hunter 1887 precedent (the
  council's acceptance). If James prefers the letter (24 May), see the open question on Duncan 1886.

### 3. Mouat co-opted, Tuesday 2 July 1940
- **ST Sat 6 Jul 1940, p4 (art. 059)**: report of the monthly meeting held over to next week; "At
  this meeting Mr Andrew M. Mouat" was co-opted.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19400706&page=0004&article=059
- **ST Sat 13 Jul 1940, p2 (art. 028)**: "NEW COUNCILLOR CO-OPTED", part of the report of the
  meeting "on Tuesday evening of last week" (2 Jul), Bailie Laing presiding. It was customary to
  co-opt the candidate next highest to the successful ones, and "Mr Andrew Mouat held that
  position".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19400713&page=0002&article=028
- Edits: election 102 ("By-Election August 1940") 1940-08-06 → **1940-07-02**
  (`fix_newspapers.py` #19); `andrew-mouat` starts 1940-07-02, confirmed (end: ST 5 Oct 1945).

### 4. Provost J. A. Smith resigns; Johnson co-opted, June–July 1941
- **ST Sat 7 Jun 1941, p2 (art. 066 and 070)**: at "the Council's meeting on Tuesday" (3 Jun) the
  Clerk read Provost Smith's letter resigning as Councillor and Provost "three weeks from this
  date", on account of his illness; accepted with regret. The letter's date isn't given (Shearer
  had learnt of it the night before the meeting).
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19410607&page=0002&article=070
- **ST Sat 5 Jul 1941, p2 (art. 031)**: at the meeting "on Tuesday evening" (1 Jul) Major Shearer
  was appointed Provost, W. G. Smith Junior Bailie, and "Mr. Arthur Johnston [Johnson] was co-opted
  to fill the vacancy".
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19410705&page=0002&article=031
- Edits: `arthur-johnson` 1941-07-01 confirmed. `james-smith-i` 1934-11-06 (already confirmed,
  ending 1941-07-01): source extended; the end date is left alone and raised in open-questions,
  since three weeks from a letter of about 2 Jun is about 23 Jun, not 1 Jul.

### 6. Gear resigns; R. A. Anderson co-opted, February 1943
- **ST Sat 6 Feb 1943, p2 (art. 057)**: "Resignation of Councillor Gear": the Clerk read his letter
  (he was now postmaster at Lyness, Orkney), accepted as he could no longer attend. Part of the
  report of "the monthly meeting ... on Tuesday evening" (2 Feb), same page. The OCR runs this
  column into a seamen's conference report.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19430206&page=0002&article=057
- **ST Sat 13 Feb 1943, p2 (art. 080)**: "Councillor Co-opted": Robert Anderson (OCR "Robert D.";
  "R. A." everywhere else) appointed to Gear's vacancy, at the special meeting "On Thursday
  evening" (11 Feb), same page.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19430213&page=0002&article=080
- Edits: `john-gear` 1941-10-07 ends **1943-02-02**, `resigned` (was 1943-02-11, `replaced`),
  confirmed; `robert-anderson-i` 1943-02-11 confirmed. Leaves a sourced 2–11 Feb 1943 vacancy.

### 7. Inkster resigns; Prophet Smith co-opted and resigns, 1944–45
- **ST Fri 6 Oct 1944, p3 (art. 059)**: "TOWN COUNCIL TRIBUTE TO RETIRING COUNCILLOR", meeting "on
  Tuesday" (3 Oct): a letter from Inkster "tendering his resignation" as he was leaving for the
  south; the Provost didn't ask him to reconsider.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19441006&page=0003&article=059
- **ST Fri 10 Nov 1944, p2 (art. 042)**: November meeting "on Tuesday evening" (7 Nov): "Mr Prophet
  Smith has been co-opted" for Inkster.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19441110&page=0002&article=042
- **ST Fri 12 Jan 1945, p3 (art. 048)**: "COUNCILLOR DEPARTS": a letter "dated 2nd January" from
  Prophet Smith resigning, as he was leaving for an appointment in Greenock. Read at the monthly
  meeting "on Tuesday" (9 Jan), art. 047.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19450112&page=0003&article=048
- Edits: `charles-inkster` 1941-10-07 ends **1944-10-03**, `resigned` (was 1944-11-07); `prophet-smith`
  1944-11-07 ends **1945-01-09**, `resigned` (was 1945-02-05). Both confirmed.

### 8. Morrison co-opted, Tuesday 6 February 1945

  vacancy ... caused by the resignation of Mr Prophet Smith" with Alexander Morrison, Brown's
  Buildings.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19450209&page=0002&article=044
- Edits: election 109 ("By-Election February 1945") 1945-02-05 → **1945-02-06**

### 9. The Nov 1945 general: Tuesday 6 November
- **ST Fri 2 Nov 1945, p2 (art. 034)**: "TOWN COUNCIL ELECTION. FOR SIX SEATS ELEVEN CANDIDATES";
  nominations closed Friday 26 Oct.
  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19451102&page=0002&article=034

  indication of a keen fight. Fortunately, the day was fine", 1991 votes, result announced at

  https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.com/image-viewer?issue=BL%2F0000666%2F19451109&page=0002&article=047
