Two councillors were paired when Bayanne joins them in four parent, child or spouse steps or fewer, the chain doesn't run through a third councillor, and they weren't both in the family network already.

Dates are Bayanne's; an approximate date is kept as the year, with a note.

Every councillor's DB birth and death years agree with Bayanne to within a year, so no Bayanne ID looks wrong.
